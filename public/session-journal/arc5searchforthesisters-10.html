Session 79 - Transcendent Showdown Part 1

  • The party entered Raeneros City and went up to the normal Gatekeeper's Inn to rest.
  • As they walked through the City, Ethiul described what had happened up north in the months everyone has been separated; they have become a fully realized Cosmic Vanguard.
  • The next day, every went to Bidrian's Biddings to visit Jobidrian, and introduce him to Erin. She ran off into the store to buy some shit.
  • The party caught him up on their progress collecting the works of Johann Sea-Bastion Bach, and he demonstrated excitement to hear the full Magnum Concierto soon.
  • After the visit with Jobidrian, the party traveled just outside the city to Melody's grave site. There, Morgan placed some flowers and asked for help. He said he was very unsure about how to approach the impending confrontation with his sister due to the many rising conflicts of interest. He said he feels lost and has been looking towards Corsair for guidance.
  • After this confession, Morgan sat beside her grade for a few more minutes before departing.
  • At the same time, Voroth ventured off to find a different grave site, but there was no grave at the location he went to.
  • The party eventually caught up to see him sitting on the ground chuckling to himself before standing up and joining everyone else.
  • Following this visit, the party decided to manually sail back to Coastgate to:
    • 1) Test out the new music piece they just acquired.
    • 2) allot Micah the time needed to find a powerful enough caster from the university to help Selene and the party with the Imprisonment ritual on Transcendent Voroth.
  • The night before reaching Coastgate, Morgan had another vision and heard stirrings of thunder outside. When he went outside to investigate he got mad, shouting at Corsair for doing absolutely nothing this whole time.
  • Upon this outcry, lightning from the storm strikes down into the ship directly onto Morgan, invigorating it with power, and finally unlocking the attunement. Corsair, has finally returned with his blessing.
  • Upon arriving back at the Otherday's Home for Gifted Children, the party met Dr. Möbius Bernoulli, a professor at Coastgate University and a very powerful ritual caster.
  • He spent a few hours explaining the imprisonment spell, its history, and how abjuration magic works in general.
  • Justin Otherday lead the party into the basement where everyone began setting up for the ritual.
  • Before being pinned down, Voroth asked Erin for a plan-b option, to which she handed him a vial of poison in case anything goes south.
  • The Ritual began, Dr. Bernoulli began casting the spell to which the party became enveloped in a veil of darkness.
  • A door appeared before them, everyone stared at it apprehensively before Selene decided to open it and walk through. This revealed a vast library of books and artifacts stretching through various maze like configurations from floor to ceiling.
  • A man in robes approached them. He is a bit timid and quiet, but revealed himself as Voroth the Curator.
  • He gave the party a task: To curate 5 of the 6 items presented into an exhibit for his library, but he wanted to be convinced why the curation was decided.
    • A Demon Tooth
    • A Brass Compass
    • A Chipped Cog
    • A White and Gold Decorative Egg
    • A Skull Key
    • A Green Dragon Scale.
  • Morgan suggested that many of the items reflect the adventures they have been together on, including the Compass, Cog, and Skull Key.
  • Voroth remarked that the Demon Tooth also fit into this classification from another one of his lifetimes (Transcendent Voroth's in Arc 1).
  • For the last item, Voroth picked the green dragon scale stating he felt more drawn to it, but could not elaborate on this further. This caused a cold, sinister energy to overcome the party before the environment around them faded to black, dealing necrotic damage.
