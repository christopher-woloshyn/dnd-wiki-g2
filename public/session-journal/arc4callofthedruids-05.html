Session 61 - Trek into the Glaciers Part 1

  • Selene will invite the party to her office at the college to showcase some magic items. They are willing to negotiate to give more than what the stated allowance is.
  • Ulrich will make sure there is nothing else they need to prepare for, and everyone will leave for the glaciers the following morning. In the morning there's a bright and twinkling light snowfall. He mentions that both him and Selene have an ice shard with ice from the glaciers which they are following the energy of.
  • As they continue upwards for about 2 hours, the snowfall begins to get heavier and the wind begins to pick up. Anyone who doesn't have cold resistance must make a DC 14 Con save. Failure results in 2d6 cold damage.
  • Ulrich asks Tassarion about his life/deal since he's been quiet the last several days. There is more conversational role play opportunities here.
  • Ulrich announces that they are about to enter more dangerous territory, and that if the weather doesn't let up, they might have to stop early, or even turn back. Asks the party what they want to do
  • Ethiul passively spots a silhouette at the top of the section of mountain they are climbing and by the time he warns everyone the creature lets off a loud shriek and an avalanche begins. If hit by the snow, the creature is knocked prone and must make a DC 15 strength save or take 2d10 bludgeoning damage (half as much on a pass) and 1d6 cold damage. Creatures swept away dissappear from the map and are blinded and restrained. DC 15 athletics to dig free on each turn. 3 failures results in getting "stuck" and not being able to attempt again.
  • During the encounter, Fen notices that many of the others were swept away by the avalanche, and she flies down to help them.
  • Once this encounter ends, the party is separated with Ulrich, Ulgrim, Fen, Calen, and Soren too far from earshot. Selene receives a message from Ulrich through sending stones:
We're okay. Fen helped us. Storm worsening with low visibility. Continue upwards to flatter ground about one hour and seek Shelter. More info tomorrow morning.
