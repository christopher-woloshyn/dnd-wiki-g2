Session 1, 2

  • We meet the party on a trade ship. Everyone was hired by the Longdale Corridor Trading Company to assist in the transport of goods to Longdale.
  • Here are the members of our party:
    • Dagwyn Koehlanna - A sneaky rogue, always up to no good.
    • Voroth - A righteous paladin and knight serving his kingdom.
    • Gynnie Greenbottle - A sassy old lady whose sass is only outclassed by her desire to make a quick coin.
    • Fen'Harel - A druid of the Moonstar Grove, out to see the world for the first time.
  • During their trip, the boat was attacked by a gang of musical pirates called the Sonnet Swashbucklers. Their emblem is denoted by a coupled eighth note with skull and crossbones for the notes.
  • The party is able to defend the ship, and fend off the band of pirates.
