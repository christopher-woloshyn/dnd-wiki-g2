Session 18

  • The party is summoned back into the Alderman's war room after a few days.
  • He tells them that they may have located the Heart of Greed, but according to the book, it would be in a temple ruin that has likely been buried years ago.
  • The High Mage says that he can perform a teleportation spell to try and transport them into the temple. He also gives the party a recall stone capable of returning them to the palace.
  • The party gets teleported underground to the entrance of the temple.
  • The first puzzle of the temple was a draw bridge with a pit. The draw bridge was up, and could be lowered by pouring gold coins down the pit.
  • The party poured everything down the pit and crossed into the next room of the temple.
  • The second room of the temple is an eldritch casino, with slot machines and card tables all operated by nearly invisible spirits.
  • Everyone in the party wants no part of this, so after inspecting the machines and playing slots once, they try to open the door into the next room and it opens.
  • The final room was filled with thousands of various kinds of coins.
  • In the center of the room sits the Heart of Greed on a pedistal with a grate that leads to running water below in front of the gemstone. Inscribed is a message stating to "pay" what you think is fair for the gem.
  • The party then proceeded to dump all the extra money through the grate into the running water below.
  • Right before finishing, someone touched the Heart of Greed, and all remaining coins in the room instantly flew to the heart forming it into a Coin Golem.
  • The Coin Golem however, was extremely week due to the party keeping practically no gold throughout their traversal into the temple.
  • They are able to swiftly defeat the Coin Golem, activate the return stone, and return the the palace with 3/4 gems obtained.
