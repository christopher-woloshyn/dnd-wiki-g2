Session 45 - Welcome to Automata

  • The party, not really knowing where to go, continues onwards towards the center of the gear
  • They come across a moving sign with directions pointing to different places: the mines, the foundries, The Wastes, Automata 25 teeth in length, and Regulus 75 teeth.
  • After several hours of travel, they must perform an investigation check to try and determine how much time has passed. They will need to rest on pass.
  • Once they find the sign and try making a plan, the Pseudo Dragon comes back.
  • Gives a small chip that can "activate" the orb. "This will clear the charges against you for trespassing, with it you have a clean slate, and can head to the nearest town"
  • "Everything you have done, all of you, has been preparing you for the moment you meet. The Master is most interested in meeting you (to Voroth). That's why I have been with you all this time; there is a vast ocean of knowledge that must be shared, and questions to be answer."
  • Ethiul's goals are aligned because you both Ethiul and Voroth needed to travel to Mechanus to complete their personal quests.
  • "Heed my instructions: in the nearest town, Automata, there is a gate. You can use this to meet the master, and leave to the material plane."
  • Ad lib my time has come meme
  • The party then travels in the direction of Automata. There is a huge line they have to wait in, but Tassarion cleverly uses illusion magic to trick the others waiting in line into breaking formation, which sends them to the back of the line allowing them to advance.
  • In line, they meet a human named Spike also trying to get to Automata. Together they are able to advance near the front of the line quickly and only have to wait a couple of hours.
  • They make it to Automata, and try asking who "The Master" is, but with a name that vague, the constructs instead take them to the Leader of the Temple of Aevum.
  • Their leader tells them about the application process to leave Mechanus taking between 18-24 months, but suggests that perhaps their entrance into Mechanus wasn't an "Accident".
  • She tells them that they can learn more about the application and the gate's connection to the planes by asking at the library and tells them to come back when they learn more.
