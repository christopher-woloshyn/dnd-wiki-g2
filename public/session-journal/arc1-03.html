Session 3 - Escape from the Front (2026-03-02)

  • While running away, the rescued prisoner explains to everyone that he had no idea this was going to happen. It appears his leader, Commander Wells, had no intention of rescuing them, and that they were used as pawns in a larger counter-offensive retaliation.
  • Rin, hearing the name Wells, recalls encountering some nobles that spoke of his very cold and calculated demeanor, corroborating these actions.
  • Thysbe spots trails of smoke leading towards Pyralis, and everyone decides its best to follow those trails in hopes of finding more survivors. They specifically want to find Helio since she seemed pretty strong, to Thysbe's dismay.
  • After Traveling for an hour or so, everyone hears a commotion in the distance that sounds like soldiers fighting. As they approach, Thysbe flies into the air to see over a dozen felled soldiers, and an injured Helio protecting an unconscious Sir Salinor.
  • Thysbe rushes into the crowd to stop their advance on Helio, and XIV on the flank throws his sword to take out the line of archers. Thysbe suffers a massive blow from one of the leaders, but on the brink of collapse, unleashes a burst of feathers from his wings taking out several enemies.
  • Beag rushes in from the top rope and together with XIV takes down the second wave of enemies ultimately rescuing Helio and Sir Salinor.
  • XIV and Thysbe rush over to them to tend their wounds, and Helio points to the mud where Squire Vane lays dead. She seems very frazzled but glad to be safe for now.
  • Rin uses her tracking abilities to deduce that two waves of forces had passed through, implying the strike on the camp was one of many coordinated attacks while Pyralis' guard was down.
  • While laying the dead to rest XIV finds a note on the captain's corpse that reads:
Captain,

The capital has confirmed the defensive relocation effort is complete. Commense Operation 'Gilded Tinderbox' immediately. Devistate their supply lines and leave no opportunity to rebuild.

- Commander N. Wells
  • This evidence together with Rin recalling General Valmor leaving to ensure the supply lines from Schlackedorf to the front are established leads Thysbe to the unsettling revelation that his home village faces an eminent attack.
  • Everyone decides to rest, very briefly, to collect themselves before encountering what might be left of Thysbe's home.
