Session 3

  • Upon disembarking at the Longdale docks, the Longdale Corridor Trading Company is satisfied with the party's work, and want to rehire them to investigate these pirates, and to find a more permanent solution to the increased number of pirate attacks.
  • In Longdale, Dagwyn vanishes after a failed attempt to steal some goods from locals around the town; the party meets and teams up with another friendly Paladin named Prim. Together they head north into the forest to confront a group of bandits said to have information on the pirates' leader Songbeard the Pirate.
  • They are able to defeat the bandits and learn about a cave hideout to the south of Longdale along the coast. They is said to be where Songbeard is operating his band of pirates.
