Session 7

  • Ultimately, the party decides to flee to the east on their journey to keep warning other towns and villages along the way. They can't convince everyone, but they want to do what they can to warn those who are willing to listen.
  • The next village they arrive at a few days later is called Riverside Crossing.
  • There, the party meets a priestess that believes them, and is willing to help.
  • She tells them that she knows of some ruins in the forest a few hours north of the village.
  • They night in the tavern, the party meets an elf named Tassarion.
  • He is a Celestial Warlock who has made a pact with the celestials to gain the strength necessary to defeat the demons flooding in from inferno and wants to help the party.
