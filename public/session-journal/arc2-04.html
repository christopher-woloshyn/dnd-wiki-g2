Session 16 - Severing the Roots (2026-07-08)

  • Exactly where the last session left off: Beag asks Laidir why he thinks Beag is dead.
  • Laidir responds to Beag telling him everyone in the village saw his body and that he bore his coffin to the graveyard.
  • He persists that he doesn't believe it's actually him and that Pyralis is playing some sort of trick. Their back and forth squabble of dialogue becomes reminiscent of their past antics.
  • Eventually, this de-escalation leads to everyone waiting on the outskirts of town while Laidir fetches Shaman Gaoth.
  • He arrives back at the field with the Shaman and his Grandon Darach, Beag's other friend. Similar to Laidir, Shaman Gaoth is skeptical of Beag's unexpected arrival. He is extra skeptical that Pyralis seems to have sent a Paladin of Ordinus as their envoy. "You wear the cold iron of Ordinus into our homeland, paladin. The legends tell of a time where Sylvaris stood eye to eye with the mighty Ordinus, but we no longer live in those times..."
  • After tense deliberation, Gaoth is eventually convinced that Nathaniel Wells was using Beag as a martyr to drive military enrollment. They expose his lies from their perspective and off to prove they have the real Beag by inspecting his grave, per Thysbe's suggestion.
  • Reluctantly, Gaoth accepts saying he will accept the consequences of defiling a grave, stating it will be worth confirming if Beag is alive.
  • They dig up the rotting corpse and Thysbe inspects it noting that the bones are definitively smaller. He gets sick from the decay, but effectively proves the point.
  • After coming to terms with this reality, Gaoth invites everyone into the temple to discuss the real reason Pyralis has come to Raon.
  • They talk of many things including a mention of the vine and tree attack outside the village. Gaoth seems incredibly surprised, but affirms that this must have been an attack from Sylvaris.
  • They also mention the similar thunderbird attack while out to sea, which Gaoth speculates was a similar wrath from Aeviana, the Goddess of Sky, wind, clouds, and freedom.
  • Gaoth theorizes that the lesser Gods must be dissatisfied with something, and are beginning to outwardly express it.
  • XIV and Beag also explain the situation they are in because of Nathaniel Wells. They argue Raon is less safe from Resurgum now more than ever and ask them to refuse their tribute to the kingdom, instead giving it to Pyralis in exchange for protection from Resurgum.
  • Gaoth says they can only accept this offer if Pyralis can guarantee two things: 1) They receive active troops in the village for protection throughout the winter and 2) The party needs to appropriately deal with the Resurgum Tax assessors that will be arriving within a tenday.
  • They accept the offer by immediately sending the mail anywhere parchment back to Pyralis calling for reinforcements, and by hatching a plan to intercept the tax assessors.
  • Over the tenday, the party scouted the fork where the trees attacked them, eventually seizing the opportunity to kill the tax assessors and pin the attack on the supernatural occurrence that struck just days prior.
  • Upon return, the party asks Gaoth if he can help them find the Sun-Crest Chateau in Resurgum. He says he doesn't know where exactly it is, but that there are many manors to the east where wealthy nobles have property.
  • He gives directions to help find a Hermit (that sometimes attends communal gatherings) who lives near the valley where these vineyards sit. They can serve as a guide for finding the Chateau of interest.
  • Before seeing everyone off, Gaoth pulls Beag aside to tell him something in private. Then everyone departs.
