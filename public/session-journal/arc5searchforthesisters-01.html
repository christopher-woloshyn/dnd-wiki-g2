Session 71 - The Grand Auction Part 1

  • Over the past several weeks the party has accomplished the following:
    • Selene properly trained and prepared Fen to overtake her as the Dean of the Magic College
    • Voroth meditated and studied on why his connection to the weave is inhibited. He spends about 4 weeks without any magic and in this time, he works with some of the students to develop some new spells while Selene finished training Fen:
      • Acid Bomb
      • Illusory Double
    • Meanwhile, every single night Voroth is haunted by the harrowing presence of Transcendent Voroth in his mind. It IS beginning to drive him mad, and it's becoming a concern
    • Ethiul and Soren continue developing their relationship and caring for the white dragon egg that was found in the Ice Queen's Lair.
  • Some days later, Selene and Voroth wake up to a note from Morgan that reads:
going to town to run errands, might be a couple days or a few weeks dont really know yet. don't go crazy while we're gone. - M & T
  • Voroth and Selene are a bit surprised, but don't think much of it.
  • Some weeks pass with Selene able to help study and restore Voroth's connection to the Weave, and block out Transcendent Voroth's influence.
  • One day, one of the Student researchers, named Elowyn, come's in and tells Ms Selene that they may have a breakthrough with Voroth on their research.
  • In the lab, the students begin a ritual that they have done routinely, but they need her to contribute her high magical abilities. The goal of the ritual is to see if it's possible to enter Voroth's consciousness. If it's possible, then they can casts a high level imprisonment spell on Transcendent Voroth and seal away his influence.
  • She must follow the words and gestures that the students tell her to do the ritual. This expends a 7th level slot from her, and she ends up inside Voroth's Conciousness, greeted by Transcendent Voroth in a liminal space.
  • Voroth can hear everything, but can't interact with either of them.
  • "She's using you y'know? Why? Why do you want to steal from us? You only care about MY magic, you don't care for him at all."
  • "I can kill him y'know. He's MY hostage. You are both just pawns in my grand plan."
  • "You thought I'd let a weakling like him kill me? He WAS my escape from the prison. And his vessel is almost mine."
  • At the end of this encounter Selene must make a DC 15 wisdom saving throw:
    • On a pass she is not frightened. She also gains advantage against being frightened by Voroth forever.
    • On a fail she is frightened and ejected from his head taking 8d10 psychic damage.
  • She is cursed. Every time Voroth casts a 5th level or higher spell she must make a DC 10 + spell level wisdom saving throw or be frightened by Voroth for 1 minute
  • When Selene and Voroth come to, Voroth realizes he has a connection to the weave again and can cast spells.
  • Selene feels like she gained some knowledge from this (and is not able to cast simple domain expansion)
  • The researchers say that the ritual worked, but in order to perform the second part of the spell the party will need two things:
    • A relic statue of Chronos, the construct demigod of Mechanus. They say it must be an original relic forged in Mechanus by the Clockwork Angels. They are extremely rare, but often sought after by ultra wealthy aristocrats that collect artifacts.
    • A mage powerful enough to cast the imprisonment spell to lock away Transcendent Voroth
    • Upon further questioning, one might reveal that the ritual can be cast with an item called a Soulshackle Skull. This item is powerful enough to accomplish both, but might be more dangerous to execute because they won't have as powerful of a mage with them.
  • Selene begrudgingly acknowledges that her family might know someone who can help cast this spell
  • After this incident a few more days pass; Morgan and Tassarion return.
  • They exchange details about the things that have happened to each other while they were separated.
  • Morgan says they need to go to the Crosa City Auction to find more
  • They tell some of what happened and the party makes a plan to go to the grand auction
  • The party sets sail for Raenaros City to withdrawal funds from the banks for the auction, and to get directions to where the auction could be.
