Arc 2 - Vignettes of Truth: Rinelle

The steam in your suite at the inn is thick, scented with expensive imported oils meant to wash away the blood, dirt, and ash from weeks of fighting and sleeping on the ground. You are submerged in a copper tub, surrounded by the quiet luxury you've been trained to exploit. Resting side by side upon the vanity is a torn cardstock envelop, and the gold-embossed letter that lived inside. It's not signed, but you know who it's from.

As you reflect on the contents of the letter, you sink yourself lower into the tub until the hot water rushes over your ears. The ambient noise of the city fades, as you hear the buzzing of cicadas and feel a warm southern wind rustle your skirt. You are ten years old again, frolicking in the pristine gardens of the estate. In your innocent hands you hold a white daisy, and pluck the first petal.

"He loves me."

Your mind flashes to the present. You think of the letter. You think of him. The way he looks at you. He doesn't look at you like the others do. Of all the lovers you've been with, he looks at you with respect. A terrifying, unfamiliar normalcy. And for a moment, you let yourself accept it. You pluck the second petal.

"He loves me not."

The water in the tub suddenly feels ice cold. A thousand eyes loom over you. A thousand ears loom besides you. Your head hurts. The voices..."Why do you fear the dread?" The pain from your head shoots down your spine into your legs and feet. You legs won't move. "Shouldn't you embrace it?" You don't feel the water anymore. You don't feel anything.

"He loves me."

As the water sloshes you feel a comforting rhythm in the waves. You hear XIV and Thysbe playing games on the deck above. The looming presence is gone. XIV and Thysbe are playing games together. You hear Beag's booming laugh as he recounts another absurd and hilarious farm story from Raon. There's a sliver of autonomy. You draw a breath of free air.

"He loves me not."

The water is boiling hot! You grab the sides of the tub but your body is too heavy. "It's a trap." You hear yourself say. You're sinking down. The water is burning. "The world is cruel, Rin." Your skin is blistering, you want it to fall off. "He's using you, Rin"

Ten year old you stands before the daisy. One petal remains. Your fingers pinch the soft texture of the flower, and it begins to darken and wilt. It spreads rapidly to the rest of the flower as it rots away. The smooth summer air turns to a heavy, suffocating, damp smell of putrid decay. You begin to sink into the swamp. Desperately trying to cling onto anything before you know it you are being submerged in a mud that seizes your limbs and blocks your nose and mouth.

You jolt upright in the tub. gasping for air, water splashing over the tub's walls onto the floorboards. You violently look around as you catch your breath only to realize you are alone again in the dimly lit room. Heart still pounding your gaze fixes itself on the large silvered mirror above the vanity. The reflection staring back is who you are.
