Session 8

  • The party begins to travel around town attempting to warn the townsfolk of the impending calamity.
  • They meet a priestess in town that believes them, and wants to help.
  • She directs them into the forest to the north. There they can find the ruins to a temple that is purported to contain an infernal tome. It's an unholy site near the town that is seen as cursed and typically avoided by the village hunters.
  • On their way in search of the ruins, the party encounters an ambush by goblins, but swiftly defeat them, and they flee the scene.
  • Following their trail, the party happens upon the goblin's camp, where they are conducting a funeral for their fallen friend.
  • The whole scene is rather awkward for the party, but they get what they are looking for, and are able to leave and continue searching for the ruins.
