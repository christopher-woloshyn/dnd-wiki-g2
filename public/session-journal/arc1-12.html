Session 12 - Return to the Capital (2026-05-12)

  • In the aftermath of the chaos from the pirate ambush on the keep, Thysbe and Rin spoke with the business leaders of the Blue Rose to convince them to continue with the exchange since the threat has been neutralized.
  • They also communicated to both Damon and the Blue Rose that they believed Resurgum was behind the attack, although they technically didn't have proof at that time.
  • Afterwards, everyone went back into the town to meet with Raspa and Gorman at the Inn.
  • During this discussion, Raspa revealed that him and Thysbe are likely kin, sharing a father whom they have also never met.
  • Raspa also revealed to them that they received the information about the exchange from an "anonymous" informant who calls themselves "Wishing Well".
  • The party quickly deduced that this is probably Nathaniel wells, and received a letter with that information from Raspa and Gorman. This serves as the missing proof connecting Resurgum to the attack, and the group now has all the information necessary to build a full timeline of Wells' actions.
  • Also during this discussion, Rin grew tired of Gorman's continuous advances on her so she used her terrify spell causing him to piss himself in the corner.
  • Lastly, Raspa revealed the mechanics of the courtyard poisoning: Raspa's wings secrete a unique poison that can be used to selectively paralyze targets who have consumed it; he told Thysbe that as he ages that his wings should begin secreting the same substances.
  • He urged Thysbe that his wings are a curse, and one that should be relegating him to a life of oppression and people's fear and misunderstanding for what he is. He seemed almost envious of Thysbe while also assuring him that he is indeed cursed and will begin to see what he means when people learn the truth.
  • After this encounter, Thysbe, XIV, Rin, and Beag helped take the gold and this new information back to the capital.
  • Upon their return to the capital, a messenger from the Paladins of Ordinus arrived with a message from the Sovereign Pass: Their prediction was correct and the bridge was attacked by a small but elite strike team of sappers trying to destroy it
  • The defensive line was barely held, saved only by Helio holding the point alone at her brink with the timely arrival of the Paladins of Ordinus sent by XIV.
  • This defense came at a sever cost. Helio was horribly burned and scarred, over surging her fire magic fusing some of her metal armor to her skin and gaining to a cacophony of burn scars on the left side of her body.
  • Sir Salinor was crushed in the rubble of explosives used to try and destroy the bridge. He is currently comatose and needs to get his legs amputated. Only time will tell if he will wake up.
  • During the subsequent downtime, everyone in the party received personal letters in the mail.
