Session 53 - Into the Ice Part 1

  • This story takes place about 10 years before the events of the main story, and the events of this story are unrelated to all other main player characters.
  • Ulrich and Selene meet each other on the boat that's heading to whispering pines.
  • They arrive at Whispering Pines, a small town at the base of Frostspire Peak, surrounded in Large, towering pine trees.
  • Ulrich and Selene go to the Eagles Roost Tavern and meet the tavern keep named Eldra who posted the job. Here, they also met two others who accepted the job: Ulgrim Glonkin and A'dam Le'vene.
  • Eldra tells the party of Soren, the melted glacier, and how the task is to save him.
  • Shortly afterwards the party meets Calen, Soren's younger brother who is an aspiring cleric with no battle experience.
  • He agrees to help since he knows the mountain pretty well.
  • The party progresses up the mountain, and talk with Calen more to get to know more about him and whispering pines.
  • The party gets ambushed by some Gnoll's and a young Shoosuva outside of the entrance to a cave.
  • During the battle, A'dam gets wounded greatly, but is healed by Ulrich.
  • At that same time, a Gith monk named Bi'ch'az falls from the sky and joins the fight.
  • Ulgrim dealt the final blow to the Shoosuva ending the battle.
