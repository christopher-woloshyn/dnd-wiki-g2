Session 3

  • Gets a letter returned by Ethiul from the grove. Apparently she missed too many days of school and needs to get an alternate education.
Greetings,

It has come to our attention that you have not yet fulfilled the necessary requirements to complete your studies within our revered grove. Your mastery over the natural arts, while promising, remains incomplete, with several critical elements of the druidic path left untouched. Thus, we decree that you are to continue your training at the Frostspire College of Magic in Whispering Pines. It is there that you will be tested in both skill and spirit. You will be tasked with completing the following areas of study:
- Flora & Fauna Symbiosis
- Elemental Convergence
- Evocation, and Conguration Theory
- Foreign Language

Make haste, Fen'Harel, for the balance of nature waits for none.

In the name of the Grove
Archdruid Realta Mané
  • She is being sent to the Frostspire College of Magic to complete her certifications
  • Party travels to whispering pines. On the way, Tassarion plays Movement 3: Rhapsody of Haste continuously to make the trip faster.
  • At one point they are stopped by a band of pirates working for the Sonnet Swashbucklers looking for a license to pass into those waters.
  • Tassarion just tells them that the party are members of the Sonnet Swashbucklers, and is so convincing, that they apologize, pack up, and leave.
  • After the party arrives at whispering pines, they all travel to the Gatekeeper's inn where they meet Ulrich Thunder, Ulgrim Glonkin, and Selene Starweaver. They are the co-founders of the Frostpire College of Magic and Selene is the dean and main founder.
  • Ulrich reveals to Morgan the curse of his family and his quest for vengeance on Sarsynanth, the ice queen who placed the curse on his family.
