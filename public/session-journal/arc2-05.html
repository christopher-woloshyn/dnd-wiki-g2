Session 17 - Witches of the Lazaretto (2026-07-29)

  • The party departs the village of Raon following Shaman Gaoth's directions to locate a Hermit near the eastern vineyards.
  • Rin has resolve to finish what she started, but seeing Seraphina has her scared for what's to come once dealing with Wells is over.
  • Beag want's to take care of business but didn't strategize HOW they were going to do it, but he just imagined running in and punching people.
  • Thysbe is feeling envious that Helio get's promoted even though she got hurt and almost failed when they succeeded in their mission. This is why he is so determined to succeed again, so he can show the wings that he is just as deserving as her.
  • A lot going through XIV's mind, and he's feeling quiet with how much there is left to do. He's instead trying to stay in the moment and believe in his own actions and the path that he's taken will lead the world to salvation.
  • Everyone arrives at a hut with a short and stout old lady living inside. She introduces herself as Greta, and offers up tea to everyone.
  • Everyone drinks the tea aside from Thysbe who feigns drinking it (our of fear it could be poisoned), and everyone finds it absolutely disgusting. Beag also notes its nostalgic aftertaste: Greta did give Shaman Gaoth the recipe after all!
  • They all depart to travel through the valley. While walking, XIV begins to ask Greta a couple of questions regarding the monster attacks they've been experiencing. He mentions the thunderbird attack at sea, the tree attacks near Raon, and the black spore attack on General Valmor.
  • Greta agrees with Gaoth's theory that the natural attacks seem linked to the various "Natural Gods" as she called them:
      • Agathorr - Goddess of the Earth, Rock, Mountains, and Resolve
      • Sylvaris - Goddess of Life, Nature, Vitality, and Survival
      • Valdros - God of Change, Tides, Oceans, and Rivers
      • Aeviana - Goddess of Freedom, Skies, Clouds, and Winds
  • The thunderbird attack was likely Aeviana's Spite, and the tree attack was likely Sylvaris' Fury. However, the fungal magic's source was still unknown.
  • XIV mentions that his intel suggests the fungal attack traces back to a group called the Veiled-Sisters, but Greta doesn't know who they are.
  • Instead, Greta asks everyone what they know about the great plague of some 200 years ago.
  • XIV responds with a recount of common Pyrian history:
    • The Great Plague, or Black Rot as they refer to it, devastated Resurgum. Famine and death ensued, consuming everyone and everything, including the king himself.
    • King Caladrius, out of desperation to save himself, makes a deal with a Devil to end the pestilence upon the land.
    • The plague vanishes, however King Caladrius succumbs to his weakened body shortly afterwards; perhaps as a parting gift from the devil.
  • Greta exclaims that the people of Pyralis claim it was a deal with a devil, and the people of Resurgum think it was a heroic act of self sacrifice from King Caladrius himself. She says that both interpretations are lies and propaganda.
  • This remark angers both XIV and Thysbe, but Thysbe more visibly.
Please, calm yourselves.. If what you claim were true then where is this devil of legend? Why has it never shown itself?

That's because it was no Devil! It was the Natural Gods, Agathorr included. Agathorr used the precious earth itself to sequester the plague. Her along with Valdros, Aeviana, and Sylvaris together. But why do I bring up the Black Rot to begin with? I bring it up because... I lived through it... I am forced to remember it... The awful smell. The death. The rot.

Rumors from the times told of a group of witches; they developed their own "cure". Through sinister, eldritch magic they fused themselves with the rot itself and became a perverse new being. When the rot was sequestered by the Gods it persisted within them. It grew; it adapted; it evolved.

The magic you encountered that day is this perversion... The women you speak of, these... Veiled-Sisters... are almost certainly decendents of the "Witches of the Lazaretto" as we called them. It is a parasite that steals from Agathorr's lands to feed itself. It hears no prayers; it respects no gods; it honors no kings.
  • Following this discussion, Greta tells everyone there is a military checkpoint they must pass before entering the valley, but she will do all the talking.
  • The military checkpoint is strangely abandoned with no soldiers posted outside. Upon exploring around inspecting, the party finds guards slimed out with their heads crushed and smeared across the fort's floors.
  • This resulted in an ambush of stone golems that disguised themselves in the fort's floors and walls. As anticipated, the only person they don't attack is Greta.
  • While fighting, everyone deals damage to the golems attacking them including a massive double-critical team-up attack from Beag and Thysbe that nearly defeats the main golem in one hit.
  • Greta begs everyone to lay their weapons down, similar to how Beag was acting during the tree attacks. This begins with Beag and Thysbe who comply and is shortly followed by XIV and Rin.
  • However, the constructs continue to attack despite their effort to surrender to the point where XIV takes matters into his own hands (literally) and, with Beag, attempts to throw one of the attackers into the river and damage it as little as possible.
  • The main golem continues its attack which forces Greta to intervene and strike it with a final blow, dispelling all the other attacking constructs.
  • Greta apologizes and says that she felt Agathorr's aggression beginning to fleet, but there needed to be more patience and resolve before she felt the attacks would stop.
  • XIV, angry at Greta, argues that he wasn't about to die for nothing and acted how he saw fit.
  • Following this tense encounter, everyone continued through the valley until they reached the vinyeards, and specifically the Suncrest Chateau.
  • The manor looks like it's making preparations for the upcoming Gala, but the new year is still a few days away.
  • Greta says her farewells to everyone and they begin to strategize what they will do next.
