Session 11 - Courtyard Coup (2026-04-29)

  • Following their deployment as a fast strike force, the next morning began with Rin and Beag slipping out of their respective evening companions' rooms.
  • The group conducted a walkthrough of the keep again to prepare for the arrival of the forces from Pyralis and the Blue Rose Trading Company. They noted that individuals with matching tattoos were still present in the keep, and staff mentioned a formal lunch was scheduled.
  • The Kingdom of Pyralis navy arrived ahead of schedule, transporting a shipment of artifacts into the keep's temple.
  • XIV and Beag pulled a tattooed brute aside to interrogate him by pretending to be co-conspirators. The brute completely misunderstood, believing they were simply complimenting his ink.
  • Anticipating trouble, XIV inspected the temple and prepared a contingency plan to use his Suspended Rod to bar the doors shut.
  • The Blue Rose Trading Company arrived with auditors, mercenaries, and leaders, and began loading their gold into the keep while the formal lunch commenced.
  • During the meal, Rin grew suspicious of the waiter serving wine and warned Thysbe not to drink it.
  • Thysbe inspected the wine, discovered a tampered additive, and warned Beag.
  • XIV returned from his patrol, was briefed by the others, and confronted the waiter in the back room. The waiter implicitly confirmed nefarious intentions, but suggested it was already too late to stop the plot.
  • A sudden yell from outside triggered a paralytic effect on most who had consumed the tainted wine.
  • The tattooed individuals shed their disguises and drew weapons. Additional raiders appeared on the keep's roof, commandeering and firing the existing siege weapons.
  • XIV handed the Suspended Rod to an allied priest. Notably, the priests were unaffected by the poisoned wine despite having consumed it.
  • Rin and Thysbe remained inside to guard the gold, while Beag and XIV rushed to the courtyard.
  • Utilizing his strength, Beag threw XIV onto the roof, allowing XIV to engage the soldiers and neutralize the siege weapon.
  • Rin cast a defensive spell and Thysbe unleashed devastating area-of-effect attacks, clearing out a large number of enemies.
  • While airborne, Thysbe was attacked by an invisible assailant who whispered in his ear before stabbing him with the same paralyzing poison. Thysbe fell to the ground but was stabilized by Rin.
  • The party's proactive preparations significantly dwindled the attackers' resources. This granted the party a massive tactical edge leading to a successful defense with very limited casualties.
  • The skirmish concluded when XIV cornered the enemy, Captain Gorman, at sword-point, demanding the invisible assailant reveal themselves.
  • Another pirate-styled individual materialized and descended, exhibiting wings that closely resembled Thysbe's.
  • The winged assailant offered a trade: allow them to leave now, and they would meet the party later at a tavern to share information.
  • Reluctantly in control of the situation, XIV agreed to the terms, warning them against any trickery.
  • The winged individual threw a smoke bomb, grabbed the captive captain, and fled the scene.
