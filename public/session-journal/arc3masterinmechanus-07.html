Session 46 - Temple of Aevum

  • NPCs often use this phrase:
    • "May the Cogs turn ever in your favor"
  • Town - POIs:
    • Central Gate: at the center of the town. A permit is required to travel through the gate with a wait time of 102 "Turns" which translates to 3 "Material Years".
    • Cog Shaped Resting House: has supplies and a place to rest.
      • Riff: One of the local humanoids who searches the wastes for scrap to sell. Has info that many of the stronger constructs out there require a special "Adamantine Weapon" to be able to pierce their natural armour. He can spare an Adamantine Shortsword (200 GP), adamantine daggers (150 GP), and an Adamantine Rapier (400 GP).
    • Automata Library: Has info about the big gate. The gate connects the planes and can be used to access more than the material planes, including demiplanes and pocket dimensions. Different items can be used to unlock or activate the gate to transport to different places. The material plane is the most standard, but other items can be used to travel elsewhere:
      • Material Plane: Planar Metal
      • Feywilds: Planar Alloy
      • Shadowfell: Planar Ore
      • Spectral Hourglass: Personal temporal demiplane when combined with an artifact from that plane.
      • Catalytic Container: Personal Spacial demiplane.
      • Negative Cog: Astral Plane
      • WARNING: activating the gate without a key is extremely dangerous. Doing this could implode the gate and cause a swarm of chaos destroying everything in the area. (Creates a vortex into Limbo).
    • Temple of Aevum: has monks the order of Aevum. Lord Kairos is their leader. They say their job is to preserve important artifacts from the wastes as not to sever connections to the countless demiplanes in existence. They travel to check if each of the demiplanes still exist, or if they have been closed. They also check if they are inhabited or not. They are currently searching for a spectral hourglass in the wastes. (They can point to where it is.) The wastes are small pockets of disorder used to discard and cleanse the rest of the plane of chaos. They warn that many artifacts are guarded by their maker's constructs if eons past.
  • The Party went to the library to find info on the master and the portals that brought us to Mechanus.
  • They learned that there are keys that can take us to personal pocket dimensions and we can open them by binding the key with an object related to the person.
  • They went back to a tavern to rest before looking for monk's that might be able to help us find the master.
  • They learned that they need either a spectral hourglass or a catalytic container, and there's one in the wastes, in a dangerous ruin; the last squad of monks that went out did not return.
  • Kairos warns that it will be very dangerous. Her parting words are "Cogs be with you".
