Session 13 - The Taste of Truth (2026-06-10)

  • The session opened with a series of vignettes for each player character following the two week time jump of time spent in the capital:
    • Thysbe's showcased a flashback to his childhood when his wings first grew, which then cut to him sitting at a desk over the smoldering ashes of the letter he recently received. He then altered his appearance before regrouping with the others.
    • Beag visited Squire Helio in the infirmary, witnessing the severe burns and scars she sustained defending the Sovereign Pass bridge. She confided her fear and sadness in Beag: a stark contrast to her usual "Golden Child" persona, and she asked him to keep her vulnerable state a secret from Thysbe.
    • XIV was training in a field just outside the capital when he was visited by a young noble. The noble reported that an investigation into the King's High Circle regarding the betrayal of their father came up clean: there doesn't seem to be a mole that divulged any secret information to Resurgum.
    • Rin was alone in a bath, reeling from a severe internal mental conflict where happy childhood memories were heavily contrasted by a looming, dread-inducing presence in her mind. The internal struggle concluded with her jolting out of the tub and taking a long look at herself in the mirror.
  • The party reconvened at an inn, where Rin revealed an invitation she received from a former client for a New Year's Eve Gala being thrown in Commander Wells' honor to celebrate his promotion to General.
  • Despite Rin's suspicions that the invitation might be a trap, the group agreed to attend the gala to confront Wells. Thysbe expressed a strong desire to capture him, while the others agreed to assassinate him if capture proved impossible.
  • The party then interrogated a captive sapper from the bridge attack. Each attempt at torture left the prisoner pleading with the others, claiming those who believe in Ordinus are tyrants of authority, and committing heresy in the face of the Phoenix King. These claims were not aided by the subsequent torturous acts performed by XIV, a Paladin of Ordinus, immediately after these pleas.
  • Each act of torture XIV and Rin subjected the prisoner to continued to whittle away at her mental resilience and weaken her resolve which improved the potency of an "Ordinal Truth Tonic". As a result, XIV only needed a few drops for it to take effect pocketing the rest.
  • Under the tonic's influence, the prisoner corroborated the party's suspicions: she works for Wells, whose original plan was to burn the fields, sink the collateral shipment gold, cut off supply lines, and turtle defensively to wait for the Millennium Rebirth.
  • With his initial plans thwarted, the prisoner revealed Wells will likely pivot to similar sabotage tactics targeting humanitarian and logistical resources rather than launching a large-scale military invasion.
  • Thysbe also questioned the prisoner about the assassination of Xander Thalos, seeking information on the inciting incident of the war. She didn't explicitly rule out Wells' involvement, but noted that an assassination of that caliber would likely even be out of Wells' scale of influence.
  • After the interrogation, the prisoner's blindfold was removed. Upon seeing Rin while still under the tonic's influence, the prisoner reacted with sheer terror, screaming "What the fuck is that?!" and stating, "This proves my point, the tyranny of Ordinus making alliances with that!"
