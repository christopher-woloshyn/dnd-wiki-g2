Arc 2 - Vignettes of Truth: Thysbe

You are sitting in your dimly lit dorm room at the capital. Its close to the barracks where the Wings train, but squires aren't afforded the luxury of nice accommodations. As you sit at the desk the candle flickers dimly, as if it is on the brink of extinguishing. In front of you are the charred remains of your letter smoldering. The smell and shape of the smoke transitions to the Pyre of the Church of Cleansing Cinders in Shlackedorf. It is a bright morning, the air is cold, but the sun is warm; a brisk breeze rustles empty branches. Father Casper is in the middle of his sermon:

"...and thusly the day of reckoning and rebirth is almost upon us. Remember that it is up to us to use the gifts we are given to prepare ourselves, to prepare our communities, to prepare our bodies for what is to come.”

Thysbe is sitting in the pews listening, neither bored nor enraptured. This time of year, Close to the day of rebirth, is always the same sermon. It's familiar and repetitive like everyday day life in the village, when suddenly, a sharp pain of frozen needles marching from his tailbone up the spine, vertebrae by vertebrae, to his shoulder blades, and Thysbe doubles over in immense pain.

“Get back on your seat, boy, or I'll see to that you won’t sit for a week.” - Marten whispers through clenched teeth.

Stella looks at Thysbe with a concerned expression, but does nothing to restrain Marten’s aggression. Thysbe tries to raise himself, but collapses to his side, falling into the aisle of the pew. The sermon stops as he begins to convulse. His back arches as he cries out in pain. His clothing begins to rip revealing splitting skin beneath his shirt as blood leaks onto the floor, and he writhes. He writhes as everyone watches until eventually, small orange-red fuzzy wings sprout from his back. He lays there, quivering from the pain.

The townsfolk and even the priest stare in stunned silence. Stella looks horrified as her eyes begin to well up with tears. Marten stares at Thysbe with anger and disgust. He steps forward and snags Thysbe, dragging him into a church closet, smearing his blood across the church floor, the boy practically in tears from the pain. Stella trails afterwards and the 3 sit in silence as Marten lights a lamp.

“You will not embarrass me like that again, boy. Now stay here and collect yourself.”

Thysbe starts to sit up, wiping tears from his eyes as Stella strokes his hair and hums softly.

“What happened, mommy?” - He says

“You’ve been given a gift” - Stella replies warmly

“A gift?” - Thysbye

“Yes, dear. A gift” - Stella

“Then why did it hurt so much?” - Thysbe

“Sometimes Igniavis blesses us with things that can also hurt us. You know, like the prickly cactus mash we put in the healing salves:" - Stella

"It only hurts us if we don’t know how to use it.” - Stella and Thysbe recite together.

A moment of silence as the two smile at each other.

“But Mommy, why me? Why now? What did I do?” - Thysbe

Concern flashes across Stella’s face, masked quickly by a plastered smile.

“Igniavis is very wise, he chose you for a reason. He has to know that you are smart enough...” she nuzzles him a little “and capable enough…” he nuzzles back “to do great things with what you’ve been given”

“A gift.” - Thysbe

Thysbe stands taller with a bit more confidence, wings flashing over the bloodstain echoes on his shirt. There’s a commotion outside, and the door opens quickly as Marten and the priest return. Marten bearing an almost mischievous smile, and the priest's mouth agape with wonder and confusion.

"It... It must be Igniavis' blessing... Yes... Igniavis' Blessing indeed!" - Father Casper initially sounds confused but belts the second remark with confidence after rationalizing it must be the King's Blessing.

Marten clasps his hands, beaming, as the pews fill with a murmur.

Marten grasps Thysbe’s hand and pulls him out of the closet and the flock cheers. He then walks Thysbe down the aisle, as all the community leaders and church elders glad-hand Marten. Thysbe wanders overwhelmed. When he reaches the threshold, he sees Marten still talking with the priest, everyone surrounding the two of them. Off to the side, far behind the main crowd, Stella stands with a smile on her face and the traces of tears in her eyes.

She mouths to him “A gift.”

And he smiles back.

The scene darkens as you sit again in your chair at the desk. What do you do next?
