Session 6

  • With the weather worsening and Voroth without protection from the elements, Selene created a tiny hut shelter and everyone takes a long rest until the morning
  • The next morning, the weather looks much nicer and Selene receives a message from Ulrich and the others saying that the mission is still on due to the improved conditions, and they they should meet at the Rendezvous point.
  • Voroth, without cold resistance, must make a DC 16 Con Save. Failure results in 2d6 cold damage and level 1 exhaustion. He is able to resist the cold, and Tassarion gives him a torch to keep warm as they continue into the glaciers.
  • The party next needs to navigate across a frozen lake. With Morgan's light-footedness and Ethiul's perception, they are able to plot a path to avoid breaking the ice and falling through the lake.
  • While everyone is attempting to cross, two frost salamanders burst through the ice.
    • 2 Frost Salamanders
    • 3 Ice Mephits on high ground casting ice knife
    • 1 new ice mephit emerges from the lake at initiative 0 and get's put in the count.
  • During this fight Voroth goes down Voroth saw transcendent Voroth visit him in his mind, calling out his hubris and overconfidence, almost dying in battle as a result.
  • He is guarded and rescued by Selene and Morgan. After the encounter Morgan seems upset at Voroth; everyone finishes crossing the lake and seek shelter in a cave - the same cave the others stayed in the night prior.
  • Selene and Voroth send a message back to the others saying they need to stop and rest, and that they will meet a day later than expected.
