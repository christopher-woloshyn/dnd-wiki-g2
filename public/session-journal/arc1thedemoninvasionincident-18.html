Session 21

  • The party returns back to the Palace after the battle.
  • The priestess and High Mage inform the party that they are able to perform a ritual that can combine the obtained shards together into the Heart of Envy.
  • The priestess synthesizes the Heart of Envy, and with all 4 hearts obtained, the Priestess and High Mage declare that they will perform a modified version of the ritual to open a portal to Celestia, the celestial plane.
  • There, the party can enter and ask the celestials for aid in this calamity.
  • In Celestia, the party is greeted by cautious celestial guards. Tassarion, being a celestial warlock, explains their situation.
  • They take the party to a nearby altar, and ask them to sacrifice an item of great importance to them each in order to summon a goddess for help.
  • Once this is done, the summoning ritual summons the Goddess Nila Aashni, the demigoddess and child of the God of Celestia "Star Platinum" in the form of a purple spectral dragon
  • For such sacrifices, she offers to grant them one wish if they can prove what they can prove their righteous intent and prove their worth as warriors and adventurers.
  • The party fights the purple dragon form (Young Blue Dragon stats) and defeats it.
  • In a flash of light she reappears in the form of a biblically accurate angel and offers to grant them one wish.
  • After a lengthy debate, the party nominates Voroth to utter the following wish:
We wish for the portal to the infernal plane in [INSERT CITY NAME HERE] to have never existed
  • Everything fades to white.
