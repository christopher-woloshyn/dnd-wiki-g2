Session 9, 10 - Demon Rift Incident Part 5, 6

  • As the party continues searching for the ruins, they continue north, wandering throughout the forest.
  • As they continue, several skeletons rise up along with a couple of shadow-y manifestations.
  • The party is able to swiftly defeat them. The stronger demons seem to leave behind gems when they are killed that then party collects.
  • One of the skeleton's body's is a journal belonging to someone named "Gerald Williams"
  • The journal entry indicates that Gerald was on an expedition to find a tome that prophesized a great Calamity that will one day strike. He was hired by a mysterious person named "The Amber Stone"
