Session 57 - Ethiul's Call Part 1

  • Ethiul leaves Raeneros City to return to the grove alone.
  • When he confronts the Elders with the Robe they say:
    • “The Robe of Constellations has presented itself to you. Please don it, and look inward” Ethiul will feel a surge of magic; DC 15 Wisdom Save
  • He feels surge of determination, and feels the touch of his ancestors.
  • “Please tell us about your parents in your own words.”
    • Ethiul remarks how he was born an only child and that his parents were not very involved in upbringing
  • “Your parents were a strong foundation in your upbringing, doing what they could to mold you into who you are today, but they could not be intimately involved in your upbringing like some of the others in the grove, for they had sworn secrecy.”
  • “Please tell us about what you know of your sister.” Ethiul won’t know.
  • “Her name is Seraphina; you have only met her once, about 20 years ago; too young for you to remember.”
  • She was visiting the Grove one last time before departing for the Astral Plane to serve on the “Council of Cassiopeia” under the Starmother directly
  • Only the most powerful and accomplished Cosmic Vanguards have the opportunity for such an esteemed position under the Starmother; however from this point, the grove and Ethiul’s parents had to conceal her identity.
  • They can tell him now because he is undergoing the trials now.
  • “As you can see, your robe is incomplete, but it will become more complete and brilliant as you continue down this path. Now go. You must rest. The trials will not be merciful.”
  • "Remember that the wise person knows that life's challenges are not obstacles, but opportunities for growth, for it is through adversity that we find our true strength and wisdom."
  • Ethiul goes to sleep, and wakes up in the middle of the night to a forest fire ravaging through the grove (From this point forward, the trial will be in an unrevealed dream space. But more and more weird stuff will happen over time)

###### Trial of Courage

  • Combat: An ambush of goblins. A wave of fear, DC 20 wisdom save. Each round this will reduce by 5 until it's zero.
  • Introduce ancestors in initiative and they can defeat the enemies and escape the fire.
  • Ancestors can say their name, but can't yet reveal that they are ancestors
  • The Ancestors
    • Thalor "Taurus"
    • Daren "Draco"
    • Escorpio "Scorpio"
    • Leo "Leo"
  • Another cloaked figure tells them to come with them, they need to escape or the forest will burn down.

###### Trial of Wisdom

  • They run off into the woods. The party gives chase but that person disappears (because they are an illusion)
  • Party is lost in the woods, and needs to camp.
  • Try to orient with perception.
  • Notice this area of the forest is very strange... Spot creatures observing them in the distance with successful perception
  • Satyr appears and tells them a riddle:
    • "I am always hungry, I must always be fed. The finger I touch, will soon turn red. I kill kings and nobles alike, and even the strongest warriors cannot escape my bite. What am I?"
    • IF RIGHT: He tells them to follow him out of the forest. (DC 25 insight) to realize he will lead them to a trap)
    • IF WRONG: Burst of flames: a level 5 fireball
  • Continue walking, passive perception Ethiul notices they've seen the same tree multiple times
  • If they figure out that the terrain is an illusion, it will dissipate and they will find themselves in the middle of a village outside of a tavern.

###### Trial of Temperance

  • In the tavern is the annual dragonborn sex convention
  • Multiple dragonbornes approach Ethiul intrigued by the presence of an outsider. DC 15 wisdom save or be charmed.
  • If he resists this the trial will end and he will be approached by an elven woman in mysterious cloaks.

###### Trial of Justice

  • She asks Ethiul if he is ready. She says he needs to search within himself and restore balance to the chaos within.
  • He realizes the room he's standing in is now empty, and all of a sudden, just him and the woman are in
  • If he says yes he is appears in a chaos realm for the final battle
