Session 6 - A Still Rot Festers (2026-03-25)

  • The following morning, as everyone was donning their armor and packing up camp, Rin slipped off to search for the largest tree near the campsite. From their she paced north and west, and recovered some kind of item from a stump.
  • After departing, everyone traveled for the next few hours to General Valmor's last known location, according to the accounts of the soldiers they had rescued, and correspondence letters from carriages they had found.
  • It began to rain as an ominous fog sets in. General Valmor's elite guard each hangs from a large central willow. Their faces, strangled and bloated, with black spores poking from their decomposing heads. A putrid, Still Rot fills the air as their bodies fester with disgusting black fungal decay. They are surrounded by a radius of Black Gilled mushrooms.
  • XIV opens the busted door to the carriage and is jump scared by an amalgamation of fused, festering corpses.
  • Beag has never seen such an organism, but identifies the mushrooms as likely being poisonous. He initially believes this to be some kind of natural sign from Sylvaris.
  • Anxiously, Rin picks one of the mushrooms to inspect it more closely. While every one is looking at the plucked mushroom, no one notices the vines slowly lowering Valmor's guard, as they slowly reanimate before everyone.
  • A fierce battle erupts fighting the elite guard. They're strong. The mass of combined corpses from the carriage is also reanimated.
  • Beag has never seen magic that has this effect on creatures, and is mortified at such a blatant disrespect of Sylvaris' gift of life.
  • To negate incoming damage, Rin activates the item she obtained which contains a spore-like magic. While it is different from the horrendous still rot they are fighting, it does immediately draw a lot of suspicion towards her.
  • Thysbe debuts an alchemical attack that seizes the soldiers in a hardened green fire, giving Beag, XIV, and Helio the space to combine a powerful attack to defeat the fused zombie fungus, blowing it to smithereens.
  • Once the battle ends, the black fungal mass retracts towards the willow and back into the ground. XIV finds Valmor's ornate breastplate in the carriage.
  • Rin places it on the ground and channels some kind of magic to obtain a vivid vision of the scene when Valmor was ambushed:
    • She sees two women ambush the cart. One with dark skin and long braided hair wearing dark leather armor with red accents. The other, fairly tall, wearing an elegant white and light-blue dress with brown-gold accents.
    • The black spores attack, seemingly controlled by the first woman. They dispatch all the guards and pull Valmor from the knocked carriage. On his knees the second woman grabs him by the chin in a caressing fashion. After some time, Valmor collapses.
    • The scene advances, and a handsome, well dressed man with darker skin is talking to the taller fair skinned woman, listening to what she is telling him intently, as the darker skinned woman is stringing up the corpses to begin the decay process.
    • The scene advances again, and the two women are gone, but the man remains, talking to some people dressed as bandits. He hands them a sack, and they depart carrying Valmor, still unconscious, to the north.
  • The rain reveals these tracks in the form of puddles, and the group is able to follow this trail for a few miles.
  • They eventually find a very small bandit camp at the top of a small hill and clearing in the forest.
  • Thysbe scouts and spots only a few guards, and a man in a prisoner cage. XIV just casually walks up and takes 3 out while Helio takes out a fourth. Rin, Beag, and Thybe ambush from the flank dispatching the rest of the bandits leaving only the leader.
  • He claims to not know who it was that hired him and offered the money the man gave them to XIV if he lets him live. He also says their instructions were to hold General Valmor for 5 days and then release him. XIV chases him down and kills him without mercy.
  • The camp is littered with military grade arrows, rations, and weapons. The payment from the main is 1200 Aurellos which is the currency of Resurgum.
  • Rin frees General Valmor and everyone begins to ask questions. When they ask him what happens, he recounts that a monster attacked their convoy, and that it scattered his guard into the forest. He barely escaped alive before being captured by bandits and passing out.
  • Beag asks him if he knows the women that were at the scene when it happened, and he simply responds "What women"?
  • There are a lot of questions that remain from everyone. But the plan is to make camp near this camp and scout over the next few days to see if any of the suspects from the crime scene show up. Then, they will head west to regroup in the city where the soldiers and refugees were heading.
