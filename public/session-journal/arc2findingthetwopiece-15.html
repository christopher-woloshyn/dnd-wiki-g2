Session 36 - Search for the Two Piece Part 5

  • Morgan uncorked the bottle to the ship in a bottle, which released a pulse of magic energy.
  • Luckily Ethiul was able to throw off Cleff from their trail by lying to him about what it was (nat 20 smh).
  • After this, Voroth was able to secretly identify that the ship in the bottle is actually a magic item called the Ship of Theseus.
  • Voroth was also able to suppress the magical presence of the compass and reoriented it to point towards the source of the sand that was in the bottle.
  • With a new objective and a boat to get there, the party waited until night to slip out of the cargo hold and abandon ship. They did so with acute stealth, a few wild shapes, and some invisibility.
  • Once off the ship they waited until it was sufficiently far away before activating the Ship of Theseus, and set course for the new direction of the compass.
  • Given the size of the ship and the lack of experience most party members have sailing, they will have to work exhaustingly hard to maintain the ship's course.
  • Eventually, the party spots an island in the distance; that's where the treasure must be!
