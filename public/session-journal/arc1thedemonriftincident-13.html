Session 16 - Demon Rift Incident Part 12

  • With the Heart of Wrath in had, the party wants to enter the city for refuge, and to seek additional help from the Alderman of the city to solve this problem.
  • As they approach the bridge and main gate, they notice a massive line of refugees, and a lot of crowding.
  • There are also a lot of guards posted at the gate, more that normal.
  • The guards state that they are unable to accept any additional refugees.
  • Through some clever trickery, Tassarion is able to turn invisible, and trick the guards into granting the party passage into the city.
  • Upon entering the big city, Fen gets nervous because she has never been in a city like this before, so she wild shapes into a mouse and hides in Voroth's pocket.
