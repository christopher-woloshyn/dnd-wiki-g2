Session 48 - The Wastes Part 2

  • The party traverses into the dungeon down a long hall (after taking like 10 minutes to go down some stairs).
  • In this hall, there is a pressure plate (DC 14) that, when triggered drops a massive plate (DC 16) 4d6 damage. They are able to avoid and disarm the trap.
  • This main hall leads to two construct guards: One lies one truths logic puzzle. Failing to solve the puzzle will lead to a battle and locking the exit.
  • Morgan thankfully heard this one as a kid, and they are able to solve the puzzle.
  • This reveals a room to a study or laboratory. After some arcana checks, they discover that Ethiul's tattoo is somehow connected to a rune representing balance. There is also a spell scroll that opens the alternate door which leads into a steam and cog filled hallway of traps.
  • Voroth and Fen are able to use some spells to disarm the traps, and advance towards the end of this room.
  • Then, there's a quite tense argument between Morgan and Melody that leads to him storming off with the rest of the party, leaving Melody behind.
  • They advance to the final clockwork platform where they are met with a clockwork dragon construct as the guardian of the dungeon.
