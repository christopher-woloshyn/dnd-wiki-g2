Session 1 - The Battle of Sruth River (2026-02-21)

  • The campaign begins with vignettes of each player character interacting with someone from their backstory:
    • Thysbe was receiving instruction on the upcoming battle on the Sruth river from Sir Salinor. He appears to be smaller and weaker than everyone else despite having divine wings and being a squire. The Loremaster assures Thysbe that "true devotion is found not in the shape of the gift, but in the resolve of those who wield it."
    • Beag found himself facing his tribe after being conscripted for battle. As a parting gift, the Shaman gives him a carved statue of Sylvaris, and tells him "the tallest trees catch the most sunlight, but the deepest roots fear no storm. Grow ever upward, but never forget the dirt that makes you strong." After departure, Beag finds himself with his friend Laidir receiving orders from Commander Nathaniel Wells about defending a critical point along the Sruth river.
    • XIV was sitting in the back of a supply cart, talking to a cloaked figure he referred to as Sifu. He is being sent to join other Paladins of Ordinus to support the front and capture a critical point on the Sruth River. This is his first real combat; Sifu assures him: "the blade that is never drawn remains sharp for when it is needed most, but steel not tempered by the flames of battle risk cracking when struck." As he departs, XIV says a prayer to his God.
    • Rin is in an ornate room alone with a handsome young noble. He tells her there are rumors of a plot to kill Prince Aric and that they aren't able to spend much time together as a result. He expresses distain for the idea of going to war over a ritual, and that he has to play a part in commanding people to their deaths. He wants to be vulnerable with her and tells her "Society sees a porcelain doll, but I cherish the cracks that form beautiful complexity. Don't let society paint over you."
    • Rin then finds herself in a different person's room, Caspian Valmor, some time later. This person is very pompous and cringe, and is trying to win her affection. Their encounter is interrupted by servants in the manor requesting that Rin join General Valmor in greeting and treating some of his "Top Brass" for their well fought victory on the Sruth River. She accepts while also successfully calming Caspian from his spoiled crashout
  • The scene shifts to a large active battlefield in the forest. XIV makes a heroic debut, rescuing a soldier from an arrow heading right towards him, and single handedly making an advance to the high ground to take out several archers.
  • Thysbe and the other squires successfully divert the attention of the War Mage that was causing a lot of issues on the battlefield; however while he may have succeeded on the mission, it wasn't the show of bravery he wanted to convey to Sir Salinor (and the others).
  • Beag and his friend Làidir were on the battlefield fighting for Resurgum. Together they were almost able to turn the tide of the battle or at least hold the line long enough for reinforcements, but the reinforcements never came and their last-ditch effort was finally thwarted by the trap set by Sir Salinor and Squire Helio. Fearing for his friend Làidir's life, Beag was able to create a window of escape before being taken prison with some other Aurelian Soldiers.
