Session 14, 15

  • Eventually, after several day's travel, the party arrives at the outskirts of Raeneros City. There are a few streets lined with housing, shops, ports, etc. all servicing the city.
  • After searching for a bit, they eventually find the priestess among a small congregation of Sulis worshipers.
  • They say they have learned more from having a few more days with the tome.
  • In order to obtain the heart of Wrath, she says she can perform a ritual to combine the lesser demonic soul gems the party has been collecting from defeating the various demons they have encountered in the past.
  • They all hike into the forest a few miles to perform the ritual.
  • This summons a large, much more powerful demon compared to anything they have fought prior.
  • The party is able to defeat the wrathful demon, and obtain the Heart of Wrath.
