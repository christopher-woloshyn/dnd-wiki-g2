Session 5

  • A bunch of frantic little monodrones begin running around speaking an unknown language (Modron).
  • The monodrones begin following some sort of threat protocol which keeps escalating.
  • Voroth and Tassarion are able to understand the language they are speaking, but cant speak it.
  • Eventually, a tier 4 drone tells them they are not permitted to speak to them directly, but that they must have the proper identification, permits, and clearances to travel to Mechanus.
  • Eventaully this escalates to a tier 5 construct which instigates that all undocumented trespassers into Mechanus must be removed, regardless of whether or not it is intentional.
  • Voroth rips a fireball which immediately evaporates dozens of monodrones. The party eliminates the threat, and from the ashes, Voroth determines that the eye of the tier 5 construct contains runes that indicate safe passage.
