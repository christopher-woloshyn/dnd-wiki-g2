Session 17 - Demon Rift Incident Part 13

  • The party makes their way uphill towards the innermost ring of the city. Once inside, the guards were much more receptive to their cause.
  • Eventually they make their way into the city palace, and here they meet Alderman Jeremiah.
  • They exchange information about the hearts and about some of the contents of the tome.
  • Jeremiah tells them about a battle they fought to the south of the city and takes the party into another room.
  • He pulls back a curtain, and reveals hundreds of soldiers from that battle that have all been petrified into stone.
  • He reveals that they all fell to a demon that they eventually defeated, and obtained a blue gem.
  • The party knows this gem to be the Heart of Sloth; therefore, they have 2 of 4 total gems required
  • A few days have passed since the party arrived at the palace. The Priestess and the City's (extremely old) High Mage have discovered that the gems are used in a summoning ritual to open a portal to the infernal plane.
  • After studying the ritual, they believe the stones can be used to perform a modified version of the ritual that can open to any plane of their choosing.
