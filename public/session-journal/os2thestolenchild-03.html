Session 2

  • After building the stage and setting appropriate traps, the concert began.
  • Isadora was able to call up the target, a very young aged elven girl being carried by her mother.
  • They spring the trap, causing a massive eruption of magic in the crowd and killing many civilians.
  • Erenhardt is able to secure the target and begins to flee the scene.
  • The mother resisted but was killed as her daughter was pried from her desperate hands.
  • A huge battle proceeded to ensue, as the party attempted to escape from the village.
  • In the turmoil, Isadora was able to feign ignorance of the attack and was able to escape quietly.
  • Back at the original tavern, the party met back up with the patron, received their payment, gave them the target, and all went on their separate ways.
