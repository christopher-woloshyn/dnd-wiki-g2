Session 31 - Enter Melody!

* Since it was just a description, there is a miss and the party TPs to a nearby island instead: dickbutt island.

* After catching their bearing, Ethiul and Fen turn into sharks that Tassarion and Voroth can ride with Morgan waterskiing on the back

* They arrive at the island that matches their visions after swimming for a few miles. The locals are all really nice and chill surfer dudes.

* They enter and meet Melody Songstrike. She explains that she left the Sonnet Swashbucklers out of fear for her safety. She thinks someone within staged a coup to obtain the keys to Songbeard's treasure: The Two Piece, and take it for themselves.

* Then she reveals that she is Morgan's true mother, and that Morgan having the sand charm means he was the one chosen as a the heir to the Two Piece.

* She gives the party the Compass of the Homesick and explains that the compass and sand charm are the two pieces needed to find the Two Piece.

* She gives Fen a +2 Moon Sickle with an inscription of Moon Druids which is an heirloom to her former grove before their forceful relocation and merging with Ethiul's commune.

* Before the party leaves, she says that their paths will cross again, and to find her engagement ring which she believes to be part of the treasure

* She also says NOT to trust the Sonnet Swashbucklers.

* The party uses their teleport scroll to teleport back to Bidrian's Biddings.
