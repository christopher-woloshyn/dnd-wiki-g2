Your eyes are jolted awake, the world around you has stopped, the rain materializes as drops of water in the sky, the howling winds non-existent. In front of you stands a singular cloaked figure, you've seen this figure once before, years ago when you swore yourself to him. Corsair.

"Morgan, you're right. You've bared the storms, you've fought in my name, you've bled, you've carried my freedom, my name, your vengeance and the chaos of this world. "Morgan, you will not die here today. You've been loyal, accept this gift. Go forth and conquer this battle, as your greatest trial still lie ahead, you will know what must be done. You both have earned this for your devotion. Let her remind you that your life must not be one pure sacrifice."
