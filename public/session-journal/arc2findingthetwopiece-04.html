Session 25 - Enter Morgan!

* The party travels to the shanty town.

* It is pretty run down and only contains fishers and criminals living in borderline wretched conditions.

* Here they meet a man named Morgan, a former member of the Sonnet Swashbucklers and Bastard son of Songbeard the Pirate.

* He agrees to help them fight the Siren and obtain the Harmony Medallion, since he also knew Melody Songstrike and wants to talk to her to learn more about the demise of his father after his banishment from the faction.
