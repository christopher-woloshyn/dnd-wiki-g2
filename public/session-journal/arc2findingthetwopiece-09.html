Session 30 - Bidrian's Biddings

  • Everyone gains inspiration from last session because it was funny and impromptu
  • The elders at the grove reveal that the best way they can find answers to where or what the location in the vision might be, they should go to the city and seek a powerful wizard that can help them identify or transport to different locations.
  • From here, the party travels to Raeneros City and are able to obtain passage into the middle ring, where they find a magic items shop and tower called "Bidrian's Biddings".
  • Tassarion buys another Harmony medallion, Morgan gets a Medallion of mimickry.
  • Jobidrian reveals a prized piece of his collection: a purported original work from Johann Sea-Bastion Bach: Rhapsody of Haste and asks Voroth if he can identify anything about it, but Voroth can't and he gets mad at Jobidrian because he doesn't care about music.
  • They then negotiate to get teleportation services to and from the location from the vision. Jobidrian performs the teleportation.
