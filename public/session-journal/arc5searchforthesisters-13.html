Session 82 - Who is Erin Otherday?

  • We began the session with Selene, Voroth, and Ethiul at the door having heard Morgan's insane proposal.
  • Morgan and Tassarion are in a tense standoff, as Morgan presents his proposal for the location of Tassarion's daughter, Maris.
  • After a tense and difficult deliberation, Tassarion accepts Morgan's offer, under 3 conditions:

1. Morgan must promise to help Tassarion rescue his wife.

2. Morgan must assist Tassarion in hunting down and eliminating every individual responsible for the attack on his family.

3. Morgan must aid Tassarion in dealing with the consequences of defying his Patron.

  • Tassarion hands over the Baton of Johann Sea-Bastion Bach and the other music pieces to Morgan, who secures them, and in exchange Morgan reveals that the "Divine Magistrate's Embrace" is a powerful artifact that bears the name "Otherday." The party realizes that Tassarion's daughter is none other than Erin Otherday, a revelation that shocks everyone.
  • With the new alliance and understanding, Tassarion confides more about his past and his patron, revealing that both him and Maris share the same patron: Davy Jones.
  • He also reveals that the ritual he has been preparing for is to be performed for him.
  • Shortly after this discussion there is a knock at the door which reveals Erin, who was looking for the party after they had been gone for a couple of hours.
  • Morgan and Selene take Erin to a secluded room to tell her the truth about Tassarion.
  • Erin immediately denies their claims, stating that she had visited her parents' gravesite and that her father's name was Terrance Everclear.
  • Morgan and Selene dismiss these lies and take Erin back to the main room to confront Tassarion.
  • Ethiul notices Erin's face begin to tremble as she struggles to maintain her denial.
  • The truth is finally revealed when Tassarion casts disguise self, transforming into his wife, who bears a striking resemblance to Erin.
  • Breaking down in tears, Erin apologizes for her confrontational and confused behavior, and she pleads with the party not to kill Justin.
  • Erin casts a spell to ensure the party's promise not to harm Justin. Everyone but Ethiul resists the spell, with Ethiul willingly failing the saving throw.
  • The session concludes with a heartfelt montage of Voroth packing up to travel to Raenaros.
