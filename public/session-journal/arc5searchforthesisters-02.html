Session 72 - The Grand Auction Part 2

  • The party traveled to Raenaros City through the Barron's Pass to withdrawal funds from the bank.
  • The party has access to up to 850,000, and Morgan decides to take all of it to the Auction, not knowing how much they would need.
  • The Party then traveled to Bidrian's Biddings to catch up with Jobidrian, ask him where the Crosta City Grand Auction is, and introduce him to Selene.
  • Selene then purchased a Deck of Illusions from Jobidrian
  • The Party then departed to cross the Sea of Cascades on a several weeks journey to Crosta City
  • On their travels, the party encountered two ships that tried to rob them of all their treasure they were travelling with, but they were lame and swiftly defeated
  • Tassarion killed one of the surrendered pirates who was begging for their life.
  • Eventually, the party arrived at Crosta City, docked the Ship of Theseus, and checked into the auction.
  • They needed to front 50,000 GP in coin as collateral to prove they can afford to attend the event, but this comes with getting custom fitted suits and dresses, and they get fancy inn rooms for the duration of the event. The party is assigned participant # 1734
  • They also received a schedule to the event and made an appointment to get fitted in the morning

Grand Auction Schedule; there are two main auction halls; East and West:

- Day 1: Opening Ceremonies
- Day 2: Art showcase 1 in both halls
- Day 3: Magic Item Showcase in both halls (Est. 100-200)
- Day 4: Tomes and Grimoires Showcase in East Hall, Art Showcase 2 in West Hall (Est. 50 - 100, 100 - 200)
- Day 5: Real Estate Showcase 1 in East Hall, Music Showcase in West Hall (Est. 100 - 1000, 25 - 100)
- Day 6: Relic Showcase in East Hall, Real Estate Showcase 2 in West Hall (Est. 50 - 500, 100 - 1000)
- Day 7: Signature item exchange - a ceremony held to showcase the grand exchange the highest valued items from each showcase.
