Session 81 - The Divine Magistrate's Embrace

  • The party slowly awakened on the floor of the ritual chamber in the Orphanage to Dr. Bernoulli, Erin, Micah, and Justin all watching closely with intent.
  • The professor told the party that Voroth is still alive, but still unconscious.
  • Eventually, when the party awakened him, he explained that the Voroth's who lived in the pursuit of power are always less prosperous than the ones that do. He also realized that their experience only contains the scenarios where Voroth is the one that fails to save the party, and therefore can never consider the realities where Voroth is never there. As a result, he severed his connection to the weave and gave his knowledge and power to his apprentice to finish the job he started.
  • After some heartfelt exchanges, Voroth declared his intentions to move to the outskirts of Raeneros City, and begin writing and publishing his knowledge from all the other Voroths.
  • Cleff offered Voroth 15,000 GP to help with moving to Raeneros City and establish himself, and Voroth thanked the party for everything, and ensured them that he will keep working to help.
  • Justin, declaring the effort a success, announced that he will hold a celebratory feast to commemorate Voroth's "Cure" and the return of Selene.
  • The Party rested for the night, and Morgan snuck off to ask his question to the Divine Magistrate's Embrace
  • The next day the party went to the southern markets to do some shopping while waiting for the feast. Morgan hung back at the Orphanage.
  • Selene went to a book store and purchased an elegant stationary set for Erin as a gesture saying she wants to stay in touch. She also purchased a graphic educational poster of the schools of magic and the weave for Micah.
  • Tassarion borrowed some funds from Cleff, and purchased 4,000 GP worth of diamonds.
  • The party returned to Morgan talking to Erin in the dining hall as the preparations for the feast were being completed.
  • Selene sat at the top of the room next to Erin and Justin. He proposed a toast to Voroth and the party for their successes and for bringing his daughter back. Selene said a few words, and presented the gifts to Erin and Micah before also begrudgingly thanking Justin for the help as well.
  • After the Feast, Morgan took Tassarion aside to another room, and told him again that he wants him to give up the ritual, this time proposing a deal: Tassarion gives up the ritual for good, and in exchange Morgan will tell him where his daughter is.
