Session 4

  • He quickly threw a smoke bomb at the party and used the cover to exit the room.
  • The party chased after him, able to spot him in the crowd.
  • Mirikian dashed forward and tackled him causing the whole bottom floor to erupt in a panic.
  • The guards began rushing to support their leader, but as soon as it had started it was already over.
  • Morgan had him on the ground surrendering and on the brink of death, a vengeful look in his eyes.
  • The party pulled him into a back room for interrogation with a few of the guards while the rest of the staff began evacuating patrons.
  • Morgan told him to answer every single one of their questions. He asked more about the Sonnet Swashbucklers and learned:
    • One of their members frequents his ship and is on the way as they speak.
    • One of the fabled works of Johann Sea-Bastion Bach is getting auctioned at the Crosta City Grand Auction in about a month.
    • The ship with the eldritch cannon likely belongs to captain Quincy Tett, and not Maris herself because she remains in hiding
  • Aqua asked again about her father, and While Ace didn't know much, he did say that there were some members of the Melodic Marauders that were fancy on Merfolk, so that would probably be the best place to continue searching.
  • Tassarion then began asking questions about the ship itself. He learned that The Marooned Sailor is Ace's greatest gamble ever; he used money from an odd job from 30 years ago to fund this venture and it ended up paying off big.
  • Tassarion then asked what the job was. He replied just a random kidnapping job in a small village north of Coastgate. Tassarion continued to probe?
  • "What Village?"
  • "Oh gosh, I cant' remember... Sib? Sibbeon? ... Simbeon?"
  • "Sirion?"
  • "Yeah Sirion! Wait... how did you know that?"
  • Tassarion grabbed him by the throat and choke slammed him into the wall.
  • He realized this man must have been in his village 30 years ago when his wife was murdered and his daughter was taken away. HE is responsible for his turmoil.
  • He continued to interrogate: "Where is she?!" "Who Hired you?!"
  • Ace pleaded for his breath and his life, but he couldn't answer these questions. He just performed the job and got paid.
  • Tassarion, restraining Ace against the wall told him "I'll see you in hell you absolute filth." and cast disintegrate on him, killing him instantly and turning him to a pile of dust.
  • Tassarion began to weep, and from the ashes of Ace's corpse emerged a magic flintlock that Morgan grabbed to hold on to.
  • After the party came to, Morgan commanded the guards and staff to begin loading all the platinum and gold from the vault onto their ship, and that they could take whatever they wanted afterwards.
  • The party prepared for the arrival of Captain Brian Teyy, where they would stage a trap in the lobby
  • The moment arrived, and Brian Teyy showed up with a few other crew. Springing the trap, Morgan emerged from the party's hiding spot and demanded more information, but Brian refused.
  • He accepted defeat upon arrival, and facing the resolve of death he fought against them until the bitter end, nearly taking Aqua down with him. With his last breaths, he declared to Morgan that he'd be back.
  • After the battle, the party fled from The Marooned Sailor, and set a course for Raeneros City.
  • There, Tassarion, Morgan, and Cleff went their separate ways from and sought out a bank to deposit all of the coin they stole while Aqua and Mirikian grouped up to continue looking for her father by finding some of the Melodic Marauders who might be able to lead her in the correct direction.
  • After depositing the funds, Tassarion, Morgan, and Cleff continued northward to Whispering Pines, to meet up with the rest of the main party and tell them of the progress they've made and the things they've learned.
