Session 69 - The Marooned Sailor Part 3

  • The party scanned the room to see dozens of tables playing various gambling games.
  • Among Roulette, 3 Dice, Fool's Fortune, and black jack, the party decided to play a round of black jack, winning about 100 GP among all of them across the various games.
  • Morgan then went back to the front desk to inquire about the higher tier packages.
  • Morgan convinced everyone to go for the Admiral's tier which cost each person an additional 800 GP per person but would give them access to both floors two and 3.
  • Then everyone went upstairs to gain more information on who "The Marooned Sailor" could be and where to find them.
  • The second floor contained a brothel with several staff members walking around with patrons in scantily clad clothing.
  • Tassarion ordered a glass of goat's milk from the bar, but the tender needed to go upstairs to the kitchen to get it.
  • Meanwhile Morgan spoke with a wealthy couple that was stopping from upstairs. He learned that "The Marooned Sailor" is an older human with an eyepatch, and that he often plays high stakes with customers in the casino.
  • With this information the party continued upstairs to investigate the top floor.
  • The top floor contained a restaurant and theatre; Cleff convinced the party to eat at the restaurant.
  • After eating at the restaurant, the waitress invited the party to watch the show which would be starting shortly. It was a new musical called "The Tragedy of Gorman the Pirate"
  • To introduce the show, a man matching the description of "The Marooned Sailor" given by the wealthy couple downstairs.

  • Everyone seemed to enjoy the show except for Morgan, who thought the plot was very similar to his own backstory, but a lot less favorable to the character representing his likeness.
  • They then proceeded to head back to the casino floor and go to the high-roller room.
  • Upon entering, everyone felt a shift in presence, Especially Tassarion and to a lesser extend Morgan; this room seemed to have an anti-magic field.
  • The same man was in the room: The Marooned Sailor. He was an older Human with gray hair, an eyepatch, and a large leather hat. He introduced himself as "Ace Maverick" and claimed to have information, but would only exchange it with bets. on a number guessing game.
  • He pulled out a gun, loaded a bullet in the chamber, and spun the cylinder.
  • Aqua went first, and bet her hand. She asked if he knew anything about who her biological father could be, but she guessed the wrong number and had to pull the trigger instead.
  • The gun didn't fire, but it advanced wherever the bullet was to the next chamber.
  • Next, Mirikian bet his hand in exchange for 1000 GP, because there wasn't really any information he was looking for. He guessed the correct number, won the bet.
  • Morgan went next, and he bet his knee and asked a question about Maris' whereabouts as well as for more information on the Sonnet Swashbucklers. He also guessed correctly and learned that the information he has on the Captains isn't up-to-date, and that he doesn't know exactly where she would be, but the captains definitely would know.
  • After a few more rounds of guesses, Morgan asked another question, but this time had to bet Tassarion's arm. He guessed the wrong number, and the gun clicked advancing the chamber again.
  • Morgan, in a miraculous bout of perception, spotted the bullet in the chamber and learned that the next shot in the gun is the one that will discharge the bullet.
  • Morgan was able to make another bet, this time a 50/50 roll, but Ace didn't seem nervous at all about the outcome. Morgan guessed odd, and the number rolled, even, meaning he loses.
  • He pointed the gun to his head and at the last second changed targets shooting Ace in the chest.
  • Silence erupted through the room, and he looked down at his chest, but there were no marks. He smiled and stood up, asking Morgan smugly if he didn't think he'd expect something like this from his clientele; the bullet was a blank!
  • He leaned in to Morgan and asked him if he knew why he loved gambling so much to which Morgan replied "let me guess... it's because the house always wins?" (sarcastic tone)
  • "That's right, Morgan, the house always wins." - ROLL FOR INITIATIVE!
