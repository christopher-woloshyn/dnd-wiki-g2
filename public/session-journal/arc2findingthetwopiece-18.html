Session 39 - Found Her

  • After everything settles down for a bit, Tassarion takes out the baton and conducts the movement again. This spawns the phantom crew which gets to work preparing the ship and hauling up the dingy with the treasure.
  • Then Morgan takes Cleff into the captain's quarters and convinces him to join the party's crew.
  • Through this questioning, we learn that:
    • Lyra and Austin followed them probably using some scrying spell.
    • Their task was to obtain the baton at any cost, but they wanted to do it diplomatically because they are intimidated by the party's strength and didn't have many numbers.
    • He doesn't know who the leader of the Sonnet Swashbucklers is, they have been very secretive since obtaining power, but after learning from Morgan that Melody Songstrike is alive, he suggests she knows who this person would be because she was the leader following Songbeard's assasination.
  • When the party takes a long rest, Voroth, Fen, Ethiul, and Tassarion receive a dream vision together in a liminal space with a disembodied voice:
    • It says: "Excellent work. You found her, and she lead you to Songbeard's Treasure. What will you do next?"
    • Voroth asks: "What should we do next?"
    • The voice responds: "You must do what I have always ask you to do: Find her again."
    • Voroth discerns that this "Vision" is much more vivid and detailed than past visions. Could the skull key that we obtained (that Songbeard wanted Voroth to have) be the means for this elevated level of communication?
  • The next morning, the party wakes up to Cleff cooking breakfast, ready to set sail towards what lies next.
