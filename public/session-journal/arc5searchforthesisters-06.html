Session 76 - The Grand Auction Part 6

  • Relics Showcase
    • Cicero's Coin of Two Fates - Flipping it can change fate and reality itself, but its true nature is unpredictable. (25)
    • Statue of Chronos forged in Mechanus by the Clockwork Angels (20)
    • The Toque of A'dam L'vine, Executive Chef of the 2 Gatekeeper Star restaurant "Buoys like Jagger" - Advantage on checks relating to cooking + Chef Feat (12)
    • Holy Chalice of Sulis - Dug from the ruins of Sirion (65)
    • King Aldric III's relic ring, said to once contain 3 wishes. It still has magical potency, some experts say it still contains 1 wish while others speculate that it doesn't. (295)
  • The following day, the party attended the exchange ceremony and collected the items they purchased from the auction, which are:
    • Soulshackle Skull (175k)
    • Divine Magistrate's Embrace - (220k)
    • Tome of Leadership and Influence - (80k)
    • Tome of Clear Thought - (85k)
    • JSB Movement 4 - Symphony of Freedom - (280k)
  • Total Spent: 840k / 950k, Total Remaining: 110k + 50k deposit (160k)
  • After acquiring the items from the auction, the party met with Lyra on the floor one last time. Morgan again emphasized that he intends to kill Maris, and will do whatever it takes to achieve that. She responded saying Songbeard was not the idealized man Morgan thinks he is, and that the Swashbucklers may not take to his righteous cause like he thinks they might.
  • Following this discussion, the party set a course for Coastgate to find Selene's family so she could ask them for help with the ritual to imprison Transcendent Voroth. The party was extremely diligent to ensure they weren't being followed out to sea, since they just acquired nearly one million GP worth of items.
  • That night on the ship, Morgan had a vivid dream at sea on the Ship of Theseus alone in a terrible storm. Recognizing this dream as extremely similar to the vision he had when he first swore his oath to the God Corsair, he shouted to the sky asking for help and looking for answers.
  • These shouts went unanswered, as lightning struck down onto the ship, lighting the masts on fire, and jolting Morgan awake in his room.
  • Morgan stepped outside, to make sure the ship wasn't actually on fire and everything was in order. Before turning around and going to bed, however, he noticed a crack in the ships sleeping quarters, and peeked in to discover a cloaked figure standing above Selene.
  • After alerting the party, a small altercation took place, revealing the intruders as scared children. Before anyone got hurt, though, Their leader announced themselves from the Crow's nest and descended down to acknowledge defeat and apologize for the inconvenience.
  • Morgan, suspicious this person was sent by the swashbucklers, but Selene recognized the leader's voice as her Sister Erin.
  • After a surprising reunion, she ensures the rest of the party that she is here on personal business: She caught word that a group called "Melody's Tempest" had purchased almost a million GP worth of items, and wanted to score something for herself.
  • She also expresses disappointment with Selene and her disappearance from their family, saying things like "father is furious with you" and "When mother died a few years ago, you didn't even write us". Selene said she didn't even know these things occurred.
  • After catching up a bit, Erin asks if she can stay the night on their ship to spend more time with Selene before heading out, to which Morgan reluctantly agrees.
  • Everyone goes back to sleep with Selene and Erin having a slumber party like they used to when they were kids.
