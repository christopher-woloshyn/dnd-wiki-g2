Session 15 - The Martyr (2026-06-30)

  • As this battle ensues, Beag notices his effigy to Sylvaris begins glowing with a primal bright green magical aura. Sensing these creatures are animated through Sylvaris' will, he attempts to stop them from attacking his friends, but to no avail.
  • XIV, Thysbe, and Rin are all attacked while Beag desperately tries to intervene. At one point, he even attempts to protect everyone by having them touch the effigy which causes the glow to cease leading to even him getting attacked in that instance.
  • After the threats are eventually dealt with, the party inspects the scene, and the cart more closely. They discover with the mail several hundred propaganda posters using Beag's likeness calling for recruitment into the military. Thysbe then finds a letter on the dead soldier they were talking to:
Lt. Daniel

In addition to your standard courier routing and duties, you are instructed to distribute these posters in the villages you visit. Try to target the young men and inspire them to join our cause. Tell of them the heroic story of 'Beag the Strong'.

- Captain Sanders, Aurelian Vanguard Regiment 18
  • These orders harbor the speculation from Thysbe that Resurgum faked the death of Beag to turn him into a martyr for their cause.
  • As the party continues towards Raon and enters the fields on the outskirts of the village, Beag spots his friend Làidir working in the fields.
  • Beag excitedly runs over to him only to be met in contrast by a horrified reaction from his friend seeing him alive. Rather than relief or happiness, Làidir displayed pure dismay attempting to run away and alert the village.
  • This resulted in a small altercation with Làidir being tackled and pinned, getting the wind knocked out of him, and Beag asking him point blank "Làidir, why do you think I'm dead?"
