Session 1

  • Cleff asks the party what they are going to do next.
  • Everyone wants to head back to Raenaros City to gear up.
  • Cleff warns them that they should avoid the harbor because of the Sonnet Swashbucklers being on the lookout for them.
  • The party snuck in through the north gate and made their way to the middle ring. The next morning, everyone stocked up on rations, weapons, armour, and spell scrolls.
  • After an exchange with Jobidrian, he learned more about the works of Johan Sea-Bastion Bach and vowed to help the party look for works. He wants to hear the full concerto from end to end before he dies.
  • Cleff hired a carriage and provisions to get the party to the MoonStar Grove.
  • There, Ethiul learns that it is his time to become a Cosmic Vanguard. To do so there are 3 rites of passage.
  • The first rite involves him getting a magic cosmic tattoo called the Lifewell Tattoo. The second is a quest to find a Robe of Constellations: a relic worn a used by Cosmic Vangaurds of the Past. The third is a trial put on by the elders. Succeeding the trial will prove Ethiul worthy, and bestow upon him an artifact. Between the three rites, Ethiul's journey will be complete and he will be a fully realized Cosmic Vanguard. There he will meet the Starmother for guidance with this newfound power and responsibility.
