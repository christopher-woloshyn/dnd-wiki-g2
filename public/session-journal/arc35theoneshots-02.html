Session 54 - Into the Ice Part 2

  • The decided to rest in the cave and restore strength before advancing deeper
  • There are tracks leading deeper into the cave, so the party decided they were on the right track and to venture deeper.
  • Deeper in the cave, the party found a notebook around some blood stains in the snow that read:
"Show your faith, turn, enter darkness, and pray to the light."
  • Eventually the party arrived at a large cavernous room with a deep gorge.
  • After some creative attempts to get across and some ominous advancing statues, they figured out that by extinguishing all light sources, that a spectral bridge would eventually reveals itself.
  • Finally, the party arrived at a temple that was seemingly hidden all the way at the base of the mountain.
  • In this temple, it was revealed that the lady that hired the party, Eldra, was actually an undead "Necro-vore" the entire time. Her plot was to open a rift to "Davy Jones' Locker" from within the temple to consume the souls that pour out for more and more power
  • The party erupted into a big battle against her and her undead minions.
  • They were successful in defeating her, but not in rescuing Calen's Brother Soren, who remains trapped in a necklace pendant.
  • After returning back to Whispering Pines, A'dam decided to move on, continuing to search for new and exotic foods throughout the realms, and Bi'ch'az returned to the Astral Plane.
  • Selene, Ulrich, and Ulgrim remained in whispering pines to help rebuild the town and free Soren from the necklace.
