Arc 1 - Vignettes of War: Beag

  • Fade in: The air is crisp with a breeze the rustles the surrounding fields causing sunlight to ripple of the crop like a pebble breaking the surface of a pond.
  • Beag, you stand tall in front of your tribe. They face you and a select few others with an anxious excitement for your undertaking. Behind you sits an army of hundreds, in marching formation ready to depart at the command of the cavalry standing tall upon beautiful beasts. What are you feeling right now?
  • An older man at the front of the crowd you're facing approaches you. Looking straight up at you he reaches up and places a wood carving of the Goddess Sylvaris in your hand before softly saying: "The tallest trees catch the most sunlight, but the deepest roots fear no storm. Grow ever upward, but never forget the dirt that makes you strong."
  • The cavalry gives their command, and the soldiers begin to march, as your tribe, friends, and family solemnly see you off.
  • A few days pass and you find yourself standing amongst mostly strangers; the largest concentration of people you have ever witnessed, even more so than the festivals you've regularly attended since childhood.
  • Standing in a crowd of soldiers receiving orders from a handsome, well dressed man with darker skin; his banner bears the seal of the phoenix. Sheathed on his hip is an elegant rapier as he sits upon a beautiful white steed.
  • "We push into the tree line today. The enemy will hit us hard. They will try to break our spirit and scatter our lines. Your orders are simple: Hold the line. Dig your heels into the soil of our ancestors. Your stand here today—your unyielding sacrifice—is the anvil upon which we will break their kingdom. If you hold them here, in these woods, you protect your families, your farms, and your very way of life. The Phoenix King watches your bravery. Do not give them a single inch!"
  • Beag, you stand before this man and his cavalry. Next to you is your friend Laidir with a smirk of determination and confidence. How does this make you feel?
  • Beag Responds
  • End Scene
