Arc 1 - Vignettes of War: Thysbe

  • Fade in: Rays of sunlight are beaming through the beautiful stained glass of the temple as the golden hour illuminates the bookshelves in the back of the room. Before you stands two older men: One wearing elegant plated armor bearing the seal of the Phoenix, and the other wearing the clerical robes of a scholar.
  • Sir Salinor: "Tonight we set out towards our rendezvous point where we will eventually make camp. This is will be our first critical assignment supporting the frontline operation to secure a forward position on the river Sruth. We are going to move fast and precise, and we are going to hit HARD! During the offensive, we will break into teams: Red team will attack the enemy from a cross position to create pressure and draw attention for the gold team to attack the direct flank. This in combination with frontline pressure from our main forces should collapse their defensive position. Squire Thysbe, eyes up here! Do you understand the directive?" - Sir Salinor
  • "This will be a difficult and dangerous assignment, but this is what we have all been training for; if you die out there, then that just means you're too weak to serve the wings.
  • "Sire, you mentioned two teams, what would you like me to do for the mission?" - Squire Helio (kissing his ass).
  • "Helio, you will of course join me on the Gold team while the other three will be the red team. Are there any other questions? If not, then everyone make sure you are properly rested and ready; we leave in a few hours and will all be flying, this time we are not in a position to wait if you're too slow..."
  • As everyone dissipates from the study walking out and talking together, Thysbe, you overhear chatter and speculation from the others if you will be able to keep up, and as fleeting golden hour turns to dusk you find yourself last in the room. How are you feeling?
  • The older man in clerical robes remains behind and approaches you: "Don't mind the chatter Thysbe. I've seen the things you're capable of, and I've seen your determination. You may not be as flashy, but just because your gift has manifested differently from most it doesn't make it any less valuable. True devotion is found not in the shape of the gift, but in the resolve of those who wield it."
  • Thysbe responds
  • End Scene
