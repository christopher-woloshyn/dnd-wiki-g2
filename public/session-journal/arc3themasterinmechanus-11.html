Session 11

  • The party returns from the ruins to the temple. They ask Lord Kairos if there is anything she can do to help expedite the application process. She says to check back with them after they rest at the tavern.
  • The party goes to the Gear Shaped Resting House and purchases rooms for the night
  • The next morning, Melody invites everyone to join her for breakfast. Morgan blows her off which makes her sad so she asks Fen for some advice.
  • She asks her how she can prove to Morgan that she loves her and that everything she's done, she's done for him. Fen simply says that she can't just say it but also has to show it.
  • She asks Fen if she thinks the Master's Temporal Demiplane exists outside of time, and that if so, she'll tell Morgan then.
  • The party heads back to the temple. Lord Kairos says that she thinks the party can bypass the line by filling out an application and having the temple sponsor them. Since they have a tier 3 clearance orb, they can use that to get their application approved instantly and bypass the line.
  • They all go to the gate office together, submit an application, wait a bit longer, and then are permitted to enter the Master's Temporal Demiplane.
  • They arrive at his space, he is wearing hooded robes; the party are all magically silenced. He then pulls his hood off and reveals that he's also Voroth.
