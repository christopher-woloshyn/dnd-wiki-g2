Session 24 - Find Her Part 2

* Party runs into a sketchy guy in a tavern at Riverside Crossing who knows of the person they are talking about. She is not a cleric (in this timeline), but the former leader of the Sonnet Swashbucklers. The region is host to several musical pirate factions, the most notable of which are the:

* Sonnet Swashbucklers

* Melodic Marauders

* Bardic Brigands

* Concerto Corsairs

* The mysterious man has a letter from Melody Songstrike that he believes contains information helpful to locating her. He will trade it for a Harmony Medallion: a magic item that can be obtained from a Siren and will meet them in Saltmarsh in a week to exchange the items.

* Tells them the best place to start looking is to search around the small shanty town on the north shore of the region
