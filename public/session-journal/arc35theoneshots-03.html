Session 55 - The Stolen Child Part 1

  • This story takes place about 30 years before the events of the main story and represent the events that take place as a part of Tassarion's backstory. The target of the attack was Tassarion's wife and daughter.
  • Tavern start, the party is brought together by hooded humanoid; he hired everyone to capture a target alive.
  • The target is a very young girl in a small town to the east called Sirion.
  • This party of kidnappers is comprised of:
    • Ace Maverick, gunslinger extraordinaire.
    • Erenhardt, a human barbarian.
    • Isadora, a tiny halfling bard.
    • Brother Harold Isaac Thaddius Leopold Esquire Reginald II.
    • Luc'a'pizza, a dragonborn paladin.
  • On their way to the town, the party encountered a security checkpoint and were able to convince the guards to let them pass to the town.
  • Upon arrival at the town, the party began to search for the target. There weren't that many houses to check, but they didn't want to come off as suspicious.
  • Eventually, they were able to identify a house to the south as the one that likely contains the target, and proceeded to hatch an elaborate plan to host a concert for the town, since Isadora is an accomplished musician.
  • The party proceeded to spread the word and build anticipation for the concert.
