Session 85 - The Search for Maris Part 2

  • The next morning, the party made a plan during breakfast in the tavern. Selene cast a sending spell to Isadora, asking how they should find her. She replies giving them a password to tell security: "Sirion" - The name of the Tassarion's town she attacked all those years ago.
  • The party travels to the city of Diyun via a teleportation circle spell provided by Selene which they used at the university.
  • From there, they navigated to a checkpoint by giving the password, and were escorted to an area to leave behind their weapons and magic items. Morgan was really annoyed he had to Afterwards, they were escorted to a VIP box to watch Isadora's concert.
  • Morgan used the open bar at the concert to once again get exceedingly drunk.
  • After the show, they meet Isadora backstage accompanied by a few body guards.
  • She offers Tassarion information about her co-conspirators in exchange for them to agree to a magical pact which would grant her immunity from any pursuit by the party. The contract reads:

This magical pact, herein known as the “Accord of Isadora,” is hereby made and entered into on this day between Isadora Underhill, hereafter referred to as the Affiant, and the members of the faction known as Melody's Tempest.

The Affiant, Isadora Underhill, agrees to provide the following information regarding the incident of the Sirion kidnapping approximately thirty years ago: the full, truthful names of all involved parties, their known professions at the time, and their last known locations and activities. This information shall be inscribed upon the back of this scroll upon both party's signatures.

In exchange for this information, and upon both party's signatures, the Affiant is granted a magical covenant of non-interference. This covenant binds the following individuals, who have granted their consent and power to a single representative signatory:
- Morgan Songstrike
- Tassarion Lanthir
- Selene Starweaver
- Ethiul of the MoonStar Grove
- Voroth

The aforementioned individuals are bound to the following terms:
1. Cessation of Pursuit: They shall, from this moment forward, cease all direct pursuit of Isadora Underhill. This includes, but is not limited to, using their abilities, resources, or agents to track, locate, or harass her. 
2. Abandonment of Vengeance: They shall abandon any and all intentions of enacting personal vengeance upon Isadora Underhill for her role in the Sirion incident or any other past wrongdoing.
3. Non-Interference: They shall not interfere with the Affiant's life, business, or travels. This clause does not apply to a chance encounter where the Affiant is the aggressor or initiates an act of harm against a member of Melody's Tempest.

Should any signatory of Melody's Tempest knowingly and willingly break any of the terms of this Accord, the Affiant, Isadora Underhill, shall be granted an immediate and irrevocable magical recourse. The violating party's connection to the weave of magic shall be severed. They will suffer a permanent inability to cast spells, use magic items, or benefit from any magical effect that they did not create themselves, for a period of one hundred  years or 50% of their remaining lifespan, whichever is larger. The Affiant shall also be absolved of all duties and protections under this pact.

This contract is to be bound by the blood and will of all parties involved. By signing below, the full terms of the agreement and the magical consequences for its breach are acknowledged.

X__Isadora_Underhill_<3__
X__Tassarion_Lanthir_____
  • Tassarion agrees to these terms, and signs on behalf of the party.
  • A parchment appears in his pocket detailing the names and current statuses of the remaining conspirators:
    • Luc'a'pizza (alive); he runs a pizza shop on the north side of Coastgate called "Pizza Pizza".
    • Erenhardt (alive); he works on a farm associated with the village of Plowshire.
    • Brother Harold (alive); he lives in the slums on the south side of Coastgate.
    • Ace Maverick, as the party already knows, is deceased.
  • With this information acquired, the party teleports back to Coastgate and Morgan asks what they should do next.
  • Tassarion insists they prioritize Morgan's quest to confront Maris, stating that the other matters can wait.
  • The next day the party meets with Dr. Bernoulli at the university again, who reveals that the skull keys from the Sonnet Swashbucklers are magic fixtures that create entrances to a hidden, demiplane-like space.
  • Based on this, the party theorizes that Maris is likely hiding in a secret citadel accessible only with a skull key.
