Arc 1 - Vignettes of War: XIV

  • XIV, as you sit in a covered wagon full of supplies a hooded figure stands before you, standing behind the wagon: "You are to join your fellow Paladins of Ordinus at their camp a few day's travel from here. Tomorrow begins a major advancement to establish a strategic defensive post at the Sruth River. You must not falter: this is a test for you, are you ready?"
  • XIV Responds
  • Excellent to hear. Remember your training, remember your oath, and remember: the blade that is never drawn remains sharp for when it is needed most, but steel not tempered by the flames of battle risk cracking when struck."
  • XIV Responds again. The wagon beings to roll, and you watch the hooded figure recede in the distance as you depart for battle.
  • End Scene
