Part 2: The New Moon Ritual and Ascension

  • On the night of the new moon, Ethiul, accompanied by the egg, journeys alone back to the Glacial Spark. The stars are incredibly vivid without the moon's light. Ethiul positions the egg at the heart of the Glacial Spark. He begins the complex ritual, channeling the raw energy of the spark and the cosmic power of the blazing stars directly into the egg.
  • As he reaches the climax, he intuitively unleashes a focused Call Lightning spell. However, instead of thunder and raw electricity, the sky above him shimmers with concentrated starlight, coalescing into a beam of pure cosmic energy that descends onto the egg. This isn't just a spell; it's a conduit for the universe's will.
  • The egg glows intensely, then cracks. A young white dragon emerges, but it's not merely a "Young White Dragon." Its scales shimmer with faint constellations, its eyes hold an unusual depth, and it seems to radiate a serene, almost celestial cold. This dragon is rapidly aging under the ritual's influence, growing larger and more capable before Ethiul's eyes. It now carries a unique blend of glacial essence and cosmic energy; the white scales have tinted with a faint cosmic purple/pink, each with unique bright spots that faintly twinkle like the night sky.
  • As the ritual concludes and the dragon solidifies in its accelerated state, Ethiul feels an undeniable pull – not physical, but spiritual. A shimmering portal, like a window to the cosmos, opens before him.
