Session 87 - Entering The Pirate's Citadel

  • "This is what you get for trusting the wrong side, you will get nothing in the end." - DJ to Tassarion
  • The party arrived at the center of the Sea of Syncopation to find a large fleet of pirate ships guarding the entrance to the Pirate Citadel.
  • A massive battle erupted, with fireballs and powerful spells being cast by both sides.
  • Morgan began creatively rearranging barrels of alcohol and gunpowder on a lower ship deck, intending to use them as bombs.
  • Tassarion, in a bold but reckless move, teleported directly to the center of the most heavily-manned ship, where he was quickly overwhelmed and went down.
  • He was saved, however, by his daughter, Erin, who had joined the fray to assist him, expending her most powerful magic to do so.
  • Tassarion and Selene unleashed a cacophony of firestorms, resulting in a colossal explosion that destroyed a majority of the enemy fleet.
  • In the chaos of the blast, Selene was launched from the ship into a random direction but remained safe.
  • In the quiet aftermath, Morgan used one of the glowing skull keys to open a strange door in the Captain's Quarters.
  • The party stepped through the glowing light and found themselves instantly transported to the interior of a house, leaving the decimated battlefield behind.
