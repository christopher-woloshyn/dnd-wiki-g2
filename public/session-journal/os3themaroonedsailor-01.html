Session 1

  • Following the conclusion of the expedition into the glaciers, the party decides to rest in Whispering Pines for some time.
  • Morgan, impatient with the others' focus on other tasks, and the overall lack of urgency towards his goals, decides to pursue them alone during this downtime.
  • Tassarion notices there's something wrong with Morgan and approaches him.
  • Together with Cleff, they decided to team up and spend the remaining down time searching for Maris, or a way to get to her.
  • Cleff informs them that they still have at least 4 weeks of downtime given how long the transition process is taking for Selene and Fen.
  • They decide to split up to prepare for an expedition; tassarion buys two weeks worth of supplies and rations, Morgan writes a letter to the rest of the party and they bring everything to port to set sail.
  • They leave on the Ship of Theseus to head to saltmarsh on Cleff's suggestion as it's the closest settlement that will have plenty of sailors with potential leads.
  • Ask how many days of supplies they want to take with them.
    • 5 GP per day of supplies, of which they get two weeks worth.
  • As they travel south through the sea of syncopation, a thick fog sets in.
  • As they continue traveling, there's a break in the fog and they see a sinking ship
  • The beginning of this encounter will run as normal initiative until about 4 or 5 rounds in when Spirits and ghosts begin emerging from the whirlpool each round.
  • In total 3 sailors got sucked into the wirlpool, but due to Aqua's quick thinking, two of them were able to breath underwater and narrowly avoid a terrible haunted fate.
  • After rescuing the crew, they said the other ship had a cannon that fired ammunition capable of creating the whirlpools; it feels like sinister magic.
  • The whirlpool seemed to pull sailors below sea and release their souls as banshees.
  • Morgan and Tassarion, realizing that two of the people they rescued in particular were extrodinarily stronger than everyone else began to get suspicious.
  • Cleff urged everyone rest until they arrive at Saltmarsh
