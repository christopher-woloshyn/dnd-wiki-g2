Session 52 - Master in Mechanus Part 3

  • Are you transcendent because you're Voroth, or are you Voroth because you transcended?

Melody Reveal

  • Morgan before we leave, I want you to know that I can tell you everything, but you need to promise me, that you're ready to hear it.
  • I want to tell you what happened on the day your sister died.
  • Many years before it happened, your father told her that he planned on giving the two piece to you and not her. You were a prodigy, and she was not a capable pirate; not in the slightest (and a woman)
  • Ever since then, she changed.
  • She became so focused and bent on getting his approval that it is all she would ever focus on; but your father never really had a knack for the ways of Magic...
  • Eventually, it must have been that enough is enough... I found letters on her desk for assassins. But the horrible thing was, the goal was not to kill your father, no, the goal was to kill you, Morgan.
  • She wanted the treasure for herself and would stop at nothing to get it.
  • Seeing her fall down this path, darker and darker, I had to intervene.
  • I confronted her, and together we came to an agreement...
  • I along with the assassins would help stage attack on Songbeard and use it to fake her death, and frame you as a scapegoat.
    • Morgan, your sister is alive.
  • She would use this opportunity in isolation to cultivate her powers, and get stronger
  • With the only heir being a banished bastard, she would come back once she felt she was strong enough and present herself to Songbeard as a magical miracle
  • But she severely miscalculated.
  • One day she returned, Songbeard wanted nothing to do with her
  • He must've felt betrayed, and ironically this probably only strengthened his resolve to give the treasure to you
  • I was in town fetching supplies when it happened, she planned around us being together and me being able to defend him...
  • Once I heard the news, I knew I had to flee. I knew right away there would be a target on my back, and I needed to do everything I could to protect the compass to the Two Piece.
