Session 7 - Reunion in Hearthward (2026-04-01)

- Two days pass while scouting the bandit camp. There are no traces of other soldiers or the women.

- With General Valmor still suffering from some migraines, Rin suggests everyone escort him back to Hearthward, the city where the rescued soldiers and refugees were all heading.

- After 5 long days of travel, everyone approached a queue of refugees being stopped by guards a few miles from the city's main gate. After informing him that they had rescued General Valmor, he immediately escorted everyone to the city to take him to the military reserve camp.

- On there way there, they passed a refugee in tattered clothing on a box preaching that the immolation has already begun, and that soon the rest of the world will be cleansed in ash.

- Thysbe, XIV, and Helio didn't really think much of it: just another distressed refugee displaced from their home as this war escalates.

- While he's preaching, two city guards tackle him off the box and arrest him for expressing fringe views about Igniavis and the Millennium Rebirth.

  • When everyone arrived at the military reserve camp, they were introduced to General Edward Marshal: a logistics focused commander trying to balance the collapse on the front with the influx of refugees to Hearthward. Sir Salinor was also present.
  • General Valmor was delivered to the commanders with great relief and to high accolades, something Helio was very proud of. Everyone was briefed about the strange scene and circumstances surrounding the Valmor's capture and release.
  • Rin, attempting to dig deeper, offered to attempt her magical commune again, this time with General Valmor directly. According to her, she was able to see Valmor's perspective but revealed that it appeared his memory had been altered by some means.
  • With this, Salinor proposed resquesting a Loremaster from the wings of life come to inspect Valmor, and attempt to restore him. It will take 2-3 day for them to arrive.
  • General Marshal then requested a detailed report of the entire incident for their records. Sir Salinor assigned Thysbe to the task, but Helio interupted saying she would write a more impressive report with her noble calligraphy skills. After some back and forth, Salinor chose Helio to stay back instead, to Thysbe's irritation.
  • Rin suggested everyone follow her to meet her Aunt Rowena who she said lives in the city for dinner and a nice bed. She took everyone to "The Ugly Duckling" Brothel in the market district. Thysbe gets the kid's bracelet.
  • Rin pays for a table with bottle services on behalf of everyone, and Beag is impressed with how different 'restaurants' are in the big city compared to his village.
  • Rowena arrives and is relieved to see Rin is okay. She says she caught the fastest vessel from the south to find her. Rin tells her everything they had all been through over the last tenday.
  • Rowena then urges Rin that if the others saved her life, and she trusts them, that she needs to tell them her secrets, to which she reluctantly explains:
    • In youth, her village in the southern states was attacked. No one knows why or by whom.
    • Following this incident, she was rescued by Druids, who took her in and taught her much of her nature magic that she knows today.
    • After several years, she was found and adopted by her Aunt Rowena: A wealthy merchant who taught her manners and the ways of nobility: "I taught Rinelle how to get what she wants from men who are too stupid to know better, just as I did to foster my wealth."
    • When Rin got older, she set out on her own to discover who was responsible for destroying her village. Believing it had to be either Pyralis or Resurgum.
    • Since, she has been investigating both kingdoms, consorting with their nobility, and trying to answer this question.
  • Rowena said that based on her independent research, evidence of these kinds of strikes never had traces to them, only the signature of a mastermind who wasn't afraid to used the most ruthless of tactics to achieve his goals:
"For this man, the end justifies any means necessary: pitifully ambitious for whatever cause he might think is righteous. The only thing I'm missing now is a name to that face."
  • Rin remembered the note they found on the soldiers heading west. It was from a Commander N. Wells. Beag recounted he was conscripted by a Commander Nathaniel Wells. This would be the same person Merrick said left him and Beag for slaughter when the war camp was attacked. The same person who commanded the burning of Thysbe's village. If he did all of these things, he must have had information on where Valmor was going to be that day; he must have been the man from Rin's vision.
  • XIV simultaneously makes this same revelation at the table. Rowena asks everyone that if they are confident its him, they should go after him and kill him. Thysbe and XIV say they will defer to whatever they are ordered to do, but intend to take this information to their superiors.
  • With an immediate plan decided, Rowena sends Rin to have a bath, and while she is gone, she implores the others to ask her questions about Rin directly. She doesn't deny that Rin still has secrets, but tells everyone:
It may not look it, but Rinelle is very shy and deeply self conscious. Please do not press, and please do not tell her I told you. The attack on her village left her scarred, both mentally and physically. She uses her magic to mask the physical scars, and she adopts personas to repress the mental scars.
  • She asks everyone if they accept this request, not just for her sake, but for Pyralis' as well. Beag agrees it will help save innocent lives. Thysbe agrees contingent upon his superiors assigning him to the mission once learning, and XIV agrees contingent upon a question he writes on a piece of paper.
  • Rowena reads the paper, folds it up and hands it back to XIV. She replies: 
"XIV, this is the kind of question Rinelle is perfectly suited to find the answer to. If you continue to prove yourself to her.. to us.. she will help you get the answers you are looking for."
  • As Rinelle returns from her bath, Rowena offers to pay for everyone's room across the street. She thanks them for considering her request and gives the group some items:
    • Schema for improved Hand Runes for Rin
    • Suspended Rod (XIV)
    • Calming Pendant (Probably Thysbe)
    • Arcane Cloak (XIV, Rin, Thysbe, or Helio)
