Session 18 - High Stakes Pregame (2026-08-05)

  • With a couple of days until the gala, everyone wanted to capitalize on the extra time by investigating the venue.
  • After observing some of the servants, Rin used glamour in front of the others for the first time to disguise herself as a maid.
  • This surprised everyone since this is the first time she has openly used this ability somewhat in front of them, normally choosing to use it in her own privacy.
  • She begins to snoop around after passing the guards and entering through the front entrance.
  • Minutes later, Beag approaches the guards loudly and pretending to be intoxicated as if he accidentally arrived to the party several days early. The guards direct him to leave, but not before he could draw the ire of those also patrolling the rooftop, leaving a window of opportunity for Thysbe to slip into the stairwell.
  • Narrowly escaping being spotted in the hallways of the third floor, Thysbe manages to hide just in time and explore some of the guest rooms.
  • Meanwhile, Rin inspects the first floor, coming across an administrative room with a woman yelling at some servants. She discovered this woman is Lady Erika Vance, the wife of High Archon Vance, the host of the gala.
  • Rin also learned that this “Vance” person, her husband, is very notorious, serving on King Aurello III’s royal council directly.
  • Lady Vance is in desperate need of flower arrangements and centerpieces to which she tasked disguised Rin with taking care of. Rin decides that Thysbe can use this as his cover for getting through the front door for the actual event.
  • In her continued scouting, Rin also learned where the main ballroom is and most importantly, where General Nathaniel Wells will be staying during the party.
  • Thysbe continued scouting, dropping down to the second floor from the outside. In his excursion, he located where Vance's personal study is, and also likely where the master bedroom is.
  • This intel came at a cost, however. He was spotted by one of the guards who confronted him in Vance’s study.
  • Thinking quick on his feet, Thysbe claimed to be a part of Vance’s personal guard performing an audit of the security in advance of his arrival. This seemed to have worked, meaning during the party there will be at least one guard who believes Thysbe works for Wells directly.
  • After gathering this intel, both Rin and Thysbe left before arousing further suspicion, noting additional exit points on their way out.
