Session 11

  • The party continues, they eventually discover an altar, which is ruined and fairly overgrown. Atop the altar there is a slot for a gemstone or other small object. Upon the altars stone base there are many mysterious infernal markings in red. The faintest of dissonant whispers begin to occupy the minds of the party.
  • Upon touching the altar, two skeletal minotaurs rise from the ground besides the base of the altar.
  • Once the minotaurs fall, their infernal essence begins whisking away from the illuminated bones and into the altar causing it to partially open
  • Pushing their way inside, the staircase down into the crypt eventually leads to an open chamber with a pedestal at the end of it.
  • On this pedestal is the tome they are looking for! However, removing the tome from the pedestal triggered a trap, and the undead littered throughout the crypt's floor suddenly awaken.
  • Overwhelmed, the party flees and is barely able to escape the partially opened dungeon door, closing the undead in after escaping.
