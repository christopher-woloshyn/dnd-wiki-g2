Session 19 - New Year's Gala Part 1 (2026-08-12)

  • The Gala Begins! Everyone is able to get into the party with their pre-planned disguises:
    • Rin uses Glamour to change her appearance to Leon Montreux, the person who's invitation Rin has.
    • XIV is Leon's +1, disguised as Victor, a minor noble from Leon's childhood.
    • Beag is disguised as Himself, as a "Beag Impersonator". The guards were expecting this, as the party apparently hired someone named "Percy" to do this job.
    • Thysbe disguised himself as someone delivering the flower arrangements established from their prior espionage.
  • Just before arrival, Thysbe placed XIV's sword atop a chimney for a potential future recall.
  • After entering, Thysbe was unloading flowers into the butler's pantry and the others had the opportunity to explore the first floor and mingle during the cocktail hour.
  • In the great room, Lady Erika Vance was complaining about the flower centerpiece debacle. She complains that the maid she had asked to do it (Rin in disguise), didn't do it and then (the real main) pretended not to know she was asked to do it
  • Erika remarks that she had to "make an example" of her, continuing to loathe the servant class for being stupid and lazy.
  • Lastly, she mentions that the flower arrangements were taken care of at the last minute by someone named Ms. Vivian.
  • In the dining room there are several spreads of hor'dourves and drinks. Spotted in the back corner is Lady Seraphina from Caspian's tea party in the Valmor's parlor.
  • Suspiciously, she is approached by another guest who hands her a Rose Moscato and calls her "Ms. Vivian".
  • They both walk to the library to continue their discussion and they are trailed by Rin who listens in on their conversation.
  • The man introduces himself as "Rohann the Brilliant", a holy warden of the "Sentinels of Light" which he describes as a faction that mirrors the Wings of Igniavis.
  • In the background, one room over from the library in the blue room, Rin's Auntie Rowena sits in a chair, uninterested in what the man talking at her has to say.
  • Meanwhile Beag is busy making his friendly presence known in the great room and XIV introduces himself to Lady Erika establishing a friendly presence. Lady Erika tell "Victor" that he can convene with the other men in the game room.
  • XIV eventually makes his way to the Game room where he joins a debate between High Magistrate Kenn Durante and another Noble:
Kenn: "If the rumors about the Pyrian counter-offensive to steal back food are true we shouldn't even even bother diverting resources at this point. Prince Aric is secure. He just needs to wait in his cage until the promise day. Since it's so close anyway, whether our peasants live or die won't matter in due time."
Other Noble: "Kenn, that line of resoning is rather shallow. For example, how can you be certain the rebirth implicates a resurrection? What if, say, rebirth were only to usher an enlightenment for the living?"
Kenn: "Then their sacrifice for our victory would not be in vein, would it? You must realize, the peasant class need something to rally themselves behind, lest they be aimless and lost. The fallen will be proud knowing they played their part in our kingdom's eternal salvation. I know that Commander, er sorry, "General" Wells would agree with me on this matter." (This is said in a somewhat envious tone).
Other Noble: "I'll concede to that General Wells' vision was certainly very risk-averse and assurant of our victory, but rather expensive, too. Unsustainably expensive, even."
Kenn: "Don't be naive; in due time, sire, merely one year, currency won't matter anymore; enlightenment will be ours."
  • Kenn asks for XIV's thoughts since he just joined the conversation, and he says something that appeals to Kenn Durante's position, gaining a positive acknowledgement from the man.
  • They then engage in a game of billiards which XIV is able to win comfortably.
  • After helping the servants gather and prepare the flower centerpieces, he is encouraged to walk the halls to check out the party. The servants encourage him to see the art gallery before he departs, and ensure him that he is more than well enough dressed to blend in.
  • In the gallery, there are 4 grandiose paintings displayed across the wall depicting Resurgum's history chronologically:
    • The First Sunrise - H. Araki (614 M.R.): The First King offers his body as a vessel to Igniavis Regum, bathing the world in light.
    • Great King's Sacrifice - M. Angelo (812 M.R.): King Caladrius Sacrifices himself to end The Black Rot and save the world.
    • Treason of Attine - G. G. Akutami (806 M.R.): Grand Justiciar Charles LeKirk Vance* righteously accuses Attine Thalos of treason for the corruption of Prince Valarion and for conspiring with Ordinus to betray the Phoenix King. Charles LeKirk Vance is the honorable Jeremiah Dimitrius Vances' Great Great Great Grandfather.
    • Cravenness of 802 - T. Cole (803 M.R.): Prince Valarion Shamefully retreats to the west with Attine Thalos and their followers.
  • Just as everyone was getting comfortable establishing their presence at the gala, Rin gets her eyes covered from behind and is prompted with a woman's voice: "Guess Who?"
  • When Rin is unable to answer, the woman turns her around and says: "Lee Lee you said you weren't coming! Was this your cute little way of surprising me?"
  • Rin is able to resist the urge to push her down the stairs, but this woman is clearly very fond of Leon, and will likely be difficult to get space from for the rest of the night.
  • She learns her name is Lady Isabelle Hazel by having her introduce herself to XIV.
  • At this time, the Host Guard announces the dining tables have been prepared and everyone can begin going upstairs to their tables.
  • Seated at table 6 are Rin, XIV, Thysbe, Beag, Isabelle, and "Ms. Vivian"/"Lady Seraphina".
  • While settling into their seats, Rin accuses Ms. Vivian of overstepping her boundaries by trying to provide flowers for the party, to which she responds that it was the least she could do given the maid tasked with providing the flowers was incapable of completing the task.
  • On the balcony above the ball room emerges the owner of the Sun-Crest Chateau and the host of the party: High Archon Jeremiah Dimitrius Vance.
  • At the clink of his champagne glass the room quiets as he gives a short speech exclaiming everyone is gathered to celebrate the new year and their victories over Pyralis, effectively declaring victory assuming they can last only one year hunkered down.
  • Following his speech he introduces the guest of honor:
Ladies and Gentlemen, I would now like to introduce our esteemed guest of honor. It is because of him that we can celebrate our glorious victories tonight. Everyone, General Nathaniel Wells.

*Beat Drops*

Standing above you, poised on the balcony he looks down. Smiling. Waving.
The man you've been after all this time. The man behind so much pain and suffering. He's the one that attacked you; he's the one sacrificed you as a martyr; he's the reason Pyralis starves; he's responsible for your mother's death.
