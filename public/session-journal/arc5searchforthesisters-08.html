Special - Ethiul's Final Acension

Part 1: The Blindfold and Seeking the Spark

  • Ethiul has donned the blindfold. He is already extremely perceptive but it still has taken some time to get use to. He begins to interpret the familiar sounds of nature and day to day living differently, feeling the rustle of leaves and bushes, the wind as it brushes the snow, and the scents of a bustling town.
  • As he continues caring for the egg, Ethiul also heightens his senses around touch, feeling the pulsing ice-cold energy. But he feels stuck not being with his comrades; there is a sense of growing boredom from just caring for the egg, Ethiul convinces Soren to join him on an excursion into the Frostspire Glaciers to practice his magic.
  • Ethiul attempts to cast spells, but his magic feels fleeting in its usual ways. Additionally, with his blindfold, he is forced to rely on his heightened other senses. He perceives the immense, raw magical energy permeating the glacier. He feels its essence – a thrumming vibration, a faint, metallic taste in the air, an intense cold that carries strange, almost resonant frequencies.
  • Through this unique perception, Ethiul senses a concentrated point of this chaotic magic – a "Glacial Spark" – a small, pulsing nexus of raw, untamed arcane energy deep within the ice. It's too strong and wild for him to directly control, but its presence is undeniable. He intuitively knows this is a source of immense power.
  • Ethiul and Soren return, perhaps disheartened by his magical performance but invigorated by the new discovery. That night, Ethiul has a vivid dream. His sister, Seraphina, appears, still blindfolded.
"Brother, it seems you are having trouble channeling the stars. The moon's light, in its fullness, can obscure the true brilliance of the cosmos. Return to the Glacial Spark during the new moon. When the celestial veil is thinnest and the stars blaze brightest. Go alone, for this is a communion of your spirit with the universe."
  • She implies that the waxing gibbous moon (which will occur during his waiting period) is a time of growth and increasing power, preparing him for the climax of the new moon.
  • Ethiul intensifies his research into the lost rituals and accelerated growth. Now, he has a new piece of the puzzle: the Glacial Spark and the New Moon's influence.
  • He theorizes that he can combine the raw power of the Glacial Spark with the stellar channeling capabilities of the new moon to perform a modified version of the magical aging rituals he's been researching. This ritual would not just hatch the egg, but significantly accelerate its early growth, possibly granting it unique properties from the cosmic channeling.
  • During the weeks leading up to the new moon, Ethiul meticulously plans the ritual, practicing the specific somatic components blindfolded, deepening his connection to his new senses. Soren supports him throughout this period, helping him gather rare components necessary for the ritual in the wild.

Part 2: The New Moon Ritual and Ascension

  • On the night of the new moon, Ethiul, accompanied by the egg, journeys alone back to the Glacial Spark. The stars are incredibly vivid without the moon's light. Ethiul positions the egg at the heart of the Glacial Spark. He begins the complex ritual, channeling the raw energy of the spark and the cosmic power of the blazing stars directly into the egg.
  • As he reaches the climax, he intuitively unleashes a focused Call Lightning spell. However, instead of thunder and raw electricity, the sky above him shimmers with concentrated starlight, coalescing into a beam of pure cosmic energy that descends onto the egg. This isn't just a spell; it's a conduit for the universe's will.
  • The egg glows intensely, then cracks. A young white dragon emerges, but it's not merely a "Young White Dragon." Its scales shimmer with faint constellations, its eyes hold an unusual depth, and it seems to radiate a serene, almost celestial cold. This dragon is rapidly aging under the ritual's influence, growing larger and more capable before Ethiul's eyes. It now carries a unique blend of glacial essence and cosmic energy; the white scales have tinted with a faint cosmic purple/pink, each with unique bright spots that faintly twinkle like the night sky.
  • As the ritual concludes and the dragon solidifies in its accelerated state, Ethiul feels an undeniable pull – not physical, but spiritual. A shimmering portal, like a window to the cosmos, opens before him.

Part 3: Trials of the Council

  • Seraphina appears again, this time actually in the flesh:
"You have woven a new destiny, brother. Now, prove your understanding of balance." She takes Ethiul and the newly awakened, cosmic dragon into the Astral Plane.
  • Ethiul and Thurial appear before the Council of Cassiopeia. His final trial, the Trial of Truth begins:
  • Test of Intent (The Fool's Reckoning):
"Ethiul, you were once called 'The Fool' by another who observed countless timelines. Your sight was short, your actions often impulsive. How do you, as a Cosmic Vanguard, ensure your grand designs consider the far-reaching echoes of your choices, particularly when they involve altering the natural order, as you have with this creature?"
  • Test of Connection (Seraphina's Reflection):
"Your path was once solitary, detached even from those who cared for you. Your sister observed this. This creature you have nurtured, it is bound to you. How will you balance the deep personal bonds you forge, and the love you feel, with the impartiality and cosmic duty required of a Vanguard, especially when those bonds might clash with the greater good?"
  • Test of Destiny (The Wyrm's Path):
"This Star-Wyrm, born of ice and tempered by starlight, holds immense potential for both creation and destruction. While nurtured by your hand, it still carries ancestral shadows. What assurances can you offer that its power will be wielded for harmony? What future do you envision for it, independent of your immediate guidance, and what sacrifices are you willing to make to ensure it walks a righteous path, should it stray?"
  • The counsel sends Ethiul and Thurial back to the material plane to continue their training together. When he returns, his senses are expanded from 10ft to 30ft.
