Transcendent Voroth Exposition

  • Since he is the one that uttered the wish spell, he was pulled from the timeline before it was erased from existence. Let's call the original timeline "The Transcendent Timeline"
  • He existed stuck in a liminal space for what felt like multiple lifetimes, forced to watch through the eyes of thousands of Voroths and their lives in their alternate timelines.
  • He cultivated the knowledge, wisdom, and experience of thousands of Voroths over time, experiencing each of their lifetimes in full, one after the other.
  • Eventually, one Voroth, which we'll call Voroth Prime, obtained the very same skull key that Songbeard gave Transcendent Voroth. This caused Transcendent Voroth to be thrust from his liminal prison into "Voroth's Dimension": A sub-dimention of Mecanus, the outer plane of order.
  • As of now, there have been 4 timelines where a Voroth has obtained Songbeard's skull key:
    • Voroth Prime (A)
    • Voroth the Late (D)
    • Voroth Sythstorm (Evoker Wizard, Member of the Groove Crusaders. Killed songbeard to get the key)
    • Voroth (PC Voroth)
  • In Voroth's Temporal Demiplane, he claims to be the keeper of timelines responsible for maintaining the order and balance of time in the multiverse. THIS IS A LIE.
  • In reality, Voroth's Dimension is a prison realm he is held in, because the wish granted by Nila Aashni distrupted the balance of the multiverse's timelines. Voroth essentially was the scapegoat for requesting the wish and was punished for it.
  • Voroth Prime refused and escaped.
  • Voroth the Late accepted and was killed because he wasn't strong enough for the transition ritual.
  • Voroth Synthstorm is another Voroth that is also interested. He is from a timeline where the party are the members in a band called the "Groove Crusaders" and this dimension is perpetually the 1980s.
  • He will try to convince Voroth he needs a successor, and wants PC Voroth to be that successor.
  • Only one alternate Voroth with the key can exist in order to perform the ritual.
  • "Are you willing to fight to be the true successor?"
  • He casts a spell which transports everyone to a temporal arena.
  • Here, Voroth Synthstorm is introduced.
