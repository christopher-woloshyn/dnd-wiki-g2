Session 75 - The Grand Auction Part 5

  • The next morning, the party had breakfast and made a plan for the Music showcase.
  • They decided that it would probably be best not to bid on the piece and sour relationship and proposition from Lyra.
  • The Music showcase was preceded by a performance from the legendary bard, musician, and performer Isadora Underhill, who has an item for sale featured in the showcase.
  • Voroth seemed very drawn to this performance.
  • Tassarion recognized that name as the woman who performed in his village the day it was attacked.
  • Music Showcase (Est. 25 - 100)
    • Notorious B.A.R.D.'s Viola Bow Signed (32)
    • Bagpipes of Stealth (15)
    • Godrick's Harpischord (23)
    • Isadora's Lyre (37)
    • JSB Movement 4 - Symphony of Freedom - PURCHASED (280)
  • Lyra seemed annoyed that the party outbid her on the item.
  • Following the music showcase, Tassarion bribed a couple of ushers to learn that Isadora was staying at the Gatekeeper's Inn in town away from the auction showcase.
  • That night, the party went to the tavern to see her performing for the locals.
  • Morgan used his Medallion of Mimickery to harmonize with her and get her attention for a conversation.
  • Voroth was excited that he got to listen to more music; the music seemed calming and therapeutic for him.
  • Later that night she was questioned by Morgan and Tassarion. She is a very adept liar with a lot of charm and Charisma, but Morgan sensed something was off (nat 20).
  • This prompted Tassarion to grab her and attempt to kill her in one shot, but after the initial attack she teleported away with her body guards.
  • The only thing that remained was a note reading:
Come find me if you *actually* want to talk.

- Isadora <3
  • The party was promptly kicked out of the tavern following this altercation, and they went back to the hotel, with Voroth excited that he was able to get an autograph from Isadora.
  • LEVEL UP (14)
