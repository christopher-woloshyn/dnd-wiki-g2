Arc 2 - Vignettes of Truth: XIV

The training grounds just outside of Cinderheart are a churned field of mud and splintered wood. XIV, you are executing a brutal, repetitive martial kata throwing your blade and magically recalling it. Your muscles burn, your breathing is measured, and you are covered in a cold, gray muck.

The rhythmic sounds of your training is broken by the heavy thud of approaching hooves. A polished carriage, escorted by a vanguard of fully armored Cavalry of Paladins of Ordinus, halts at the edge of the field. A figure steps out, draped in an lush, heavy velvet cloak. He is young. No older than 19 or 20.

XIV: "You brought an entire cavalry detachment just to cross the city? Afraid of the refugees, or just trying to look important?"

The young noble carefully steps to avoid the worst of the mud, a wry smile on his face: "A Lord requires his escort, you should know that. It seems a shame you did learned all the proper manners just to to wallow in the mud like a common soldier."

XIV pauses, resting his great sword against his shoulder: "The mud right now is serving its purpose... What is yours today?"

The young noble's smile fades. He pulls a sealed ledger from his cloak: "I’ve concluded the internal investigation. The King, the Prince, the entire council... I tore through their records, their guards, their finances. I even subjected the Chamberlain, the Justiciar, and Cassian Fawkes to the Ordinal Truth Tonic."

XIV: "And? Who's responsible?"

Noble: "No one is responsible, and the tonic is absolute. It strips away every mental block, every falsehood, every illusion. Under its effects, they swore they knew nothing. Treason is out of the question."

XIV: "Resurgum knew exactly where father would be on that day. They knew exactly what he was plotting, and they knew exactly how to stop it. If it wasn't treason, what was it?"

Noble: "Theft. The information must have been stolen somehow. . or perhaps obtained from someone without them even knowing... Regardless, the only people with the operational capacity to act on that intelligence are in the Resurgum high command. Since you intend on finding this 'Wells' fellow to support The Final Mission, he may also know more about where this leaked information came from. Please find out, for me; I'll make them pay."

XIV: "Ad lib response, saying something about getting Wells"

Noble: "Thank you, brother."

XIV reaches out to shake his hand, then chuckles and withdraws it. "In our time apart I must've forgotten your hatred for anything filthy."

The young noble chuckles, smiles, and turns to step back toward his carriage and then pauses.

"Before I depart... The Paladins you sent captured someone from the strike team at the bridge. They’ve been interrogating for days, but the woman's a fanatic. She won't break. Shall I have the guards prepare her... for a conversation with you?"

XIV Turning back to the shattered training dummy, his voice dead and cold: "make sure she hasn't slept in a few days, and make sure she's hungry, too."

The young noble nods: "May Ordinus guide your blade."

He enters the carriage. The cavalry wheels around, riding back toward the capital, leaving XIV alone in the mud to prepare for the violence ahead.
