Session 42 - The Cosmic Vangaurds Call Part 3

  • The party is leaning towards calling themselves the "Rhythmic Rizzlers"
  • The party makes their way back to Bidrian's Biddings. Ethiul asks him for more information regarding the Robe of Constellations
  • Jobidrian says he happens to have just the right books; many years ago, he helped someone much like Ethiul, and probably from the same grove, in their quest to become a Cosmic Vanguard. He asks if Ethiul or Fen knows her, but they don't.
  • He has two books about it, one about the Robe of Constellations, and the other about the Upper Planes. From what he has read, he thinks the Robe presents itself to those who have been touched by the cosmic energy of the upper planes.
  • Read the passages from the books.
  • Jobidrian says he has to close the shop for the night, and they should come back tomorrow to continue the discussion.
  • That night, Ethiul is flooded with two visions, one from his original backstory in the city where the rift of inferno opened, and the second is a vision of Voroth declaring a wish to a biblically accurate angel.
  • Then everyone (except Morgan) appears in the liminal dreamscape again. The voice asks Voroth "Did you see that?" but Voroth doesn't get the vision.
  • Addressing everyone the voice says "If Voroth didn't get the vision, that's okay. I know now that Ethiul and Voroth's goals are aligned: You must return to Melody at once."

Books from Jobidrian

###### Celestial Weavings: Chronicles of the Star Druids

By Allan Timberborne, Professor at Coastgate University School of Anthropology

In the sacred annals of the Star Druids, there exists a celestial tapestry woven with threads of cosmic energy. Among its most revered artifacts is the Robe of Constellations, a garment that transcends the boundaries between the mortal realm and the astral expanse. One who seeks the robe simply won't find it; instead it seeks those with a profound connection to the realms. To don this robe is to become a steward of the celestial balance, chosen by the very stars that adorn its dark fabric.

It's not a quest of physical pursuit but a journey of self-discovery and alignment with the celestial forces. Only those who have been touched by the Upper Planes will the robe reveal itself to. To seek it is to embark on a journey of the soul, guided by the very stars that illuminate the night sky. May those who aspire to don this robe find their true celestial calling and become the stewards of the cosmic balance.

###### Cosmic Harmonies: A Star Druid's Guide to the Upper Planes

As transcribed by Aeliana Moonwhisper, Cosmic Vangaurd of the Starlight Grove

In the vast expanse of the multiverse, the Upper Planes stand as beacons of cosmic purpose, embodying the very essence of alignment and divine influence. Scholars and seekers alike delve into the annals of these celestial realms, unraveling the mysteries that shape the very fabric of existence.

Celestia, the celestial bastion, mirrors the ideals we, as Star Druids, hold dear. A canvas of cosmic energy, resembling the vastness of the night sky, it hosts the celestial descendants of radiance and righteousness. Here, selflessness, charity, and the alleviation of suffering reign supreme. True celestial beings, akin to [biblically accurate] angels, call Celestia home. As a Druid of the Stars, one may find solace and guidance in the benevolent light of Celestia, where wishes are granted to those deemed worthy of spreading righteousness across the planes.

Contrasting the celestial brilliance is the fiery Abyss of Inferno, a realm ruled by demons and devoted to the pursuit of suffering. Forsaken souls endure eternal torment in this nightmarish hell-scape. Demons, driven by a malevolent purpose, seek the destruction of the material world. As we traverse the realms, beware the chaotic energies of Inferno, for it embodies the very essence of evil, craving the maximization of suffering upon the material plane.

Mechanus, the plane of order and structure, stands as an intricate tapestry woven with rules, laws, and process. Here, denizens prioritize strict organization, understanding that without structure, they are nothing. Mechanus manifests itself as lawful, embracing principles of logic, order, and methodical process. As Star Druids, we can glean insights into the balance of nature by observing the structured harmony within the Loom of Order.

In stark contrast to the structured order of Mechanus lies Limbo, a swirling vortex of chaos. Here, elements collide in a never-ending dance of unpredictability. Rocks, trees, and entire landscapes intermingle amidst strong winds and pockets of ever-changing matter. As a manifestation of chaos, Limbo celebrates disorder, randomness, and entropy. Star Druids traversing this realm may find themselves challenged by the unbridled energies that embody the chaotic echoes of existence.

In our cosmic journey, let us recognize the significance of these Upper Planes – celestial realms that shape the very fabric of our reality. May the stars guide us as we navigate these diverse dimensions, embracing the cosmic harmonies that connect us to the vastness of the multiverse.
