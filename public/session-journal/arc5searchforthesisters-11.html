Session 80 - Transcendent Showdown Part 2

  • The party next found themselves in an unfamiliar forest outside a cozy looking cabin. Inside, they met a version of Voroth called "Voroth the Alchemist". He asked them to find "lavender and Blueberry" in the forest.
  • Ethiul was able to use their improved perception to find lavender, but no one could find blueberries.
  • They did, however, spot a puppy in the forest which was very suspicious to everyone. Voroth cast the spell Tongues on the puppy so that it could understand common and they could communicate with it.
  • It appeared to be friendly, and the Puppy's name tag revealed to be "Blueberry."
  • Upon returning to the cabin, Voroth the Alchemist used the lavender to finish a puppy meal for his dog Blueberry.
  • An elderly woman, Voroth the Alchemists wife, came into the main room and offered the party pie, which most of them ate before the scene ended.
  • The party then appeared in an alleyway with a sociopathic Voroth dressed as a butcher. He presented them with two bound figures: a young boy and a powerful mage.
  • He forced the party to choose one to save: the boy or the woman, with the other being killed.
  • The party chose to save the young boy. Voroth promptly executed the woman, causing caused the scene to shift again.
  • In the next scene, the party appeared in on a stage, greeted by a new Voroth: "Voroth Vinny Vegas". He is the host of a game show about the campaign's past arcs/events. The party successfully answered all the questions, causing the scene to shift and avoiding more damage.
1. "For 100 points: This is the dimension of order, where rules are followed to a T and everything sits within a strict hierarchy!"** (Answer: What is Mechanus)
2. "For 200 points: This is the town where Voroth and Tassarion first met?" (Answer: Longdale)
3. "For 300 points: The name of the nautical musical that is extremely reminiscent of Morgan's Backstory?" (Answer: What is Gorman's Tragedy?)
4. "For the grand prize, what is going to happen next if you fail to imprison the great one: TTTTTRAAANSCENDENT VOOOOOROTHHH?" (Open ended answer - allow player creativity, perhaps hinting at madness, the multiverse unraveling, or Transcendent Voroth consuming PC Voroth's mind entirely).
  • The next scene placed the party in the ruins of a palace where they faced a conqueror-like Voroth: "Voroth the Conqueror" who espoused a philosophy of Social Darwinism.
  • Morgan and Ethiul engaged this Voroth in a philosophical debate, verbally decimating Vorath's worldview.
  • Voroth, enraged after being called "bald," rage quit and sent them to the final area.
  • The party entered the final area. Transcendent Vorath's voice tempted towards Selene to betray Voroth, but she immediately refused.
  • The party fought shadow versions of Voroth while Selene prepared the imprisonment ritual with the Soulshackle Skull.
  • Transcendent Voroth eventually appeared, knocking Tassarion into a sleep and approaching Selene and Voroth.
  • Right around when Selene finished the ritual (thanks to some stalling from Morgan), Transcendent Voroth cast his "domain expansion" capturing only Voroth.
  • The two Voroths shared a dialogue recalling days earlier an interaction they had in Raeneros near Melody's Grave site.
  • This vision transferred to the entire party as well and showed Voroth's own weakness where Voroth declared his realization; he realized he wasn't strong enough, but that his purpose was to empower the others he's traveled with to complete the job.
PC Voroth: "Voroth, back at the grave sight you showed me something."

*Cut back to when PC Voroth left to visit the other grave site. He looks down at a patch of grass where the grave should be. A gentle breeze whispers through the grass.*

PC Voroth: "Not here either, huh..."

T Voroth: "You don't know him. He never existed here. You'll only ever have the privilege of knowing him through *my* memories."

PC Voroth: "Even still, we loved him like our own brother."

*The wind stills. T Voroth physically manifests, a shimmering, malevolent counterpart, standing near the grave.*

T Voroth: "A want to show you something..." (Starts playing Mozart Requiem in D-minor)

*PC Voroth kneels, closes his eyes. The scene shifts instantly to a bustling market street in Raenaros City. A magician, resembling another version of Voroth stands on the street performing an act for a crowd. There are children surrounding him which show visual excitement for the tricks he is performing, witch the children resembling many of the other characters.

PC Voroth: "Voroth, I know better than anyone else that you aren't inherently evil. So why? Why play this game with my mind? What do you truly want?"

T Voroth: "I want you to know that you are insignificant. One of a million others. A data point... Only I am eternal. 

*The scene suddenly shifts to a nightscape. The houses from the town sit ablaze; bodies strewn about the plaza in pools of blood; the very children admiring Voroth's act, pierced with swords and arrows, burned, and massacred. Voroth slumps over them crying.*

T Voroth: "I want you to know that you are weak."

*T Voroth snaps his fingers causing the scene to shift again instantly. Classical music is playing as we see Morgan on his knees bound by chains. He faces up to a vengeful looking woman with glowing purple eyes. Ethiul, Selene, and Cleff lay dead, their bodies strewn beside Morgan. The two observe another Voroth, standing behind Morgan attempting to interrupt the ritual with a spell, but the magic from his wand crackles and fades; he is not strong enough to intervene. The woman draws a sword as she slowly approaches Morgan, beheading him in one sinister slash. His headless body collapses, and a flash of light erupts upon the completion of a ritual. A dark figure emerges beside the woman, and another man holding a baton joins her; they both kneel before it as the Voroth fades out of consciousness and the scene fades to black.*

T Voroth: "I want you to know that you need me. No matter how hard you try, you will never be strong enough to protect anyone."

*PC Voroth sits in silence and awe for a few minutes, and then suddenly begins smirking and chuckling upon his realization. Cut back to the battle.*

PC Voroth: " Every Voroth I've met, every memory I've shared... I've seen the awful things we've gone through. I've also seen the journeys we've been on. I've seen the people we've met, the things we've learned.

*PC Voroth draws a dagger dowsed with the poison Erin handed him.*

PC Voroth: I realized that our collective experience makes *you* who *you* are... Without us, you'd be nothing..."

T Voroth (Enraged): "Silence! I know what you're trying to do! The *only* way you can help your allies is with my power."

PC Voroth: " I also realized that you're right. I'm not strong enough, and I never will be. I can't protect them. But you assumed that I'm the one that's supposed to help. Other incredible mages exist, Voroth. So no, I don't need your power. Selene, take it from here.

T Voroth: "NOO"

*PC Voroth stabs himself with the dagger causing an eruption of energy, screeching with the sounds of  the souls that occupy Voroth's mind. They all flow into Selene as T Voroth Wretches in pain. Selene feels a surge of power and knowledge through her veins as everything warms up. You feel like you have the power to overcome. 

Selene: DOMAIN EXPANSION

  • The party attacked the weakened Transcendent Voroth, and Selene cast him into the imprisonment skull, completing the ritual.
