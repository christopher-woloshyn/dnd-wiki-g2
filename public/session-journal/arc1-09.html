Session 9 - Tempest and Trauma (2026-04-15)

  • Loremaster Amram took Thysbe aside and said he is very proud of him. He is happy that Thysbe took his advice by not letting the difference of his wings inhibit his devotion from serving the Phoenix King. Thysbe did not tell him about the nature of his wings.
  • Everyone is introduced to Captain Bramwell, Helmsman Silas, and Quartermaster Finn: A crew for a small messenger vessel. Together they travel to the city docks and depart of Crescent Isle.
    • Beag has seen small river boats before, but this one was big just like the city.
    • Thysbe hasn't been on a boat before, and he gets a bit seasick from the rocking and occasionally floats above the deck with his wings.
    • XIV has heard stories of boats before, but finally being on one made him feel like it was romanticized more in the stories.
    • Rin feels a much quieter presence; one she has not felt in a long time.
  • While on the ship, Captain Bramwell explains that the refugees coming in has made rationing tough on the soldiers too; he thanks everyone for their efforts to save Valmor because there mission has given them extra food and resources.
  • He then takes out a few bottles of Rum, one of which is very high quality. He pours a few tankards for everyone on deck, gives one of the cheap bottles to Beag, and proposes a toast to the Millennium Rebirth, declaring that the promise day will usher in utopia and put an end to suffering.
  • XIV, feeling he is finally able to let his guard down for the first time since entering the battlefield, drinks more than he ever has before. He is properly drunk.
  • Rin goes below deck for a moment, and while gone XIV challenges Beag and Thysbe to a drinking competition. Beag accepts and Thysbe declines but agrees to judge. Intrigued by this, Rin also accepts the challenge.
  • Some time passes as they continue to go through the whole extra bottle. XIV spills some on his shirt and takes it off revealing a large canvas of scars across his body. He says they were necessary to ensure he was ready.
  • He then asks Rin if she could teach him how to talk to a woman. Beag also offers advice on this before XIV passes out.
  • When unconscious, Rin performs a weakened version of her commune on his unconscious body which invokes the vision of one of XIV's Memories.
  • After all this, Beag goes to pick him up and help him downstairs. The moment he's touched XIV jolts away in a rush of adrenaline, as if he just experienced that trauma collectively in an instant.
  • He aggressively yells at Rin asking "what the fuck did you do to my head?!". She denies that she did anything but Beag and Thysbe know she did. He tells her that was not okay, and to never do that again. If she wanted to know about his past she should have just asked.
  • After the adrenaline wears off, XIV drunkenly passes back out again on the deck while everyone else sleeps downstairs.
  • The next morning, a small squall moved around the ship. When Thysbe tried flying above the clouds to investigate he was attacked by some mysterious giant birds.
  • After freezing its wings, a much larger magical thunderbird descended from the sky and attacked the ship. In the chaos of battle, both the Helmsman and the Quartermaster were killed.
  • After the battle, the Captain was unnervingly quite, exclaiming:
"I’ve sailed all across the southern gulf for over twenty years. You learn the tides. You learn the storms. You learn the beasts. But that... I've never seen anything like that. That wasn't just a beast. It was pure, wild fury... like the sky and sea itself was trying to tear us apart. A monster. The Priests have long preached the intervention of a Devil trying to steal the Millenium Rebirth. But tell me, what Devil manifests and commands the winds and the sea like the God's themself?"
  • Due to the loss of crew, it was more difficult navigating to the crescent isle, but they were able to arrive in time.

XIV's Memory

Your eyes open to dim streaks of light leaking through an old, run down barn. You are shackeled by your arms and legs upright as you hear the rumble of the door open.

"Sifu?... Is that you?..."

Without speaking, a tall figure walks past you and approaches a table stocked with various tools. The person picks up a whip and begins cracking. *crack* *crack*

You screech out in pain, crying desparately. Some scars are already adorned on your body, others are forming or actively healing from recent similar session. These will be just one of many scars you will grow familiar with throughout the training.

"I will only stop when your fortitude hardens!" *crack* *crack*

Slowly, over the course of minutes, your screams slowly grow accustomed to the torture. Your face is empty, devoid of emotion, and eventually, you feels the straps around your arms and legs loosen as you crumble to the floor clutching at the dirt.

The man next to you picks you up and say's "Son, that's enough for today, you might not be there yet, but you will some day. I know you will be stronger than I ever was."

The scene transitions: Your position on the barn floor crossfades into the same position XIV currently lays in while he's passed out on the ship.
