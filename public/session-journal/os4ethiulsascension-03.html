Part 3: Trials of the Council

  • Seraphina appears again, this time actually in the flesh:
"You have woven a new destiny, brother. Now, prove your understanding of balance." She takes Ethiul and the newly awakened, cosmic dragon into the Astral Plane.
  • Ethiul and Thurial appear before the Council of Cassiopeia. His final trial, the Trial of Truth begins:
  • Test of Intent (The Fool's Reckoning):
"Ethiul, you were once called 'The Fool' by another who observed countless timelines. Your sight was short, your actions often impulsive. How do you, as a Cosmic Vanguard, ensure your grand designs consider the far-reaching echoes of your choices, particularly when they involve altering the natural order, as you have with this creature?"
  • Test of Connection (Seraphina's Reflection):
"Your path was once solitary, detached even from those who cared for you. Your sister observed this. This creature you have nurtured, it is bound to you. How will you balance the deep personal bonds you forge, and the love you feel, with the impartiality and cosmic duty required of a Vanguard, especially when those bonds might clash with the greater good?"
  • Test of Destiny (The Wyrm's Path):
"This Star-Wyrm, born of ice and tempered by starlight, holds immense potential for both creation and destruction. While nurtured by your hand, it still carries ancestral shadows. What assurances can you offer that its power will be wielded for harmony? What future do you envision for it, independent of your immediate guidance, and what sacrifices are you willing to make to ensure it walks a righteous path, should it stray?"
  • The counsel sends Ethiul and Thurial back to the material plane to continue their training together. When he returns, his senses are expanded from 10ft to 30ft.
