Session 60 - Fen's Call Part 2

  • The party meets Ulrich in the tavern the next morning to head to the college for the magic demonstration
  • During the demo, Voroth assesses each of the students. He realizes that while some students have good potential, none of the students are even close to as strong as Ethiul or even Fen, and therefore none of the students are strong enough to be a suitable apprentice to him.
  • Fen does her demo last, and shocks the students and Selene with magic power that is nearly on par with Selene herself.
  • The Students want Selene to do a demonstration herself because they want her to prove that she is stronger than all the students.
  • She casts a massive fireball (at the 6th level) directly at her students. After a massive explosion, the flames dissipate revealing that all the students in the blast are completely unscathed.
  • This demonstration shows Voroth that Selene so much stronger than then rest, and he asks her to become his apprentice.
  • Selene refuses, saying she has a school to run and she can't just uproot her life and abandon everything; she's built a new life for herself that's really successful and doesn't want to give that up.
  • Voroth continues to insist, and eventually this prompts Selene into asking him to demonstrate his magic.
  • Voroth Casts a weakened version of his Domain Expansion that he learned from Transcendent Voroth - a small black orb appears and dissipates, but its magical presence is enough to convince both Selene and Ulrich that Voroth is the real deal.
  • Ulrich tells Selene that the party is powerful enough to help them with an expedition, and Selene tells Voroth and the party that she will consider being his apprentice IF they help them with their next expedition and she herself can find a suitable successor for the school.
  • Back at the tavern, Calen arrives to share a meal with the party as Ulrich explains the research they've been doing.
  • The Frostspire Glaciers are permiated by an unknown, inherently chaotic magic source. They know that Sarsynanth the Ice Queen resides near the source, and that this magic enhances her to be far more powerful than an ordinary dragon. Together, they can venture deeper into the glacier to potentially learn more about the source of the chaotic magic, or to locate Sarsynanth's lair.
