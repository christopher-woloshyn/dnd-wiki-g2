Session 4

  • The party met up with Jobidrian again the next day to ask him for teleportation services. He also informed Fen that he would introduce her to a blacksmith that can combine her Staff of Healing and Moon Sickle.
  • Using the Moon Sickle, Jobidrian is able to use it as a reference to teleport the party back to Melody's island on target.
  • Once everyone reunites with Melody, they tell her about what they found in the Two Piece, and give her the engagement ring back.
  • Then, she tells Morgan a bit more about the Sonnet Swashbucklers. She says that Morgan was made the scapegoat for his sister's death to remove him from the inheritance. But, Songbeard chose him anyway which caused Melody to have to hide; she can't say why.
  • She also reveals that she has a gear piece that is almost identical to the Gear that was in the two piece, but she has no idea why.
  • She learns more about the visions from Ethiul, and the voice's insistence that Melody is the key, so she placed her gear inside the other gear.
  • This causes a rift to open in Melody's hut that pulls everyone inside, transporting them to Mechanus.
