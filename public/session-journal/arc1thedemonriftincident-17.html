Session 20 - Demon Rift Incident Part 16

  • The battle begins. The party faces the first wave of demons and defeats them swiftly, but then everyone is overcome by darkness.
  • Everyone awakens in their own separate shadow dimension confronted by a shadow-y copy of themselves.
  • After each party member defeats their copy, they receive a fragmented green shard.
  • After each victory, that person wakes up on the ground in a cloud of shadows among everyone else. Those who have not defeated their shadow copy have shadow essence covering their eyes and mouth.
  • Party memebers that are out can jump back into some else's shadow dimension and aid them in battle.
  • One the party members have all defeated the battle begins to simmer down.
  • One final wave of enemies approaches, and the party, exhausted, have to give everything to defeat them.
