Session 84 - The Search for Maris Part 1

  • The next morning, the party had breakfast at the inn and discussing their next steps. Tassarion was seated in a booth already, exclaiming he spotted Erin near the Orphanage overlooking a bridge.
  • Erin lamented the complexity of the situation, and is thankful Tassarion didn't harm Justin. She also confides that she's very confused and just needs some time alone to process.
  • Following this, the party decided to track down Isadora to question her more about what happened in Tassarion's village.
  • Selene cast a sending spell, to which Isadora replied, requesting to meet them backstage after a show she was performing in the city of Diyun in two days.
  • The party made a plan to leave the following morning after Selene prepares a teleport spell.
  • In the meantime, they decided to visit the local university to find spells related to scrying to either find or learn more about Maris and her whereabouts.
  • On their way to the university, they encountered several people wearing red gnome hats and drinking. Selene recalls this must be an annual party event called Gnome-con.
  • Morgan approached a couple of the bros and asked for a drink. He was given a magical cup that automatically refilled with a foul-tasting, alcoholic liquid.
  • Selene tried a sip and coughed, prompting one of the bros to mock her for being old and lame.
  • Frustrated, Tassarion cast banishment on the man who was making fun of Selene. He dropped his magical cup, which Morgan then took.
  • The party continued to the University Bookstore, looking to purchase some items. Morgan then proceeded to get extremely drunk from having kept drinking from the cup. Selene, not wanting to deal with this also kept taking some swigs from her waterskin, which had been filled with a cup of the vile liquid as well.
  • Tassarion was able to end Morgan's intoxication by finding a "Pocari Sweat", and the party purchased several healing potions, two scrolls of scrying, and Selene bought a Hat of Wizardry, applying her university affiliated discount at checkout.
  • After shopping, they attended Dr. Mobius Bernoulli's office hours. He explained the mechanics of scrying and what they would need to make the spell successful.
  • Morgan didn't have anything belonging to Maris for the scrying spell, but he told him about the skull keys he had collected from the Sonnet Swashbucklers.
  • Dr. Bernoulli agreed to examine them, noting their magical properties, and said he might be able to find a way to use them to locate other copies or learn about them in general.
  • The party made plans to return to him after their meeting with Isadora, and headed back to the "Good" Gatekeeper's Inn to rest.
