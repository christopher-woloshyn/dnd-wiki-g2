Session 20 - New Year's Gala Part 2 (2026-08-19)

  • Wells gives a rousing speech and proposes a toast:
Look around this room. The glass in your hands, the warmth in these halls, the peace of our lowlands: these are not accidents of fortune. They are the harvest of our absolute vigilance.

For a thousand years, Resurgum has kept the covenant of the First Sunrise. We have guarded the true, unbroken bloodline of Igniavis Regum while heretics huddled in the western crags, prostrate themselves before a cold and ruthless false God of order and tyranny. They believed they could subvert prophecy with theft and murder. They believed striking at the heart of our succession would seed ultimate control to Ordinus.

They were wrong. (Light Applause)

Our counteroffensive has broken their front along the Sruth. We have turned their granaries into ash and forced their armies to choke on their own supply lines. Every step our legions take secures the cradle for Prince Aric, the rightful vessel of Igniavis Regum.

When our enemies squirm, they only reveal their exhaustion. In the Isles of the South, they have forfeited priceless artifacts and holy assets merely to pay their debts. In the mountains to the West, their strongest Holy Warriors sacrificed themselves and shattered their command simply to preserve a crumbling supply line to an overpopulated and restless camp of refugees.

They celebrate these desperate stands as victories, but a dying beast always thrashes hardest before the end. They buy mere hours with blood and ash. We will measure our triumph in eternity! (Light Applause)

There is no cost too high, no sacrifice too severe, and no sentiment that outweighs the divine mandate before us. In this coming year, the Millennium Rebirth will be realized. The sun will rise, the dead will be lifted from the ash into glory, and the fractured kingdom will be forged whole once more under the true flame.

This is a toast to the eve of the old centuries, and to the dawn that follows.
To Resurgum, to the Phoenix King. and to the mission that must not fail.

(Cheers and Thunderous Applause)
  • Following the toast servers immediately fan out on the ballroom floor to begin serving an extremely lavish meal:
    • Appetizer: Valdros Oysters on the half shell with caviar OR Swan foie gras dipped in champagne acid chocolate. (Paired with an in-house Sun-Crest Chateau 12 Year Aged Sauternes).
    • Dinner: Lolipop Lamb with Sylvarian-mint jam served aside au gratin Agathorr potatoes on a black truffle crema (Paired with a local Breachview Manor 10 year Barrel-aged Rum Brunello) OR Line caught sea bass over an autumn harvest Ratatouille Risotto, Iganiavis-broiled with a smoked saffron garnish (Paired with a local Goldfire Estate 10 year aged Lambrusco).
    • Dessert: Lemon tart with a whipped Aviana cloud-berry toasted meringue (Paired with Northern Sruth-Region Centennial Ice Wine)

  • Following the dinner, High Archon Vance announces there will be an intermission while the ballroom is cleared for the ballroom dance and opera show. He invites all business guests to the third room to engage in the contract auction during the intermission, and encourages all other guests to continue to mingle downstairs.
  • XIV gets a fancy bourbon for himself and Kenn Durante, who he smooth talks into getting invited to the contract auction. Kenn, beginning to like "Victor" agrees.

  • The first several contracts are mundane business, logistical, and supply contracts taking up most of the time in the room.
  • The last contract before XIV is asked to leave is a contract hiring several mercenary legions tasked with intercepting and slaughtering Pyrian supply runs to the west attempting to steal winter food.
  • For the last contracts, about half of the group, including XIV, is asked to leave. Rowena remains.
  • XIV is the last one to leave, and he is able to quickly scope out the room, but remain back to eavesdrop on the final contracts.
  • The first secret contract is a bid to compete for supplying smuggling routes to funnel weapons and propaganda to a growing insurrectionist group called "The Immolationists". This contract was won by the Green Pinion Partners.
  • The Immolationists are a growing cult in the city of Hearthward who believe the Millennium Rebirth will end the world in fire, and light the way for a new world to be reborn in the ashes. Resurgum wants to incite a civil uprising behind Pyrian lines.
  • The second secret contract is for exclusive merchant rights to deliver supply shipments to the Temple of Natilux in the capital to aid in the preparation for the Millennium Rebirth. This is the main central temple that's the holiest site in all of Igniavis worship. This contract was won by the Black Quill Trading Co.
  • After listening to these secret contracts through the wall, XIV subtly slipped out and back to the party.

  • In the study she finds "A True History of Resurgum, 4th Edition" text book on his desk and an open letter from an archeologist from the capital:
Expedition Report: Rot-Scarred Valley Search Region 
Patron: High Archon Jeremiah Dimitrius Vance
Status: Complete, No Artifacts Recovered

We have finished our excavation of the valley. Many historians have speculated this is the region where King Caladrius perished after ending the Black Rot in 801 M.R.

Our mandate was to locate his remains and return them to the capital for a proper cremation, ensuring his soul can ascend during the Millennium Rebirth. However, the site was completely barren. We found absolutely nothing. There is no tomb, no sarcophagus, and no body.

We also failed to locate the Tablets of Caladrius, which the priesthood claims contain his final prayers to the truest followers of Igniavis Regum. Instead, the deepest cavern yielded only physical anomalies: deep gauges carved into the stone and strange vines that appeared to shift positions between search days.

I am requesting authorization to expand our search grid into the neighboring mountains. We must find his remains and the tablets before the Promise Day.

Chief Historian Marcey Jones

I acknowledge that Pyralis will always remain a threat to the Millennium Rebirth. However, we need only hold our ground for one year. I fear turning to more extreme measures will be a tactical error.

I am writing to formally object to invoking "The Moth" again. My doubts lie not in his skill, he could undoubtedly reach Valerion and his son at our word, rather they lie in his volatililty.

He possesses no loyalty to our cause. He is driven solely by the challenge of killing. If we point him toward the Pyrian crown, I fear the thrill will drive him towards our own. We cannot control someone like that, so why even risk it?

High Magistrate Kenn Durante

By the Light of Igniavis,
