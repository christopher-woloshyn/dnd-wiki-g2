Session 14 - Teatime and Tactics (2026-06-24)

  • Following the interrogation, the party receives a summons from General Valmor to his Manor to discuss a plan of great importance. The courier then gives an additional letter to Rin from Caspian Valmor:
Dearest Rinelle,

I trust the city air hasn't decayed your spirits? I’ve heard whispers that your divine bosom has graciously returned to the capital. It has been far too long since I’ve enjoyed such supple company.

I am hosting a small gathering for some tea at the manor this afternoon. I have also invited my new maiden to the occasion. She is a southern noble of quite exquisite taste and stature. To be quite frank, she is much prettier than you, compliments my intellect endlessly, and boasts significantly larger breasts.

Perhaps if you attend, I will graciously reconsider how poorly you treated me during our last encounter, and all three of us can spend some quality time together in the boudoir.

Ever yours,

Caspian Valmor
  • Upon arriving at the estate with the party, Caspian uses the occasion to stroke his own ego, flaunting his "new maiden," Lady Seraphina, to try and make Rin jealous.
  • She stands tall and beautiful, donning an elegantly long, dark dress adorned with red accents. Her perfect, dark skin is complimented by thick, perfectly consistent Fulani-style braids collected into an updo that resembles a crown.
  • Thysbe's perception links this woman's likeness to the memory of General Valmor's attacks uncovered by Rin, although he can't confirm anything definitively since he's trying to recall from a memory of a memory.
  • During this afternoon tea, Seraphina makes several passive aggressive comments towards Rin, both insulting and insinuating knowledge of the party she was invited to, saying things like she wishes she could go and that she would be fit for dancing there.
  • Following the discussion the party leaves the parlor to go to General Valmor’s war room, while Thysbe hangs back, not wanting to interact with the higher-ups.
  • Thysbe continues snooping around, asking the house staff for more information about Lady Seraphina, and learns: she has only been around for maybe a week at most, but that's longer than the people he hires. They don't understand why she's dating him but she appears to also be a noble which is culturally more acceptable than him hiring escorts and hookers all the time.
  • In the meeting, General Marshal provided official commendations to the party for their actions at the front lines, the crescent isle, and the bridge, specifically highlighting Squire Helio's sacrifice to save the Sovereign Pass.
  • Valmor briefs the party on "Operation Winter Harvest," a planned counter-offensive to raid and empty the winter food stockpiles of farming villages along the Sruth River to solve the kingdom's food shortages and starvation crisis.
  • During this discussion, XIV asks if this operation includes targeting Beag’s village of Raon, to which he initially tried to deny and talk around.
  • This tense debate ultimately resulted in the party being “assigned” to the takeover of Raon via any means necessary, being assured that any failure on their part wouldn’t ultimately result in violence or hostility for the village. The party instead hatched a plot to use Beag to try and convince his people to join the Pyrian effort and provide excess food through their own volition.
  • After finalizing this plan, everyone departed for the weeks long journey back to the front, specifically witholding the information of General Wells’ promotion gala, and their intentions to strike.
  • The party departs the capital and travels east, arriving at the forward military staging ground positioned directly at the Sovereign Pass bridge.
  • They cross paths with General Edward Marshal, the logistics general from Hearthward on-site, who is actively overseeing the structural reinforcement of the bridge and managing the eastern refugee logistics.
  • Marshal stands on the pass and compliments Squire Helio’s defensive stand to the party, noting that her bravery preserved the vital supply lines keeping the refugees in Hearthward alive.
  • He also makes remarks that while things are stabilizing with the influx of refugees in Hearthward, there is some growing concern of a cult of "Immolationists" that are growing more restless and insurgent, but it is being monitored now.
  • Before they depart, he also provides the party with some supplies:
    • Magical "Mail Anywhere" Parchment
    • Acidpaste
    • Ogre Musk
  • As the party crosses the Sruth into Resurgum territory, they eventually discover a decimated Resurgum courier squadron pinned down and butchered against a large oak tree.
  • One of the soldiers is still alive tangled in vines. He tells everyone that he had thought the stories of primal monsters attacking - Thunderbirds out at sea, sentient trees in the forests, rock golems in the mounts - were just rumors or stories for camp, but they are real.
  • As XIV attempts to free him from the vines they collectively lash at him, trigging a reanimation of the nearby tree and summoning several smaller sentient saplings.
