Session 89 - Davy Jones Locker

  • Maris has the dark baton, +2 studded leather armor
  • Davy Jones Locker / Finale
  • Evil corrupt versions of the pirate captains (Austin Otto, Brian Teyy, and Presto Bassfinger)
  • Also evil corrupt Ace Maverick, Songbeard, and Maris
  • Tassarion's form dissipates into motes of light that envelop his wife's spiritual form. Her eyes open, filled with a mixture of confusion and wonder. Tassarion, now an etherial representation of himself, looks back at the party as he ascends; she is resurrected in the Material Plane.
  • Jobidrian has the ring of ram in his shop, Selene gets bracers of defense and the robe of the archmage
As the last note of Bach's Encore fades, you are all left standing in silence as the souls from Davy Jones' Locker continue peacefully walking through the gates as they ascend upwards. Their forms change from a dark ghostly green to a serene, silvery-white glow as they move past the threshold. This is the endless parade: a final, beautiful procession.

Within the march you all see a man in ornate seafaring attire appears, his magnificent beard sculpted in the shape of a duplet eighth note. He holds hands with a little girl in a beautifully flowing dress. As his head turns back he locks eyes with Morgan, a proud smile on his face. The girl looks up at Morgan, too. Her smile is innocent, like a light that was never touched, never corrupted by the darkness of the Locker. They both turn back to face the exit and walk free; their souls liberated.

Selene, your gaze locks onto two spirits that stand on the opposite side of the procession. They are a man and a woman embracing hand-in-hand facing towards you. They both bear a gentle but proud look. They are proud that the little girl they once knew has grown into a woman who has not only carved her own destiny, but empowers others to do the same.

Tassarion, as you lower your the baton and look out at the sea of souls marching towards the gate, your gaze catches a nostalgic, familiar face. For the first time in 30 years, Eladria stands before you, still, facing against the flow of walking. Her form shimmers, fragile and spectral, yet unmistakable.

Tassarion's Choice

At this point Tassarion has two options:

1) You may take Erin's hand and together embrace Eladria's saved spirit together, as a family, for the first and only time. Her Spirit is saved and she is at peace; she will ascend and rest eternally.

2) You may doff your robes and reach out your right hand towards Eladria. Doing so will trigger a ritual allowing you to use your magic to swap places with her soul. This will resurrect her, but at the cost of Tassarion's life.

Script

Erin: "You did everything you could, Dad.."

A shimmering, golden radiance begins to emanate from Tassarion's very core. He extends his right hand towards Eladria's spirit, and from his fingertips, golden threads of emerge. They wisp and sparkle through the air, luminous against the ghostly green of the Locker, and anchor themselves to Eladria's spirit. The golden light pours into Eladria, and her shimmering, ethereal form becomes more opaque as it begins to solidify.

As her form continues to materialize, you all realize that this golden light is not being restored, but rather it is being transferred. Tassarion's body begins to shed. His skin loses its color, and his hair turns to ash as his form becomes translucent, dissipating into motes of golden light that stream down the threads and into his wife's spirit.

For Erin, the realization begins to set in. "Wait!" she cries out, rushing forward. Her hands grasping towards his for a hold. But her fingers meet only sparks of light as they disperse around the motion of her arm—the last remnants of her father's physical form.

Tassarion, now a luminous echo of himself, turns to face the party. You eyes Tassarion, though without substance, meet Erin's with tears welled in her eyes.

Pauses to Tassarion the opportunity to say his final words.

Tassarion turns his head towards the exit, and a serene smile etches itself onto his face. His spirit, now a silvery-golden beacon, joins the silent, joyous march of ascending souls.

As his spirit disappears into the distance, Eladria's new body collapses. She takes a deep, gasping breath as she pushes herself up. Her hand trembles as she reaches into the empty space where he stood. Her gaze, a mix of dawning grief and profound awe, lifts to meet Erin's. A mother and daughter stare at each other—one reborn, the other hollowed by loss. The world shatters and mends itself in an instant. It is a testament to a father's sacrifice, and a promise that these final words will never be forgotten.

Tassarion's Letter

To the members of Melody’s Tempest,

If you're reading this, it is likely that I have paid the price for my involvement with Davy Jones, and my and my decision to defy his wishes. I have committed atrocities and transgressions during my time bound to him, that which I neither expect nor ask for forgiveness of. However, while our time together, as a group, was short, I leave behind my final expression of gratitude to you all.

To Ethiul,

Your wisdom and guidance has been a tremendous help, not just for me, but for all of us. You have been our moral compass: keeping us on the right path, keeping our dynamic in balance, and helping all of us see each other and ourselves in a new light. I don’t know what would’ve happened if you weren’t there when we all needed you the most.

To Voroth,

It fills me with great joy knowing that you are able to live out the rest of your days to their fullest after defeating Transcendent Voroth. I'm very grateful I was able to play a part in saving you from such a terrible fate. In doing so, you and the many versions of you have taught me that perfection and power are overrated; you were very wise to choose peace and love in its stead.

To Morgan,

The two of us were certainly a force to be reckoned with on the battlefield! I know we've had our differences in the past, and occasionally were even at odds with one another, but through all the turmoil it was you who showed me the error of my ways. I truly cannot thank you enough for the sacrifices you made on my behalf, and what that has given me—you brought my daughter back from the dead... There is an ancient proverb from the sages that read: "It is only during the hardest time where your true friends reveal themselves", and I think it speaks for itself.

To Selene,

I cannot express with the words on these pages what you have done for me. For your counsel, for your patience, and most importantly, for the way you’ve stood by Erin when I could not. Seeing how you two interact has made me realize that she isn’t alone—that she’s never been alone. When I see the bond you share with Erin, it feels as though a part of me has been given back. You remind me of the daughter I lost for so long, and because of that, I cannot help but see you as my own.

I trust you, wholly and without hesitation. Not just to guard her life, but to keep her spirit safe, and to make sure she never forgets who she is. I could ask for nothing greater than that—and I know you will not fail her. For whatever my trust and love are worth, they are yours. You carry them with you now, as surely as you walk beside her.

And lastly, to my beloved daughter, Erithyl,

I'm sorry that I couldn't be there when you needed me most. I missed the sound of your laughter, the chance to watch your steps grow steady, the privilege of telling you I was proud of the person you would become.

I cannot give you an answer as to why all this had to happen, but know this: not a single day passed when you were not somewhere in my thoughts, hoping that one day you might understand the depth of my love.

What I hope for is that you remember me not as a ghost from a life that never was, but as a man who has loved you fiercely from the moment you were born. If there is a trace of me that remains, let it be in the strength you carry, in the compassion that shines through your choices, in the courage that I know beats within you.

Though I cannot walk beside you now, I am with you at every sunrise, in the hush of starlight, in the breath you draw when the world is too heavy. And when your own journey one day brings you to the edge of the unknown, know that you will not cross it alone.

With a love beyond the reach of death,

Tassarion
