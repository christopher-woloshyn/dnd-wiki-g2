Session 2

  • After a long rest following the whirlpool encounter, the rescued crew, Morgan, Tassarion, and Cleff arrived in Saltmarsh.
  • The crew tried to give Morgan a sack of gold for saving them, but he declines and lets them keep the coin for themselves.
  • At this point the other two crew members introduce themselves. Aqua is a half-merfolk swashbuckler, and Mirikian is a Duegar monk/thief.
  • Mirikian says he was on the ship to protect the crew, and Aqua said she was hitching a ride to search for someone.
  • She reveals that she is looking for someone named "The Marooned Sailor" in the sea of cascades who is said to broker information about factions and people's whereabouts.
  • Morgan, hearing this, offers to let them travel since they are also looking for someone and could also use "The Marooned Sailor's" help. But first, he wants to go into town to a tavern he used to frequent for more information and supplies.
  • The party goes to a tavern called "The Sea Sharppe"; it's dingy and dirty, and no one seems to enjoy their presence, but they grab a table and order food regardless.
  • Morgan recognizes the bard there, a halfing named Anna Log. He approaches there and learns that he shouldn't be here. She tells him that things are very different since the death of his father, and the Sonnet Swashbucklers have regressed into a secretive group. She is sad that they don't bring the same life into taverns like they used to.
  • She leaves Morgan with a warning that many are out for him, and hands him a bottle.
  • The party then is able to de-escalate some hostile language from some of the lackeys in the tavern before being kicked out and told not to come back.
  • Mirikian remembers that Morgan is "The Disgraced Pirate" that everyone had told him about and that he betrayed the Sonnet Swashbucklers and formed a new pirate faction called "Melody's Tempest".
  • He also heard that Tassarion was a member of the Melodic Marauders, but deserted them to join Melody's Tempest as well.
  • The party then purchased supplies for their voyage and decided to head south to the city of Coastgate to sail through Barron's Pass.
  • While sailing southward, Tassarrion finally confided his backstory to Morgan.
    • He revealed that about 30 years ago, his wife was murdered and his daughter was kidnapped by some thugs.
    • After years of trying to get them back, his unanswered prayers led him to forging a pact with a mystical being that promised to help.
    • Using the powers imparted to him by this patron, he has been collecting the pieces to Johann Sea-Bastion Bach's Magnum Concierto, which he needs for a ritual.
    • He also confides in Morgan that his sister Maris is also collecting these works and is a necessary part of this ritual.
    • They agree not to dwell on what this could mean for them in the future.
  • After several days of travel, the party arrives at Coastgate. Wanting to avoid paying any fees, they recall their ship into its bottle and walk around the coast to re-embark in the Sea of Cascades.
  • Morgan checks the bottle he received from Anna. It contains a message that reads:
The Marooned Sailor holds the key,
She is majestic, bold, and bright.
Follow crimson flags to sea,
And find her warm embrace tonight.
  • The party is able to spot a small transport vessel, and they follow it into the fogs of the Sea of Cascades.
  • Eventually, they emerge from the fog to find a gargantuan vessel with the lettering "The Marooned Sailor"; She was not a person, but a ship all along.
  • The party is able to dock and recall their ship and approach the lobby.
  • There, they steal the name "Morgan Stormsail" from the ledger to check in, which they do, paying 100 GP each with a 50 GP discount.
  • Entering into the main room reveals a massive casino.
