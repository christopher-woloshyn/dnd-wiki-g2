Session 83 - Confronting Justin Otherday

  • The party has a send-off for Voroth, who plans to return to Raeneros to begin documenting the knowledge of the other Voroth's whose memories he experienced, and begin researching things about Davy Jones and the ritual to continue to support the party from afar.
  • Morgan and Selene confront Justin Otherday about his involvement with Erin's parents. Justin denied any wrongdoing, sticking to the same story Erin previously told the party about Terrence Everclear.
  • Morgan, frustrated by Justin's lies, attacks him. Justin retaliates by opening a trapped book containing five runes. The doors and windows of the office explode outwards, drawing a crowd and alerting the building.
  • Morgan miraculously passes all but one of the saving throws, using his Guarded Mind ability to deflect the final one. (There was a 0.67% chance of this outcome.)
  • Morgan uses a new spell from his magic gauntlet, Melody's Grasp, to bind Justin in spectral green chains that deal necrotic damage.
  • As Morgan interrogates the bound Justin, Tassarion and Ethiul hold back the growing crowd with a combination of deception and Ethiul's spectral monk arms.
  • Justin admits to Morgan and Selene that he was the one who placed the kidnapping request for Erin, but denies responsibility for her mother's death since he simply placed the hit and cannot control the actions of the assailants.
  • Erin arrives in a panic and pleads with Morgan not to harm Justin. Morgan, wanting Justin to confess to Erin, continues his torture. Justin, however, says his final farewell to Erin, expecting to die.
  • Tassarion, ashamed and unwilling to upset Erin, hangs his head. Morgan, growing more frustrated, casts Zone of Truth. Justin fails the save and, after being pressed, admits his actions.
  • Erin, deeply upset, asks if Morgan is satisfied and again pleads with him to let Justin go. Morgan releases him, storms out of the building, calling Tassarion a coward as he passes.
  • Erin rushes to Justin's to help him and Selene gives him a health potion to stabilize him. Everyone else leaves, while Erin stays behind to tend to Justin for a bit longer.
  • Later at the "Good" Gatekeeper's Inn, Tassarion and Selene are joined by Erin, and the three sit in a stunned silence. Selene and Tassarion apologize to Erin. Tassarion attempts to reconnect, stating he just wants to make things right with his family, but Erin coldly dismisses this, saying the family he longs for is just a fleeting memory that no longer exists.
  • After Erin leaves to get another drink, Selene reassures Tassarion, telling him to focus on the future and that he can't force a relationship with her.
  • Tassarion follows Selene's advice upon Erin's return, but Erin announces she needs to be alone and leaves the tavern.
  • At another Gatekeeper's Inn, Morgan expresses his frustration to Ethiul, lamenting that he feels he regressed by torturing Justin. He feels that everyone else's personal quests have been resolved while his has been put on the back burner. He feels especially let down by Tassarion's inaction. He also resents the fact that once Maris is dead, he will be without any family, while everyone else gets to reunion with theirs.
  • Ethiul doffs his cosmic blindfold to look Morgan directly in the eyes and reassures him that the party will support him and his quest. He imparts wisdom, and explains Morgan's strength and their dependence on him as a leader.
  • Tassarion and Selene arrive, and Selene realizes that this is actually the Good Gatekeeper's Inn, and Tassarion apologizes to Morgan, but Morgan states he doesn't believe him. Morgan declares that once Maris is dead, Melody's Tempest will be no more and that the party should go on their separate ways.
