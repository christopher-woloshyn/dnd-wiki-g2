Session 65- Mountaintop Battle Part 3

  • Phase 2 of the fight continues.
  • The Elder Tempest releases a massive wave of energy and magic knocking down Voroth, Fen, Ulgrim, and knocking back Ulrich and Selene.
"Give me control for just a moment. I can end this." - Transcendent Voroth
  • Voroth allows Transcendent Voroth to control him, allowing him to cast DOMAIN EXPANSION
  • Voroth engulfs the downed members of the party into his domain, blessing and fortifying them. He regains consciousness, but needs to maintain concentration on the spell.
  • The party continue to fight, and attempt to retreat into the domain. Morgan, Calen, Ethiul, and Soren barely make it, but Calen is able to massively heal everyone once inside.
  • As the calamity continues slamming into the walls attempting to break in Selene readies a fireball.
  • Once the calamity breaks in it's unleashed, and everyone attacks whittling it down and dealing the final blow.
  • After the party defeats the elder tempest the storm immediately dissipates.
  • Voroth Collapses from exhaustion and is cared for by Fen while the rest of the party investigates the Dragon's Lair for loot and items.
  • Voroth eventually wakes up and staggers his way over to the rest of the party to be lambasted by Morgan, who is concerned that the power he is indulging will eventually corrupt his mind.
  • After this heated discussion calms down, the party use a Teleportation Scroll they found to return to whispering pines while Ethiul and Soren travel back on foot which takes about two days.
  • While traveling down the mountain, Ethiul and Soren share a romantic moment looking at the stars.
  • Back at the Gatekeeper's Inn, the party celebrates and divides their loot. Selene decides she wants to take up Voroth's offer to become his "apprentice", although she's fairly clear that her motivation seems to be about ensuring the knowledge of Domain Expansion is not lost from Voroth's recklessness in dangerous settings.
  • Accordingly, she appoints Fen to take her place as the new dean of the Frostspire College of Magic.
  • Shocked by this, Fen decides to accept since she doesn't feel cut out for the level of danger the party is continuing to incur, and she can complete her educational training for the MoonStar Grove in parallel.
  • Upon learning about this when arriving the next day, Ethiul gets quite upset, feeling like he is going to lose a sister who has always been by his side. He runs off and turns into a pickle.
  • Fen, upon finding him sulking in pickle form, also turns into a pickle so they can be pickles together for a bit.
  • Morgan having just witnessed Ulrich fulfill a major milestone of his oath, becomes upset and slightly resentful over his companions' lack of urgency towards his own goals...
