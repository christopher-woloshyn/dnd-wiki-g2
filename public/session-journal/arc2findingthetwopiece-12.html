Session 33 - Search for the Two Piece Part 2

  • Ethiul and Voroth went to the Enchanted Ass-ylum. Ethiul got a #1 and Voroth got a #2 (pop rocks).
  • Tassarion contacted a group of Melodic Marauders, telling them he thinks he has a means of finding the Two Piece.
  • After making a deal with the ship's captain: Captain Barry Tone, a group of Sonnet Swashbucklers arrived to convince the party to take them to the treasure instead.
  • They say that Melody Songstrike is a traitor, and that the Sonnet Swashbucklers are the rightful heir to the Two Piece.
  • They want to recover a specific magic item for their new leader purported to be within the two piece: a magic item with ties to Johann Sea-Bastion Bach.
  • The party decides to travel with the Sonnet Swashbucklers instead of the Melodic Marauders cautious of their intentions, but wanting a better deal with the treasure.
  • Outraged, Captain Barry Tone attacked, but conceded after a fireball destroyed a large portion of his ship.
