Arc 2 - Vignettes of Truth: Beag

The military infirmary deep within the stone undercroft of Cinderheart is filled with desperation and suffering. Beneath the heavy, choking clouds of smoldering myrrh, zinc ointment, and lavender water, there festers the unmistakable reek of cooked meat. Beag, you are sitting on a wooden stool that creaks precariously under your massive frame. Your hands, covered with the dark soil of the capital's public gardens where you've been spending your downtime, look absurdly massive against the edge of the cot you sit besides. Laying before you is what remains of Squire Helio.

The pristine, "Golden Child" of House Fawkes whom you have come to know has been erased. The left side of her scalp is a weeping, hairless expanse of mottled black and purple skin. Where her elegant, sharp cheek once sat, the fire has tightened and split the flesh into a jagged, permanent tear. The singed skin pulls back from the corner of her lips, leaving her teeth and graying jawbone exposed in a grotesque, fixed grin. Her left eye is a sunken, clouded marble, tracking nothing.

Worst of all is her torso. The heat of her oversurged fire magic did not merely burn her; it melted the master-crafted Pyrian steel plate she wore directly into her body. The physicians could not cut it all away. Dark, bubbled iron slag and silver filigree are fused directly into her ribs, left shoulder, and left arm. Blackened metal barnacles erupting through raw, oozing scar tissue that crack and bleed every time she draws a shallow breath.

Helio’s good hand reaches out towards you blindly. It hits your massive, dirt-stained arm and grips your wrist with a desperate, terrified strength.

Helio: "Beag... you came to visit me?"

Beag: Responds

Helio: "I can feel the iron inside my chest when it gets cold at night. Look at what I've become..." Her grip on your wrist tightens. Her knuckles turn white. "I wasn't strong enough."

Beag: Responds

Helio "The Wings are expediting my rites given this so called 'Heroic Triumph'. Dame Helio Fawkes. They look at this body now and call it a 'monument to devotion.' They want to parade me in front of the new recruits as a hero."

Beag: Responds

"I'm terrified, Beag. I screamed for Igniavis at the bridge. I gave myself to him. I channeled his light... and he let the armor melt into my marrow. If the Phoenix King's bless is a gift, then why does it hurt so much?"

She releases your wrist, her hand falling limply onto the sheets. She stares up at the vaulted stone ceiling.

"The new year is approaching. It will be 999. One year left until the Millennium Rebirth... and for the first time in my life, I'm scared for what's to come."

Beag: Responds

Helio turns her face away, hiding the exposed bone from your sight, weeping quietly into the pillow. You are left sitting in the heavy incense, the weight of her fractured outlook hanging in the air.
