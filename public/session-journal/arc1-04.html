Session 4 - The Fate of Schlackedorf (2026-03-11)

  • After a brief rest and some armor repairs from the fallen soldiers, Thysbe lead XIV, Beag, Rin, Sir Salinor, and Squire Helio towards Schlackedorf in Pyralis.
  • As they crest the final hill and set eyes on the town, all they saw before them was a devastating seen of flames, smoke, and ash.
  • Thysbe rushed directly to his house past the church, with XIV, Beag, and Rin while Sir Salinor and Squire Helio broke off to investigate the church for survivors.
  • There were screams coming from the top floor and arsonists out in front fighting with a young man.
  • Thysbe used his wings to rush through the window of the top floor, spotting his younger brother Ettienne and successfully pulling him to safety.
  • Beag punched one of the arsonists into the building while XIV and Rin dispatched the other.
  • On the bottom floor of the house, Thysbe's mother Stella and little sister Mary were in the center of the room. Mary was crying while Stella lay there crushed by a fallen support beam.
  • Beag picked up the beam allowing Thysbe and XIV to pull them out of the collapsing building, however Stella's injuries at this point were far to grave.
  • In her dying breaths, she reveals to Thysbe that she lied to him, and that his wings aren't a gift, but that they come from another man:
"Thysbe... please forgive me... I lied to you... Marten always suspected something... Your wings... look closely at them... touch them.. They are not divine... They come from another man... I lied to keep you safe... I am so sorry I betrayed you."
  • After her passing everyone made their way to church where most of the remaining townsfolk had gathered. Among them was Marten, Thysbe's "father", who after learning of his wife's fate completely broke down.
  • He blamed Thysbe for not being strong enough, but Thysbe held his tongue. XIV de-escalated the encounter by asking where he should place her.
  • Simultaneously, Rin took Mary and Ettienne to one of the Pews away from the tension. Not Trusting Marten with the children now without a mother, she also used a commune to inform someone that newly orphaned children may be coming their way.
  • After this, XIV, Beag, and Helio went back out to search for more victims and survivors while Sir Salinor worked with Father Casper to come up with a plan.
  • There were 5 options in total:
A) Escort the Refugee's West further inland where it's safer
B) Go to the Capital with Sir Salinor, report to the Crown/Wings what happened
C) While forces are attacking in the West, Slip the net and head East back towards Resurgum (Beag Home?)
D) Chase the arsonists like vigilantees
E) Search for surviors amongst the scattered troops, try to reconsolidate the forces, and search for General Valmor.
  • After ruling out C and D it was decided that Sir Salinor would fly back to the the capital Cinderheart immediately to report the incident.
  • XIV, Beag, Thysbe, Helio, and Rin would begin an effort to reconsolidate scattered troops and search for General Valmor, and the freed prisoner (named Merrick) was assigned by Sir Salinor to escort the refugees west.
