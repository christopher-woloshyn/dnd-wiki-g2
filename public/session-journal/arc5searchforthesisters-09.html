Session 78 - The Otherday's Home for Gifted Children

  • Selene entered the inn room to Morgan face down in a bucket with Tassarion comforting him.
  • She filled everyone in on the talk she has with Erin and reluctantly told everyone she is ready to go to her childhood home: "The Otherday's Home for Gifted Children".
  • Upon arriving at the orphanage, the party is greeted by two young children. Selene says she is a sister which prompts them to escort everyone upstairs to her father's office.
  • After a tense reunion with her Father, Selene said she's back because the party needs his help finding someone to cast a very powerful spell. His name is Justin Otherday.
  • Selene makes several remarks during the discussion about her distain for this place as she does not consider it home. She also makes several remarks about how she "had to get out of there".
  • Erin and Selene's brother Micah join the party for the meal in the cafeteria. Selene asked why they've stayed all these years.
  • Erin stayed because she works for "The Syndicate" and is their Father's favorite, serving basically as a right hand to him. Micah stayed because he is studying at the university while still living at home.
  • Justin makes an offer that he can find someone powerful enough to cast the ritual and imprisonment spell, but it will take a couple days negotiation and upwards of 20k Gold, but he can get someone fast.
  • After he leaves, the party make a plan with Erin to ambush the Sonnet Swashbucklers when she turns in the job. She seems intrigued by Selene and the party's intentions and with the potential magical capabilities of the music, and wants in.
  • She offers to help both with teleportation magic and scrying magic.
  • The transaction occurs, and the party executes an ambush by teleporting in.
  • This catches both captains: Captain Clarie Anne Nette, and Captain Presto Bassfinger extremely off guard, a fight ensues
  • Midway through the fight, an enlightened Ethiul descended hanging from a hatched astral-touched wyrmling.
  • This overwhelms them, and they are defeated by the full might of Melody's Tempest (and Erin). Morgan collects their skull keys, adding the total of his collection up to 4.
  • The ship they were fighting on got destroyed by the other ship, and Erin teleported the party out.
  • In the stress of the situation, she is unable to teleport back to the original rendezvous, and at first it seems like the party is in a remote location, but after finding some high ground, they realize that they are actually closer to Raeneros than they would have been otherwise.
