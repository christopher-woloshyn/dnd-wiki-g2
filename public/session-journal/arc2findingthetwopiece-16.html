Session 37 - Search for the Two Piece Part 6

  • The party arrived at the site of the second location of the two piece.
  • They spent some time surveying the area and making absolutely sure that there are no traps or guardians like last time.
  • After they collected and inventoried the treasure, they struggled to push the treasure back to the dingy and made their way back to the Ship of Theseus.
  • On the ship there are signs of other people present. In the Captain's quarters, Captain Lyra Strummings is there along with First Mate Cleff, and a few other cronies (2 bandit captains, 1 mage) to confront the party.
  • She introduces herself to Captain Austin Otto: He has a proposition after revealing a bound Melody Songstrike.
  • He reveals that he knew all along they were traveling with Morgan and urges the party to leave the baton for them.
  • He says things like "You failed the Sonnet Swashbucklers, Melody deserted the Sonnet Swashbucklers, and now you're trying to usurp a treasure that doesn't belong to you OR Melody." and "Think about what your sister would want: she would want us every opportunity to reclaim his treasure and use it to enact justice on those that betrayed Songbeard."
  • Morgan lashes out and shaters the Emerald Gem releasing a Water Elemental; roll for initiative!
