Session 8 - A Mind Unspooled (2026-04-08)

  • The next morning, Rin looks for Rowena to have a word with her before parting ways, but she is already departed.
  • Beag hears that someone else is up, so together they go downstairs and share a breakfast. She asks him if he might know or remember anything about Commander Wells.
  • The two also write letters to Beag's Village and a Resurgum Pen Pal of Rin's. To send them, Rin needs to find someone who can and will take Mail to Resurgum.
  • At the same time, Thysbe flies out the window to deliver an incident he wrote containing the additional insights they uncovered about Nathaniel Wells.
  • He delivers it to the Generals' and Sir Salinor's surprise, but impresses them. Helio is upset at him for upstaging her; she catches him before he leaves to challenge him to a wrestling match.
  • XIV uses the morning to head to the Military camp to train on his own. He also writes some letters to the capital.
  • Once everyone has returned to the inn, they go to the Refugee slums to question them about what they experienced, and to look for Thysbe's siblings.
  • They learn from the refugees that their villages were all destroyed in a similar, clinical fashion: only the fields were targeted before the soldiers moved on. They didn't target civilians.
  • Thysbe is spotted by someone from Schlackedorf named Carrie. She tells him Marten hasn't been acting very different since everything happened. She also says Kalash enlisted in the army and is hopefully with Aidian in the military camp. Then she takes him to see Mary and Ettienne.
  • Mary gives Thybe a vial of ashes and says:
Papa made me scoop it from the church before we ran. He... he said it’s Mommy. I told him we should bury the ashes, like you're supposed to do, but he yelled at me. He said we aren't allowed to cry anymore. He said the fire made her perfect.

He took the big jar with him. He said he has to go share Mom with the others... so that everyone will be ready when the sky burns.
  • After this bit of investigation, Rin went to the brothel that night to make some money. She made 253 Marks.
  • The next day, everyone goes back to the military camp so Thysbe can accept Helio's wrestling challenge.
  • Thysbe tries using his wings to give himself an advantage, but as he darts around, she is able to pick out his movements and swiftly slams him to the ground.
  • After defeating Thysbe Beag steps into the ring. Helio doffs her plate armor to be more evenly matched with Beag. As they continue in a long drawn out stalemate, Helio slowly gains the upper hand, she pivots Beag into a disadvantaged state.
  • Right as she's about to win, XIV yells "Sweep the legs!" which causes Beag to snap out of his enjoyment of the moment and pivot to gain the upper hand. Slowly, his much higher weight slowly presses her back down pinning her.
  • Beag was very happy and impressed; this was the hardest fought match he's every had.
  • The scene changes to the next day, when everyone arrives back at the General's tent. Loremaster Amram is there, and he immediately stands up to greet and praise Thysbe for all the great work he has done.
  • Amram then prepares a ritual: He uses Rin's commune ability to try and channel Valmor's memories, using Helio, XIV, Beag, and Thysbe as anchors who were also exposed to the spores. He begins the ritual which causes everyone to collectively experience a flash of memories from one another. The order of the memories is Helio, Thysbe, Beag, XIV, and Valmor last.
  • Everyone experiences the events of Valmor's capture again, this time from his first person perspective. Everyone sees the ambush, and see him getting dragged out of the carriage before a woman caresses his chin with her hand. In this moment three additional memories flash, as they are copied out of his mind.
  • After this happens, Valmor collapses, sedated, but conscious. The woman relays all the information from the memories she extract to the man from the scene. She then says:
"Mr. Wells, we're done here. What will you do with the general now that you've captured him?"

"I will let the General go, of course. He is unaware his memories have been extracted and tampered with. He who doesn't suspect anything amiss will never think anything can go wrong. And just like that, the bridge falls and the gold sinks."
  • Everyone comes to after this collective memory. Amram is sweating profusely from the ritual, and Valmor gives the look of someone embarrassed and violated. They all reel at the severity of the situation.
  • Both the Sovereign Pass Bridge, and the Collateral Shipment face an eminent threat. They surmise Wells will act fast on the information, since the longer he waits, the larger chance he learns that Pyralis discovered the truth of the plot and can prepare defenses.
  • Being the more front-line focused leaders, General Valmor and Sir Salinor argue in favor of defending the bridge. Helio also takes this position after Sir Salinor.
  • General Marshal and Loremaster Amram in contrast argue in favor of defending the shipment. This leaves the leadership divided at 2-2.
  • After some additional discussion, it was decided that XIV, Thysbe, Rin, and Beag will be a fast strike force, to head to the Crescent Isle as quickly as possible to aide against any potential attack. Sir Salinor and Helio would head to the Sovereign Pass and meet several Paladins of Ordinus (that XIV will request via a letter to the capital) to defend any potential attacks there.
  • Everyone then departed to the naval tent to get a vessel prepared immediately. As they were walking over there, Loremaster Amram took Thysbe aside to ask him something.

Helio's Memory

The scratch of a quill and a smoldering hearth. Helio flawlessly recites complex scripture. Her father, Lord Cassian, doesn't look up from his ledger. "Good, but.. you try too hard, dear. You will make a fine bride for a pious lord one day, yes, but if Igniavis is to bless this House with his wings, that gift will belong to one of the men of our bloodline; leave the holy ambitions to them." She stiffly curtsies, before exiting the room.

Thysbe's Memory

"The scent of crushed mint and bitterroot. Thysbe’s hands are stained green as he grinds the herbs with a heavy stone pestle. Beside him, his mother Stella hums a quiet, familiar tune; one that she would hum for his siblings as she luls them to sleep. She is carefully pouring a simmering vial. It is warm, safe, and entirely devoid of war."

Beag's Memory

The taste of mud and the roar of laughter. Beag is in the center of a cheering circle at a festival taking place in the steppe this year. He lifts Làidir over his head before tossing him into the muck. In the background, the earth rumbles as a herd of massive, pitch-black horses gallop freely under an endless blue sky. It is pure, unbridaled vitality.

XIV's Memory

"The cold bite of iron shackles touches your wrists. There is total darkness. A heavy oak door creaks open, and a small sliver of light spills in. XIV's younger voice says: "Please, Father, I beg... why must I endure this pain?" The silhouette in the doorway doesn't move; a cold silence stares: "This is for your own betterment, son. You must be ready for whatever is to come. And I am prepared to ensure that happens no matter the cost."

Valmor's Memories

Valmor in a dimly lit vault, he is overseeing the packing of the magical artifacts and religious relics as someone besides him inventories through a checklist. He confirms they are to be shipped to Crescent Isle in the Southern Gulf as collateral for a massive loan of grain, steel, and gold from the Southern Merchants. They discuss the importance and dates of this shipment, noting that without it, the loan will be void, and the kingdom would struggle greatly without that aid.
Valmor is in a strategic briefing with several generals showing the Sovereign Pass Bridge on a map. They are discussing the structural flaws and needed repairs of the bridge in addition to how they will distribute defenses given the scarcity of troops and resources. They also explain its importance for allowing the quick transfer of resources between the east and west.
Valmor is in his manor, pacing back and forth giving Caspian a lecture on how to contribute to their family. He is disappointed in him, and is setting expectations for him to start doing more since one day he will be the head of their house. Caspian is detached from the conversation.
