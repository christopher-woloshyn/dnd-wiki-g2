Session 5 - Long Road Back (2026-03-16)

  • Thysbe returned to the apothecary to explore the ruins and reflect on all that had happened. He gathered herbs from the garden, and found a small ornate lockbox under the counter with his name on it, but he wasn’t ready to open it.
  • Back at the church, Thysbe approached his family to say goodbye, much to Marten’s dismay.
  • “Where the fuck do you think you're going? Your mother just died and you're just going to run away from the problem like nothing happened?” Thysbe continued to hold his tongue, but XIV eventually stepped in: “The pews seemed to have been kept quite warm with your fat ass sitting in them the whole time while we were out actually saving people.”
  • "You want to make this about me, huh? I was securing the sanctuary! I was with Father Casper praying to the great Igniavis. I was doing my part in the holy work, and you failed the physical work."
  • "And your prayers were answered: Thysbe arrived and saved the rest of your family." - XIV
  • Following this discussion, everyone departed to travel back east towards the front. On the journey, XIV asked Rin why she was coming with back to the front when she could just continue west with the refugees. She mentioned that General Valmor is an important client to her and that she is concerned for his safety.
  • Meanwhile Beag was yapping about some crazy farm stories to Helio who was pretending to be interested.
  • Over the next few days, a montage of following tracks, turning fallen supply wagons, investigating campfire smoke in the distance, and finding/treating groups of soldiers lead to an incredibly successful recovery effort. Soldiers retreated to the west to regroup where the refugees were also heading.
  • It was a resounding success, and they scored a 113 netting everyone:
- 3 Armor Stitchers
- 2 Bolster Potions
- 2 Stride Potions
- 4 Minor Health Potions
- 1 Stamina Potion
- 2 Snap Powder
- Rin, Thysbe, and XIV obtain upgraded armor
- Thysbe obtains an upgraded sword
- Dozens of rescued groups of soldiers, included a larger rallied crowd of ~30-40.
  • Several supplies were recovered, including some lost high quality alcohol and meats from the banquet at the war camp. This was adjacent to General Valmor’s unit, and contained instructions in where he should have been during the attack.
  • That night, Beag prepared a hearty and delicious stew for everyone, and the evening was a quiet and peaceful change of pace over the last several days.
  • Thysbe stared into the fire recounting the trauma he had just endured, Rin thought about how this is the only time she can really remember not working, Beag and Helio talked about wrestling, and she accepted his challenge to a match. XIV remained stoic, preparing for what’s to come.
