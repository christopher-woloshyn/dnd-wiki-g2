Session 66 - Down Time

  • This wasn't a real session, rather an "asynchronous" session where we discussed down time activities.
  • Several weeks pass and the party remains in whispering pines during this time to tie up the loose ends.

Selene, Fen, and Voroth

  • Selene trains and prepares Fen for assuming the role of Dean of the College.
  • Voroth is incapable of casting spells or manipulating the weave for several weeks. This power eventually returns after about 6 weeks.
  • Together with Voroth, they spend most of their time at the College studying, teaching, and preparing to take over Selene's responsibilities.

Ethiul and Soren

- Ethiul and Soren continue to develop their relationship, caring for the Egg and going on hunting excursions together into the mountains.

- One night, when Ethiul and Soren are watching the aroura together, Ethiul has a dream where he is visited by his sister. This time, she is much closer, and the details of her face reveal that she is wearing a blindfold.

"Brother, as the path before you grows clearer your eyes may not perceive it; vision can be a gift, but it is also a veil. The next step in your journey requires you to don the blindfold. It is not merely a cloth to cover your sight but a reminder to look inward. With your eyes closed to the chaos around you, you will learn to perceive the eternal harmony that binds the cosmos.

Remember, Ethiul: the wise do not flinch at darkness, for they know it is from within that shadows fall away, leaving only clarity. You must walk with unwavering trust, guided not by what you see, but by what you know to be true. In this, you will find the strength to endure, the serenity to lead, and the wisdom to ascend."
  • Ethiul Dons the blindfold, to begin this ritual. Ethiul now automatically fails all perception checks that rely on vision, and gains blindsight and truesight at a radius of 10ft.

Morgan and Tassarion

  • Morgan, impatient with the others' lack of urgency towards his goals, decides to pursue them alone for a bit.
