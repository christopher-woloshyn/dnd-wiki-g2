Session 6

  • Ethiul tells the party that the calamity they are about to face is a massive flood of demons from his Homeland far to the west over the sea. A rift had been opened (Events of Group 3, Arc 1) that forced them to flee east in search of help.
  • When everyone returns back to Longdale, Prim and Gynnie go their separate ways from the rest of the party.
  • Attempts to warn the townfolk initially do not go well. Most people do not believe in this Calamity, however, the people of the West Longdale Trading Company listen, just due to how much the party had helped them in the past, but the rest of the townsfolk are not convinced.
