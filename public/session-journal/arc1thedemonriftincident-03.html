Session 4 - Welcome to Longdale Part 4

  • The party travels back to Longdale and continues south along the coast until they reach Songbeard's hideout.
  • They sneak into the lair to confront Songbeard only to find all of the pirate henchmen have been slaughtered, and Songbeard lays dying before them at the end of the hideout.
  • He warns them of a great impending catastrophe is about to unfold, and directs the party of a bag of things to aid them before dying.

Songbeard's Gift

  • 2 Potions of Flying
  • Decanter of Endless Water
  • Sending Stones
  • 1 Amethyst Gem
  • His necklace, which has a key-shaped pendant. The base of the key is a skull with ruby encrusted in the eyes.
