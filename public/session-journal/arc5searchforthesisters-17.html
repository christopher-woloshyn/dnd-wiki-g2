Session 86 - The Search for Maris Part 3

  • The next morning in the tavern, the party makes a plan. Selene casts sending to reach out to their allies from Whispering Pines for help.
  • They agree to meet in a few day's time in the port city of Saltmarsh near the Sea of Syncopation to ready for their trip.
  • Selene goes to find Erin. While she's gone, Morgan acknowledges Tassarion's efforts to help him, and they reconcile their tension at the realization that given Tassarion's actions, they're in this together now.
  • When Erin and Selene return, Erin apologizes for her anger towards everyone, and offers her help to Morgan and Tassarion, saying that all of these problems stem from her, and while she doesn't blame herself for the situation she's in, feels an obligation to make things right for her own sake.
  • She also says, facetiously, that she'll forgive Morgan once Maris has been dealt with and their problems are behind them.
  • The party then meets at the Gatekeeper's Inn with Ulrich, Fen, Calen, Soren, and Ulgrim. Morgan gives a rousing motivational speech to the group, recounting the journey they've been on together, looking towards finally finishing what they started.
  • After the speech, a strange frog-man named Vorth shows up, saying he's been following them trying to deliver a letter. The letter is from Voroth:
My Dearest Companions,

At this time you are likely at the precipice of your quest to defeat Maris. In my research I have learned the following about Johann Sea-Bastion Bach from Jobidrian and his libraries:

As you know, Bach's 'Magnum Concierto' contains the ritual used to seal a great evil, that which we've come to know as Davy Jones. The ritual is completed once the full Concierto has been performed at the ritual site. These works also recount the story of the conflict between his crew and Davy Jones; each movement a piece to the overarching narrative culminating with Movement 10 - Requiem of the Fallen - A lament of Bach's sacrifice for the cause.

Other documents I have found refer to Davy Jones as "The Pirate Lich". An extremely powerful and dangerous undead that feeds off the souls of the damned. If my assumptions are correct, then I believe Davy Jones' locker to be phylactery of souls, or the source of his power, and notably it is a power source that cannot be destroyed.

This would explain Bach's decision to seal Davy Jones rather than destroy him - because destruction was simply not an option. I surmise that Maris intends to play the Concierto backwards, reversing the effects of the seal thereby freeing Davy Jones. Once Maris is defeated the threat of Davy Jones still looms large. If there were some way to destroy Davy Jones' Locker, or free the souls of the damned from his influence, you could possibly destroy him, permanently.

I hope this information proves useful, I'm looking forward to a feast together soon. 

With love,

Voroth
  • Based on this information, Tassarion remarks that they should not try to destroy the locker, as there's a chance his wife's soul could be trapped there.
  • Nonetheless, the party heads to sleep. The next morning, they set sail for the center of the Sea of Syncopation. As they approach, they are greeted by several ships waiting for their arrival.
