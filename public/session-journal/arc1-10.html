Session 10 - Crescent Isle Scrutiny (2026-04-22)

  • Upon arriving at the Crescent Isle docks, Captain Bramwell presented his seal and mission orders. Everyone cleared check-in without issue, benefiting from Bramwell's survival during the thunderbird attack.
  • Rin, Thysbe, and Beag immediately headed for the keep to scout the location of the collateral shipment exchange scheduled for mid-morning the next day.
  • During reconnaissance, Rin noticed a brawny, tattooed individual who starkly contrasted with the typically posh, noble society of the Crescent Isle. She also noticed a few others both in the kitchen and the courtyards with similar or matching tattoos
  • Thysbe asked the head guard about past incidents and blacklisted factions. The guard said it had been many years since the last major incident occurred.
  • The last time something happened was with a group called the Black-Quill Trading Co. which lead to the keep having to payout a massive sum to them for what had happened. The guard was pretty bitter when telling the story insinuating it was staged by them to scam them out of a lot of coin which is why they were blacklisted.
  • Meanwhile, XIV investigated the local tavern. The barkeep didn't have the same kind of shady knowledge that you would expect from a more seedy tavern, but they noted an influx of ill-mannered and poorly dressed patrons, advising XIV to return during peak hours to see and ask for himself.
  • After this, everyone regrouped to spend some time in town before nightfall. Rin bought some nearly rotting fruit as a fruit stand, and mailed her other letters at a courier.
  • Following visited a magic item shop. Purchases included Root Harden Wraps for Beag, a pair of Homing Compasses, and a Smuggler’s Coin for XIV.
  • The shopkeeper provided the party with free fortune readings together in the back room. She said that since they travel together, their fates are likely intertwined, and therefore would receive readings together. To receive their fortune, they must provide their full name when called upon. Most people did not receive the most uplifting fortunes. XIV seemed particularly irritated, and he didn't even receive a prediction.
  • Returning to the bustling tavern that evening, Beag and Thysbe engaged in a game of Ale Pong with some rowdy, bro-like patrons to liven the atmosphere, winning his initial matches.
  • Two of the rougher, ill-mannered patrons (mentioned earlier by the barkeep) then challenged Beag and Thysbe. After they narrowly won, the stakes were raised: He offered his female companion for the night against a night with Rin.
  • Rin secretly hoped Beag and Thysbe would lose the match so she could organically infiltrate the man's ranks for espionage. Unaware of this plan, Beag and Thysbe won the match.
  • Despite the victory, Rin approached the opposing man and accompanied him to his room. Once his shirt was removed, she confirmed he bore the same matching tattoos as the suspicious individuals at the keep.
  • Meanwhile, Beag spent the night with the wagered woman, performing with such high proficiency that she is unable to walk the following morning.

Purchased Items

  • Root-Harden Wraps: These coarse, enchanted fibrous wraps resonate with natural magic. When the wearer successfully makes an unarmed melee attack, they may spend 1 Hope to deal an additional 1d4 damage and force the target to become Vulnerable as the wraps magically expand from the hands and bind the limbs of the target.
  • Homing Compass: These two compasses point towards each other no matter how far apart they are.
  • Smuggler's Coin: A mundane-looking gold coin. When flipped and given a target's name, it invariably points its face toward the general direction of that target, provided they are within a several mile radius. It breaks after three uses. If the person is not within range it lands tails. The coin breaks after three uses.

Fortunes

  • Rinelle - The Moon:
The moon makes such a beautiful reflection in the water, wouldn't you agree? But what more is a reflection than a trick of the light upon water's surface? It hides what the ocean truly holds within; the ocean is cold and dark; creatures live and multiply where no one can see. It would seem you are so very careful to ensure everyone's eyes remain on that reflection, but your water is rippling, and water with too many ripples eventually loses its reflection.
  • XIV - Justice (Inverted):
Hmmm, I can't quite see an indication of the future... But that alone tells me a lot. This card also tells me a lot; it tells me your cause may not be the same as those you march beside. It would seem you carry a heavy, terrifying burden—one that you fear others will not understand. The inversion also reveals to me your hypocrisy: you demand honesty from your allies while tightly guarding your own secrets. Know that you cannot achieve your goals alone; a solitary shield inevitably splinters under the weight of an army.
  • Beag - Hanged Man:
The Hanged Man represents a sacrifice; I see a sacrifice in your future. A tragedy stalks you and one whom you have grown to admire for their strength. I see one being struck by fire, maimed and scarred by the licks of war. For the other I see loved ones; they are mourning over a marked grave, broken and empty as if they are missing a piece of themselves. I can't see who's fate is whose, but the answer will reveal itself to you soon enough.
  • Thysbe - Devil (Inverted):
You carry a nervous demeanor before you. You likely tremble before authority, and you probably fear yourself a fraud. Your wings differ from those who serve Igniavis; the power in your blood is no holy gift, it's a dark inheritance. Your father is no saint—he is a sadistic shadow walking in ruin. Yet, the Devil inverted promises shattered chains. This means your shackles to the 'old' wings will break, but whether you inherit his cruelty or forge your own path is a choice that remains yours alone.
