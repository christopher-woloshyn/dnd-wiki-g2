Session 77 - Enter Erin Otherday!

  • The next morning, Erin suggested to Selene that they return home together, since "father would probably be more accepting if I were there, too."
  • Together they make a plan to meet up after she completes the job she had started and the party arrives in Coastgate. Specifically they say they will meet up at "The Good" Gatekeeper's Inn.
  • Erin then teleports away with the children she was coaching, and the party continues sailing to Coastgate.
  • The night before arriving, Morgan has another dream much like the one a few days prior. It's darker in tone, highlighting a cold feelings coming over him with motifs of being bound. Then, he appears on a hill on what seems like the outskirts of Raeneros City. There is a headstone there as lightning strikes the grave, and he wakes up in a cold sweat.
  • As he get out of bed and immediately notices magical chains wrapped around his left forearm.
  • He goes to the rest of the party for help, with Selene eventually identifying the magic as being divine; Tassarion, however, kept insisting to remove his arm.
  • The party made their way to the tavern, and a few hours later, an excited Erin arrived wanting to celebrate completing her job.
  • A few drinks later, and she began to loosen up a bit. Tassarion began questioning her about her job where she eventually revealed that she was tasked with stealing some rare original music.
  • She wouldn't explicitly say, but accidentally heavily implied that it was the Sonnet Swashbucklers that hired her.
  • Selene and Erin stepped outside to give the party space to come up with a plan. Morgan then attempted to convince her to show him the piece. Eventually she did show him, but wouldn't let him touch it.
  • In a panic at that request, she teleported herself and Selene to the crime scene to prove where she got it from.
  • Unfortunately, that left them without the magical means to come back, so Selene and Erin had to hide out in the forest.
  • The next morning, Selene and Erin were able to talk one on one and clearly state intentions. She affirmed that whatever happens to her clients after her job is done is none of her concern, and insinuated to Selene that the party could use her for information on their location to land a surprise attack.
  • Erin then teleported them back and they agreed to meet up at their childhood home later that day.
