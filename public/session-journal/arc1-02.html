Session 2 - A Short-Lived Victory (2026-02-21)

  • A few days after the battle, a sufficient outpost has been built in the forward strategic position. Thysbe is invited by Squire Helio and Sir Salinor to observe some prisoner interrogation.
  • XIV stands with another Paladin of Ordinus. There is discussions and disagreements about who the "True Prince" is which leads to no useful information being shared
  • Beag reveals that he was just there to protect his village, but quickly learns that the opposing army doesn't seem to care about harming his village at all.
  • Eventually, one of the prisoners reveals that their leader is Commander Nathaniel Wells.
  • The scene shifts to nightime, with General Valmor proposing a toast to a well fought victory, before departing to ensure the supply lines from Schlackedorf can be properly established.
  • During the celebration, Rin services the Top Brass while learning about future reinforcement and supply line strategy.
  • XIV sneaks away a bottle of alcohol and shares it with Beag through the cage while they play cards and learn more about one another. XIV describes the entire origin of the war from Pyralis' perspective and leaves no opportunity for the other prisoners to impart their own opinions on the matter.
  • Thysbe, upset with his performance during the battle, drinks more than he should, and stumbles over to the training dummies to "train".
  • After consorting with the leaders Rin makes her rounds throughout the camp. She talks to Thysbe briefly before ending the night on a long private conversation with Squire Helio.
  • Rin learns that she is the only member of House Fawkes to have every been given the gift of divine wings, which is extraordinary given the Millennium Rebirth is only a bit over a year away.
  • The next morning, everyone is awoken by eruptions of siege weapons befalling the camp. There is a surprise attack, and they are aiming to wipe everyone out with no regard for prisoners.
  • XIV breaks Beag out of his cage. Thysbe sees some of the leaders have been assassinated, and everything is falling into disarray as soldiers march into the camp.
  • The bottom exit seems to be clear, so everyone makes a break for it, with Rin getting hit by a surprise attack and nearly going down.
  • All together, XIV, Beag, Thysbe, Rin, and one of the prisoners narrowly escape the chaos, hoping to eventually find and regroup with survivors later.
