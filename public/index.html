The year is 999 M.R.

One thousand years after the First Sunrise, the continent of Nidus stands on the brink of holy ruin. Bound by ancient scripture yet split by opposing dogmas, the sprawling lowlands of Resurgum and the rugged mountains of Pyralis are locked in an escalating, total war. Both empires claim their royal heir alone is the rightful vessel destined to inherit the power of the Phoenix King.

As angered primal gods awaken in the wilds and dark syndicates manipulate from the shadows, survival demands more than blind faith. Caught between warring crowns and divine zealotry, those in the crossfire must uncover buried truths before the promised day to survive. The Millennium Rebirth approaches.
